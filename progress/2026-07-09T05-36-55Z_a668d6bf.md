## Accomplished

Proved **Problem 4.12.2(b)** (`Etingof.Problem4_12_2.irreducible_iff`, issue #6037,
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Problem4_12_2.lean`), sorry-free: the Heisenberg
representation `R_z` is irreducible iff `z ≠ 1`. `#print axioms` reports only
`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

Proof structure:
- Reduced `IsSimpleModule ℂ[G] ρ.asModule` to `IsSimpleOrder (Subrepresentation ρ)` via
  `isSimpleModule_iff` + `Subrepresentation.subrepresentationSubmoduleOrderIso`, so the whole
  argument runs over honest `ℂ`-subspaces of `ZMod p → ℂ` invariant under the two generator
  operators.
- New reusable lemma `Heisenberg.closure_gens_eq_top`: `xGen`, `yGen` generate `G` as a
  submonoid (from `eq_gen_prod` + `central_word`). Used to turn "invariant under X and Y" into
  "invariant under all of G" when building the counterexample subrep.
- `z = 1 ⟹ reducible`: the constant line `span {1}` is a proper nonzero subrepresentation.
- `z ≠ 1 ⟹ irreducible`: `z` is a primitive `p`-th root (`orderOf z = p`), so `t ↦ z^t.val`
  is injective. A minimal-support argument (strong induction on `(support f).card`, peeling off
  eigenvalues via `Y - z^{t₂.val}`) lands an indicator `e_t` in any nonzero `Y`-invariant subrep;
  then `X e_s = e_{s+1}` sweeps all indicators, which span, giving `⊤`.
