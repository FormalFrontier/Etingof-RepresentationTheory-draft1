  `LinearMap.add_apply` on the representation side closes every case.

### Gotcha found for D2→D3 (object equality is NOT free)

Turning the reconciliation into a `ModuleCat`-object equality
`ModuleCat.of (A₁⊗A₂)(M₁⊗M₂)[instM] = extTensorFunctorLeftObj …` needs MORE than
`instModule_eq_extTensorModuleLeft`: `extTensorFunctorLeftObj` also equips `M₁`, `M₂` with the
*restricted* `k`-structure (`Module.compHom (algebraMap k Aᵢ)`, the local `restrictModule₁L`), which
is a different instance from the ambient `Module k Mᵢ`. They agree only propositionally via
`IsScalarTower k Aᵢ Mᵢ`. So the object identification must reconcile the `k`-structures too (or route
around it with a `ModuleCat` iso / `LinearEquiv` transport rather than a strict `=`). Recommend the
iso/transport route over `Eq` of objects.
