**The two-term complexes** (`section Disk`):

- `diskX V i n = if n = i ∨ n = i + 1 then V else 0` and `disk V i`, the cochain complex with
  `V` in degrees `i` and `i + 1` and the identity between them. The differential is
  `if m = i ∧ n = i + 1 then eqToHom _ ≫ eqToHom _ else 0`; `disk_d_self` exposes it,
  `isIso_disk_d` records that it is an isomorphism, `isZero_disk_X` that the complex vanishes
  elsewhere.
- `diskDesc (f : V ⟶ Lⁱ) : disk V i ⟶ L` — a morphism out of a disk is exactly a map in degree
  `i`; `diskDesc_f_of_eq` / `diskDesc_f_of_eq_succ` compute its two nonzero components (stated
  with a hypothesis `n = i` resp. `n = i + 1` rather than at the literal degrees, so they apply
  without transporting along `Int` arithmetic).
- `disk_hom_ext`: two morphisms out of a disk agreeing in degrees `i`, `i + 1` are equal.

**The decomposition** (`section DirectSum`): with `Kⁿ = Zⁿ ⊕ Wⁿ` (already in the file),

- `Wob K n` is `Wⁿ` as an object, `Winc`/`Wproj` the inclusion and projection, `Wsplit` the
  contracting homotopy landing in the complement. `projW_add_d_sW` is the degreewise splitting
  `Kⁿ⁺¹ = Wⁿ⁺¹ ⊕ dⁿ(Wⁿ)` that makes everything work.
- `fromDiskSum : ∐ᵢ disk (Wⁱ) i ⟶ K` is `Sigma.desc` of `Wⁱ ↪ Kⁱ`; `toDiskSum : K ⟶ ∐ᵢ …` is,
  in degree `n`, `newPart` (project to `Wⁿ`, inject into summand `n`) plus `oldPart` (contract
  to `Wⁿ⁻¹`, inject into summand `n - 1`).
- `isoDiskSum hK : K ≅ ∐ᵢ disk (Wⁱ) i`, and the public

## Current frontier

Issue #7435 is complete; every bullet of its "Required fix" list is met.

Two design notes for successors:

1. **Never construct the degreewise structure of a coproduct of complexes.** Both round-trip
   identities are proved without ever computing `(∐ F).X n`: one direction is checked degreewise
   on `K`, the other via `Sigma.hom_ext` followed by `disk_hom_ext`. Any future "`K` is a direct
   sum of …" statement should follow the same shape.
2. **Index-arithmetic discipline.** `toDiskSum`'s degree-`n` component mentions the summand
   `n - 1`. `oldPart hK m n` is therefore defined for *all* `m, n` with an internal
   `if n = m + 1`, so that its type does not depend on `m` and `simp only [show m + 1 - 1 = m …]`
   is a legal rewrite. Every proof then destructures the degree as `m + 1` before doing anything
   else. Deviating from this leads straight to motive-is-not-type-correct errors.

`disk`, `diskDesc`, `disk_hom_ext` are general-purpose (any `CochainComplex (ModuleCat k) ℤ`,
nothing about acyclicity) and should be reused rather than re-derived.
