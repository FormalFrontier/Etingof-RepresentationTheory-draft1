## Key mathematical findings (roadmap for successors)

- `range matHom₄` (the `𝔰𝔩₃` twisted `A₂⁽²⁾` loop image) is infinite dimensional **iff
  char k ≠ 3**. The `t`-degree climb carries the scalars `3` (`ad(y)³x = 3(E₀₁+E₁₂)`) and `6`
  (`ad(y)⁴x = 6E₀₂`); at char 3 these vanish and the middle vector `ad(y)²x = -(E₀₀-2E₁₁+E₂₂)`
  becomes `-I` (central), collapsing the image to 4 dimensions.
- **char 2 works via `matHom₄`** (3 = 1 ≠ 0 there): a small 3-cycle climbs `t`-degree. So the
  only genuinely hard case is char 3.
- The char-3 collapse is **intrinsic** to the `𝔰𝔩₃`-loop: `ad(y)²x ∝ I` mod 3 for any scaling
  `y = a E₀₁ - b E₁₂`. Char 3 needs a different representation (a bigger/other loop, or a
  ℤ-form bidegree argument).
