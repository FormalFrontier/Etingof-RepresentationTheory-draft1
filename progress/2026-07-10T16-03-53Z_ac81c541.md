- `ext1_simpleRep_vanishes_iff`: `Ext1Vanishes (simpleRep i) (simpleRep j) ↔ IsEmpty (i ⟶ j)`.
  Since both simple representations have all arrow maps zero, the Ext differential (`extDiff`)
  is the constant zero map, so Ext-vanishing (surjectivity of `extDiff`) holds iff its codomain
  is trivial. The codomain component at an arrow `a ⟶ b` is `Hom(S_i(a), S_j(b))`, nonzero
  exactly when `a = i` and `b = j`, i.e. when an arrow `i → j` exists.
- `two_dim_classification`: a total-dimension-2 representation of an acyclic quiver is either
  decomposable or has a distinct-endpoint arrow acting bijectively. Case on whether such a
  bijective arrow exists; in the negative case every arrow map is zero (a nonzero map between
  the two forced dimension-1 vertices would be bijective, contradicting the assumption), so
  the representation splits along a nonzero vector and a chosen complement (`Submodule.exists_isCompl`),
  contradicting indecomposability.
