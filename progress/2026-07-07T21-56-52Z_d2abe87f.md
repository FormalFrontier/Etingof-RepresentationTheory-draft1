  `q ∘ ρ_V(a) = ρ_{V/W}(a) ∘ q` (both hold by `rfl`).
- Split `ρ_V(a) = ρ_V(a) ∘ pW + ρ_V(a) ∘ (s ∘ q)`; cyclicity of the trace
