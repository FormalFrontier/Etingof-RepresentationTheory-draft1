## Key decisions / patterns

- The assembled classes are `def`s/`theorem`s parameterised by `k`, not global `instance`s:
  `IsFiniteAbelianCategory (FGModuleCat A)` does not mention `k`, so a global instance keyed
  only on `A` could never recover the ground field. Capstone examples activate them with `letI`.
- Preadditive diamond: instantiating `IsFiniteAbelianCategoryOverField k (FGModuleCat A)`
  against an *opaque* `[IsFiniteAbelianCategory (FGModuleCat A)]` hypothesis makes
  `Linear k (FGModuleCat A)` fail to synthesize (two non-syntactically-equal `Preadditive`
  instances). Stating the over-field structure relative to the *concrete*
  `fgModuleCatIsFiniteAbelianCategory k A` (whose `toAbelian` is Mathlib's canonical instance)
  keeps the `Preadditive` canonical and resolves it.
