Restored fresh-buildability of `Chapter5/Theorem5_15_1.lean` (issue #7512). A direct
`lake env lean` source check now succeeds with no `sorry`, `admit`, or replacement axiom
(warnings are pre-existing linter noise only). Fourteen distinct elaboration failures across
five root causes were repaired:

1. **`asModule` smul agreement (`smul_eq`, ~L155)** — the `hadd`/`hsmul` cases now expand
   both sides (`rw [add_smul, hx, hy, add_smul]`) and close with `exact rfl`, since
   `Representation.asModule := V` makes the `+`/ℂ-smul instances coincide at default
   transparency (only the group-algebra smul genuinely differs).
2. **`MvPolynomial.coeff_smul` on a `zsmul` (L261)** — the Vandermonde expansion carries a
   `ℤ`-smul; converted to the ℂ-smul with `Int.cast_smul_eq_zsmul (R := ℂ)` around
   `coeff_smul`/`coeff_monomial_mul'`.
3. **`hφ_smul` (L482)** — replaced the fragile `Finsupp.induction_linear` (which yields
   `→₀`-typed terms that break `add_smul`/scalar-tower matching) with
   `MonoidAlgebra.induction_on`; the `of g` base case uses the `spechtModuleAction`
   defeq + `hφ_group`.
4. **`Module.Free ℂ` (L707, L856)** — `inferInstance` no longer resolves the
   `Submodule.addCommMonoid` vs `AddCommGroup.toAddCommMonoid` diamond; supply
   `Module.Free.of_divisionRing ℂ <explicit type>` (sibling-file pattern).
5. **`IsSemisimpleModule` of `X ⊓ Y` (L529)** — dropped the broken `of_injective` call;
   `PermutationModule` is semisimple over the semisimple ring `ℂ[Sₙ]`, so the intersection
   is semisimple via `IsSemisimpleModule.submodule` + `inferInstance`.
6. **Schur restriction diamond (`hom_from_wrong_isotypic_eq_zero`, L813)** —
   `isotypicComponent` bakes `Ring.toSemiring` into `S` while `f` uses
   `MonoidAlgebra.semiring` (defeq, not syntactic). Built `f|S` as an unascribed structure
   literal passed straight to `bijective_or_eq_zero`, letting its `[Ring]`/`[AddCommGroup]`
   context drive the instances.
7. **restrict-scalars / scalar-tower cluster (`trace_isotypic_eq_mult_trace`, L1000+)** —
   `of_restrictScalars_finite` and `LinearEquiv.restrictScalars` re-synthesise
   `IsScalarTower`/`CompatibleSMul` with the codomain still a metavariable and no longer
   find the local instances. Passed `iST` explicitly via `@of_restrictScalars_finite`,
   hand-built `e'_ℂ` (using `map_smul_of_tower`, underlying maps unchanged), and pinned
   `M`/`N` in `trace_conj'`.
