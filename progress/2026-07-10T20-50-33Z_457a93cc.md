## Accomplished

Proved `edges_decomposition` (Chapter4/Problem4.12.5, issue #6231) sorry-free — the
icosahedral edge representation `Fin 30 → ℂ` of `A₅` decomposes as
`1 ⊕ 3 ⊕ 3' ⊕ 4² ⊕ 5³` (dimensions `1,3,3,4,4,5,5,5`, the two `3`-summands
non-isomorphic). `#print axioms` shows only `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`
(no `sorryAx`, no `native_decide`).

New shared infrastructure added to `Chapter4/Problem4_12_5.lean` (section `Multiplicity`),
reusable by the two remaining sorries:

- `sum_classfn` — collapses a conjugation-invariant `F : A₅ → ℂ` to the class-size-weighted
  sum over the five class reps (sizes `1,20,15,12,12`), the honest replacement for
  `native_decide` on character inner products.
- `fixCount_conj` — conjugation-invariance of the fixed-point count of any `act`.
- `monotone_initial` — initial-segment characterisation of a down-closed predicate under a
  monotone tuple; pins each `type k` from the fibre cardinalities.

Proof shape: consume `exists_sorted_isotypic_decomposition act` → compute the multiplicities
`⟨χ_perm, χ_i⟩ = (1,1,1,2,3)` from `fixCount_edges = (30,0,2,0,0)` and the class-rep
character values (`irrepA5_character_book`, `FDRep.char_conj`, `classRepA5_inv_conj`) via
`sum_classfn` → deduce `m = 8` (`card_eq_sum_card_fiberwise`) and `subst` → pin

eight finranks and distinguish the two `3`-summands at the 5-cycle class by `√5 ≠ 0`.
