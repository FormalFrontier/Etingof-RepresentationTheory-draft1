## Accomplished

Re-audited all 58 external-dependency coverage records, with particular focus
on the 24 records previously classified as partial or missing, against the
project's pinned Mathlib v4.32.2 revision.  The result is 41 exact, 10 partial,
and 7 missing records, replacing the stale 34/15/9 classification.

Promoted newly verified coverage for finite abelian group classification,
Newton identities, Jordan–Hölder for modules, Wedderburn–Artin, quotients by
free modules, Frobenius reciprocity, and the Reynolds projection.  Refined the
nilpotent-ideal record while retaining its partial status because Mathlib's
commutative ideal induction does not cover the book's noncommutative setting.
Reclassified generalized Schur orthogonality from missing to
partial because Mathlib proves finite-group character orthonormality but not
the full matrix-coefficient/compact-group statement.  Updated other partial
records to name the currently available semistandard-tableau, Lie-group,
idempotent-lifting, character, and Ext APIs.

Corrected ten stale or non-resolving declaration pointers throughout the
ledger.  Also repaired the swapped Chapter 5 dependency attribution:
Frobenius reciprocity now uses the book's `Res ⊣ Ind` orientation and points to
Theorem 5.10.1/Problem 5.10.2, while the genuinely Mackey-style dependency is
attached to Exercise 5.27.3.

Added a generated `MathlibCoverageDeclarations.lean` module containing a
compiler `#check` for every non-empty declaration pointer.  The validator now
requires that module to be mechanically synchronized, verifies audit metadata
against the exact Mathlib revision in `lake-manifest.json`, and has regression
tests.  CI validates the ledger and builds the checker explicitly.  Regenerated
all affected `.refs.md` companions and fixed their generator to handle derived
overlay records and remove stale generated companions.
