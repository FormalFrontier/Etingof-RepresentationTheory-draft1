## Accomplished

Milestone (b) of the four-fold rearrangement for the `Tor` Künneth formula (issue #6686):

New file `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter8/RearrangeBidegreeNat.lean` (sorry-free):

- `Etingof.tensorOverMapₖ` — the `k`-linear functoriality of `tensorOver A N (-)` in its
  right-module argument. Given a `k`-linear, `Aᵐᵒᵖ`-compatible map `f : P →ₗ[k] P'` it produces
  `tensorOver A N P →ₗ[k] tensorOver A N P'`, `⟦p ⊗ n⟧ ↦ ⟦f p ⊗ n⟧`, built via `liftTensorOver`.
  Supporting: `tensorOverMapφ`(+`_tmul`/`_smul`/`_ker`) and the simp lemma `tensorOverMapₖ_mk`.
- `Etingof.restrictK` — restricts an `Aᵐᵒᵖ`-linear map to `k` along the scalar tower
  `IsScalarTower k Aᵐᵒᵖ P` (using `c • p = (c • (1 : Aᵐᵒᵖ)) • p`). With `restrictK_apply`,
  `restrictK_op_smul`.
- `Etingof.map_ext_op_smul` — `f₁ ⊗ₖ f₂` is `(A₁⊗A₂)ᵐᵒᵖ`-compatible for the external actions
  pinned by `hM`/`hM'` (proved on simple tensors by `TensorProduct.induction_on`).
- `Etingof.rearrangeBidegree_naturality` — **the milestone (b) deliverable.** The commuting square
  relating the `tensorOverMapₖ` of `f₁ ⊗ₖ f₂` on the external `tensorOver` and
  `tensorOverMapₖ f₁ ⊗ₖ tensorOverMapₖ f₂` factorwise, intertwined by `rearrangeBidegree`. Proved by
  `QuotientAddGroup.mk_surjective` + nested `TensorProduct.induction_on`, reducing to the two
  factorwise generator computations (both sides `⟦f₁ p₁ ⊗ n₁⟧ ⊗ₖ ⟦f₂ p₂ ⊗ n₂⟧`).

`#print axioms` for all new results is limited to `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`
