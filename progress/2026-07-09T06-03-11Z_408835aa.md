## Key decision / finding

The parent issue's suggested route (relate `#irreducibles` to `finrank Z(k[G]/rad)` and show
`finrank Z(k[G]/rad) < finrank Z(k[G])`) has a **genuine mathematical gap over non-split
fields**: the quotient map `Z(k[G]) → Z(k[G]/rad)` need not be surjective, and
`finrank Z(A/J) < finrank Z(A)` can even reverse for general algebras (upper-triangular
`2×2`: `finrank Z(A) = 1`, `finrank Z(A/J) = 2`). Over a non-split field a Wedderburn block
is `Matrix dᵢ(Dᵢ)` with `finrank_k Z(Dᵢ) ≥ 1`, so the block count is bounded **below** by
`finrank Z(A/J)`, not above. The **cocenter/trace-form route** (which the book's `P²=0` /
"zero trace" hint actually points at) is field-general and gives `≤` cleanly, with
strictness from `P̄ ≠ 0`. All three sub-issues are written around this correct route, with
the caveat documented in #6047.
