## Accomplished

Closed the fidelity gap in `Chapter8/Theorem8.1.5` (issue #5630). The book's
Theorem 8.1.5 states three equivalent conditions (i)-(iii); the Lean file had
only (i)↔(ii) plus Baer's criterion, dropping condition (iii) ("the functor
`Hom_A(?, I)` is exact").

- Added `Etingof.Theorem_8_1_5_i_iff_iii`, formalizing condition (iii) as
  preservation of short exact sequences by the contravariant functor
  `Hom_A(?, I)`: for `0 → K →[ι] M →[π] N → 0`, the sequence
  `0 → Hom(N,I) →[·∘π] Hom(M,I) →[·∘ι] Hom(K,I) → 0` is short exact. This
  mirrors the sibling `Etingof.Theorem_8_1_1_i_iff_iv` (exactness of
  `Hom_A(P, ?)` for projectives, #5629/#5697).
- Forward direction: left exactness (injectivity of `·∘π`, range = ker of
  `·∘ι`) holds for every `I`, using `liftQ` + `quotKerEquivOfSurjective` to
  factor through the cokernel; the surjectivity conjunct is the extension
  property via `Module.Injective.out`.
- Backward direction: applies exactness to the canonical SES
  `0 → N →[α] M →[mkQ] M⧸range α → 0` to recover the extension property.
- Reframed the module docstring and the Baer theorem's docstring to mark
  Baer's criterion as a supplementary characterization, not one of the book's
  three conditions.
- Updated `progress/items.json`: fidelity `gap` → `faithful`.
