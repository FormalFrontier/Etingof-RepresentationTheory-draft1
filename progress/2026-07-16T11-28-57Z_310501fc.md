### The spec bug

`Problem_8_2_8_ext` requires only `Nᵢ` finite-dimensional; `Mᵢ` are arbitrary. That is insufficient:

- **Counterexample to the natural iso** (`i = 0`, `k = A₁ = A₂ = k`, `N₁ = N₂ = k`, `M₁,M₂` infinite-
  dimensional): LHS `= (M₁⊗M₂)*`, RHS `= M₁*⊗M₂*`, and `M₁*⊗M₂* → (M₁⊗M₂)*` is a proper (non-
  surjective) injection. The mandated proof route (finite-dim Hom-tensor iso + field Künneth) yields a
  *natural* iso, which is therefore false here. The crux degreewise map
  `Hom_{A₁}(P₁,N₁)⊗ₖHom_{A₂}(P₂,N₂) → Hom_{A₁⊗A₂}(P₁⊗P₂,N₁⊗N₂)` is an iso only when the `Pᵢ` are
  finitely generated projective, i.e. only when `Mᵢ` admits an f.g. projective resolution.
- **Nuance:** the Lean conclusion is `Nonempty (_ ≃+ _)` — a bare additive-group iso, not natural or
  `k`-linear. That weak form is not *strictly* refuted by the counterexample (both sides can be
  abstractly `≃+` by dimension counting), but it is not provable by the intended route and is weaker
  than the Tor half (a `k`-linear `ModuleCat k` `≅`).

### Recommended fix (in issue comment)

1. Add finiteness on `Mᵢ`: `[FiniteDimensional k A₁] [FiniteDimensional k A₂]` +
   `[FiniteDimensional k M₁] [FiniteDimensional k M₂]` (or `Aᵢ` Noetherian + `Mᵢ` f.g., or `Mᵢ` FP∞).
   `Nᵢ` finite-dim then becomes optional but harmless.
2. Strengthen `≃+` to a `k`-linear / natural iso to match the Tor half.
