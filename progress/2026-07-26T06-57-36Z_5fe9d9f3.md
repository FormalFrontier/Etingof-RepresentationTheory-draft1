## Key patterns discovered

**The trace only sees the diagonal, so no triangularity of the *induced* matrix is needed.**
The book says "put `A` in upper-triangular form, then the induced maps are upper-triangular in
the induced bases". Formalizing that literally would need an ordering on the index sets plus a
triangularity proof. It is strictly easier to compute each diagonal entry on the nose and never
mention the off-diagonal ones:

- Exterior: `exteriorPower.ιMultiDual_apply_ιMulti` says the `s`-diagonal entry of `⋀^n A` is
  literally `det` of a matrix of `b.coord`s, which after `Matrix.det_transpose` is
  `det ((toMatrix b b A).submatrix e e)` for `e = Finset.orderEmbOfFin s`. Because `e` is
  strictly monotone the submatrix inherits `BlockTriangular id`, so
  `Matrix.det_of_upperTriangular` finishes it.
- Symmetric: expanding `symTprod` by `MultilinearMap.map_sum` then
  `MultilinearMap.map_smul_univ` writes the `g`-diagonal entry as
  `∑_{f} (∏ i, A_{f i, g i}) · single (tupleSym f) 1 (tupleSym g)`. Upper-triangularity kills
  every term with `¬(f i ≤ g i)` for some `i`, and `eq_of_tupleSym_eq_of_forall_le` (same
  multiset of entries + pointwise `≤` ⟹ equal, because the index sums agree) kills the rest.

**`obtain ⟨g, rfl⟩ : ∃ g, tupleSym g = s` beats `set g := symIndexRep n s`.** Substituting `s`
away keeps `s` from reappearing *inside* `symIndexRep n s` when you later rewrite backwards along
`tupleSym_symIndexRep`. The `set`/`rw ←` version produced the unusable goal
`toMatrix b b A (symIndexRep n (tupleSym (symIndexRep n s)) i) …`.

**`Finset.sum_subtype` needs its predicate pinned by the expected type.** Used as a `rw` inside a
goal it leaves `?p` unsolved (`⊢ #x = n ↔ ?m x`). Stating the conversion as a standalone `have`
with the subtype sum written out — `∑ s : Set.powersetCard (Fin N) n, …` — determines
`p := (· ∈ Set.powersetCard (Fin N) n)` and it elaborates.

**Mathlib gap confirmed: there is no triangularization theorem.** No `exists_upperTriangular`,
no `schurTriangulation`; `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Eigenspace/Triangularizable.lean` is about
`iSup_maxGenEigenspace_eq_top` and never produces a basis. That is why the hypothesis here is a
triangularizing basis rather than "the characteristic polynomial splits".
