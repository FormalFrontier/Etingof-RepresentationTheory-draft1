## Accomplished

Formalized both `sorry` theorems of Etingof Exercise 9.6.3
(`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter9/Exercise9_6_3.lean`, issue #6206), sorry-free and
axiom-clean (`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`):

- `isProgenerator_iff_hom_simple_ne_zero` — in a finite abelian category, a projective `P`
  is a progenerator iff `Hom(P, L) ≠ 0` for every simple `L`.
- `exists_progenerator` — any finite abelian category has a projective generator.

Key structure:
- New helper `projective_biproduct`: a finite biproduct of projectives is projective. Mathlib's
  `Projective (⨁ g)` instance constrains the index type to the morphism universe `Type v`; this
  universe-polymorphic restatement is needed to biproduct over `Fin n` / the simple-index type in a
  category with arbitrary morphism universe.
- New helper `exists_epi_biproduct_of_hom_simple` (the generation lemma): by strong induction on
  `Etingof.clength X`. A nonzero `X` has a simple subobject `S ↪ X`
  (`CategoryTheory.exists_simple_subobject`, using `Etingof.wellFoundedLT_subobject` to get
  `IsArtinianObject X`); `clength (X/S) < clength X` via `Etingof.clength_additive`; induction gives
  `P^m ↠ X/S`; lift through `X ↠ X/S` and adjoin `P ↠ S ↪ X` to build `P^{m+1} ↠ X`. Epi proven by
  showing the cokernel projection vanishes (cancel the two epis `g` and `π_Q`).
- Forward direction extracts a nonzero component `P ⟶ L` from an epi `P^n ↠ L` via
  `biproduct.hom_ext'`.
- `exists_progenerator` takes `P = ⨁ᵢ Projective.over (simpleObj i)`, projective with `Hom(P,L)≠0`
  for every simple `L`, and applies the characterization.
