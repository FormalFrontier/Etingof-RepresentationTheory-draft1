Structure of the proof (top-down, three commits):

1. **Algebraic assembly of `exists_dihedral_swap`.** The stabilizer `H` of the
   principal pole `b` has order `k`, hence index `2` in `G` (`|G|=2k`), hence is
   normal (`Subgroup.normal_of_index_eq_two`). A coset representative `g ∉ H`
   conjugates `ρ` into `H`; the `SO(3)` image `σ = gρg⁻¹` fixes both `β` and
   `g·b`, and is nontrivial, so by `nontrivial_fixed_unit_vectors` its fixed unit
   vectors are exactly `{β,-β}`. Since `g·b ≠ b`, this forces `g·b = -β` (the
   pole-swap). Then `s := g`.

2. **`so3_sq_of_swap` (pole-swap is an involution).** New shared helper
   `so3_swap_induced_plane`: for `g` with `g·β₀ = -β₀`, the plane `W=(ℝ·β₀)ᗮ` is
   `g`-invariant and the induced isometry `f : W ≃ₗᵢ W` has `det f < 0`
   (orientation-reversing), computed via the axis-quotient acting as `-1`. Then
   `f² = id` via the oriented-angle identity `o.oangle (f y)(f²y) = -o.oangle y (f y)`
   (from `oangle_map` + `map_eq_neg_iff_det_neg` + `oangle_neg_orientation_eq_neg`
   + `oangle_add`). Lifting `f²=id` to `euclideanIso g ∘ euclideanIso g = id` on
   the axis line and `W` gives `g*g = 1`.

3. **`so3_conj_of_swap` (conjugation inverts the axis rotation).** Purely
   algebraic once `so3_sq_of_swap` is available: `g` swaps `β↔-β` so `g²=1`; and
   `g*ρ` *also* swaps `β↔-β` (since `ρ` fixes `β`), so `(gρ)²=1`. Hence
   `gρgρ=1 ⟹ gρg=ρ⁻¹`, and `g⁻¹=g` gives `g*ρ*g⁻¹ = ρ⁻¹`. No second plane
   argument needed.
