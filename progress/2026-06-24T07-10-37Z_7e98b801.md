- Closed the vacuity in `Chapter5/Proposition5_3_2.lean` (issue #5128): replaced

  complete, sorry-free proof.
- Statement: for an irreducible `V : FDRep ℂ G` (`[Simple V]`) of a finite group
  and `g : G`, the number `λ = |C(g)| · χ_V(g) / dim V` (with `C(g)` the conjugacy
  class of `g`) is an algebraic integer (`IsIntegral ℤ`).
- Proof follows Etingof's book proof verbatim: the class sum
  `σ = ∑_{h conj g} ρ(h)` commutes with the `G`-action, so by Schur's lemma
  (`FDRep.finrank_hom_simple_simple`) it equals `c • id`; the trace computation
  gives `c = |C| · χ_V(g) / dim V`; and `c` is integral because `σ` is the image
  of the class sum in the integral group ring `ℤ[G]`
  (`IsIntegral.of_finite`, module-finite over `ℤ`), transferred through the
  representation ring hom and the injective `algebraMap ℂ → End ℂ V`.
- The proof is ported from the already-proven `private lemma
  class_sum_scalar_isIntegral` in `Theorem5_4_4.lean` (which is logically
  downstream, 5.4 > 5.3). Proposition 5.3.2 now lives in its own upstream file as
  intended; a later cleanup could have `Theorem5_4_4` consume it.
