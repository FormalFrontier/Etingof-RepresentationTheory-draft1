## Key decision (plan correction)

The issue planned to prove `trace_combination_vanishes_of_torus_vanishes` from `hLtop`
(spanning ℕ-weight spaces) alone. That is insufficient: the density argument needs the
character to be a *regular* function, i.e. `IsAlgebraicRepresentation`, which is strictly
stronger than `hLtop` for abstract-group representations. So the hypothesis was changed
from `hLtop` to `hLalg : ∀ i, IsAlgebraicRepresentation N (L i).ρ`, threaded through the
seam `formalCharacter_simples_coeff_eq_zero_of_torus_trace_eq_zero` and
`schurWeyl_simples_formalCharacter_linearIndependent_complex`, and *produced* at the
genuine call site `schurWeyl_simples_formalCharacter_classification_core_complex` (which
carries the `e`/`he` decomposition data and `hSne`) via the new summand-algebraicity
helper. No external callers were affected.
