## Accomplished

Proved **Problem 3.3.3(b)** (issue #5996): the representation theory of a single matrix
algebra `Mat_d(k)`, discharging all three `sorry`s in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Problem3_3_3.lean`:

- `std_isSimpleModule` — the standard representation `Fin d → k` is a simple
  `Matrix (Fin d) (Fin d) k`-module (any nonzero vector generates via `E_{ji} (vᵢ)⁻¹`).
- `simpleModule_iso_std` — every finite-dimensional simple `Mat_d(k)`-module is
  isomorphic to `Fin d → k`.
- `finite_iso_std_pow` — every finite-dimensional `Mat_d(k)`-module is isomorphic to
  `Fin n → (Fin d → k)` for some `n` (a direct sum of copies of the standard rep).

Following the issue instruction and the book's hint, this is the **elementary matrix-unit
argument**, deliberately avoiding the Wedderburn–Artin infrastructure that `Theorem3_3_1`
uses. Key device: the private auxiliary lemmas (`smul_comm_k`, `E_smul_E`,
`sum_single_diag_eq_one`, `sum_E_diag_smul`, `A_smul_col`) and the `Mat_d(k)`-linear map
`psi v : (Fin d → k) →ₗ[Mat_d(k)] V`, `w ↦ ∑ₐ wₐ • (E_{a0} • v)`, whose image is the
subrepresentation `S(v)`.

- For (b)-uniqueness: pick a nonzero `v` fixed by `E₀₀`; `psi v` is injective with a
  nonzero image, hence an isomorphism by simplicity.
- For (b)-decomposition: take a basis `v₁,…,vₙ` of `E₀₀ V` (`Module.finBasis`); the map
  `Ψ(f) = ∑ᵢ psi (vᵢ) (fᵢ)` is `Mat_d(k)`-linear, injective (apply `E_{0j}`, use basis
  independence), and surjective (reconstruct `x` from the columns `E_{0a} • x ∈ E₀₀ V`).
