Closed the fidelity gap on `Chapter3/Corollary3.2.1` (issue #5368, wave-1 epic #5338).

- **Gap:** `Etingof.irreducible_interpolation` hypothesised `LinearIndependent A v`
  (independence over the algebra `A`) instead of the book's `LinearIndependent k v`
  (independence over the base field). In a simple `A`-module any two distinct nonzero
  vectors are `A`-dependent, so `A`-independence forces `n ≤ 1`; the old proof literally
  `exfalso`'d the `n ≥ 2` case, making the genuine interpolation content unreachable.

- **Fix:** Restated with the faithful hypotheses — `k` an algebraically closed field, `A` a
  `k`-algebra, `V` a finite-dimensional simple `A`-module with `IsScalarTower k A V`, and
  `LinearIndependent k v`. Proved as a direct corollary of the density theorem
  (`Etingof.density_theorem_part1`, Theorem 3.2.2(i), already in the project):
  1. `k`-independence makes `v` a basis of its span (`Basis.span`), so `Basis.constr`
     defines a `k`-linear map on the span sending `v i ↦ w i`;
  2. `LinearMap.exists_extend` extends it to `f : V →ₗ[k] V` with `f (v i) = w i`;
  3. density gives surjectivity of `A → End_k V`, so `f = a • -` for some `a ∈ A`, whence
     `a • v i = w i`.
