Discharged the `isModularLattice_subobject` sorry (issue #7652, split from #7645) in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Infrastructure/HasFiniteLengthSubobject.lean`: proved that the
subobject lattice of any object in an abelian category is modular. This is a genuine Mathlib gap
(`IsModularLattice` appears nowhere under `Mathlib/CategoryTheory/`).

The proof adds two reusable private helper lemmas and mirrors the module-theoretic modular-law
argument via categorical "refinements" (Bergman element-chasing):

- `factors_of_epi_comp`: epi-descent for subobject factoring — if `π` is epi and `π ≫ a` factors
  through a subobject `T`, then so does `a`. Proved via `a ≫ cokernel.π T.arrow = 0` and
  `ShortComplex.cokernelSequence_exact` + `Exact.lift` (mono `T.arrow` is the kernel of its
  cokernel).
- `sup_factors_refinements`: join membership up to refinements — anything factoring through
  `P ⊔ Q` decomposes, after precomposing with a suitable epi, as `p ≫ P.arrow + q ≫ Q.arrow`.
  Established by identifying `P ⊔ Q = imageSubobject (biprod.desc P.arrow Q.arrow)` and using
  `surjective_up_to_refinements_of_epi` on `factorThruImageSubobject`, then `biprod.desc_eq`.

The modular law `(P ⊔ Q) ⊓ R ≤ P ⊔ Q ⊓ R` (for `P ≤ R`) then follows: decompose the intersection
arrow through `P ⊔ Q`, observe `q ≫ Q.arrow = (π ≫ a) - (p ≫ P.arrow)` factors through `R`
(subtraction in the Hom-group — the categorical stand-in for "b = x - a"), so `q ≫ Q.arrow`
factors through `Q ⊓ R`, and reassemble, descending through the epi.
