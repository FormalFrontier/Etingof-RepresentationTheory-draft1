  (`Etingof.subrepresentation_of_semisimple`, `Chapter3/Proposition3_1_4.lean`)

  citation is not a hidden load-bearing dependency.
- **Formalized Problem 2.3.15 anyway** (it was marked `sorry_free` in items.json
  but had no dedicated file), in `Chapter2/Problem2_3_15.lean`:
  - Part (a) `Etingof.exists_isSimpleModule_of_finite`: a nonzero finite
    dimensional representation of a `k`-algebra `A` has a simple `A`-submodule
    (Artinian-over-`k` ⇒ Artinian-over-`A` via `isArtinian_of_tower` ⇒ atomic
    submodule lattice ⇒ atom = simple submodule).
  - Part (b) `Etingof.not_exists_isSimpleModule_polynomial`: the regular
    representation of `k[x]` on itself has no simple submodule (no minimal
    nonzero ideal; `(x·s)` is strictly smaller and nonzero).
