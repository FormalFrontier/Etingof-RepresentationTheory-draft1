One-shot session on #5863 (`sl₂` JM-uniqueness: assemble `V ≃ W` from equal E-nullity
sequences). The full deliverable `sl2Rep_iso_of_e_jordanType_eq` is a four-piece program;
this session landed the two self-contained, structure-theory-free **invariant-core pieces**
as `Chapter2/Sl2NullitySequence.lean` (added to the `Chapter2` aggregator, axiom-clean:
`propext / Classical.choice / Quot.sound` only):

- **Piece 2 (block-sum nullity).** `piMap_mul`, `piMap_pow`, `kerPiMapEquiv`,
  `piMap_ker_finrank`, `piMap_pow_ker_finrank`: the nullity of a power of a block-diagonal
  operator splits over factors, `dim ker((piMap f)^k) = ∑ i, dim ker(f i^k)`. Specialised
  to standard Jordan shifts as `piShift_pow_ker_finrank`: nullity sequence of `⨁ᵢ J_{0,nᵢ}`
  is `k ↦ ∑ i, min k (n i)` (uses the on-main `jordanShift_pow_ker_finrank`).
- **Piece 3 (multiset inversion).** `nullitySeq_injective`: `k ↦ ∑_{a∈s} min k a` is
  injective on multisets of positive integers. Proof via the first-difference identity
  `null(k+1) = null(k) + geCount(k+1)` (`geCount s k` = #parts ≥ k) and count recovery
  `geCount(v) = count v s + geCount(v+1)`.

Together these show the nullity sequence is a complete invariant of the block-size multiset.
