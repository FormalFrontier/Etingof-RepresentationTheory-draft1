  through `resultant_deriv` (`discr = ±leadingCoeff·res(f,f')`),

  field, for `0 < natDegree f` with `f'.natDegree = natDegree f - 1` (automatic
  in char 0), `f.discr ≠ 0 ↔ f.Separable`. Both directions via `resultant_deriv`
  + `resultant_eq_zero_iff` / `resultant_ne_zero`.
