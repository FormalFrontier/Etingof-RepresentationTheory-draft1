## Current frontier

`of_shortExact` (length additivity): for `0 → X₁ → X₂ → X₃ → 0`, the map
`θ P = (pullback f P, «exists» g P) : Subobject X₂ → Subobject X₁ × Subobject X₃` is monotone;
`finiteDimensionalOrder_prod` + `krullDim_le_of_strictMono` finish **once θ is shown strictly
monotone**. Strictness = injectivity on comparable pairs = the modular-law / short-five-lemma
fact. Blocked on two facts absent from Mathlib: `IsModularLattice (Subobject X)` for abelian
`C`, and the `pullback f`/`«exists» g` ↔ `⊓ K`/`⊔ K` correspondence (`K = im f = ker g`). No
product-krull-dim or modular-Subobject lemma exists upstream; the short-exact stability of
Artinian/Noetherian *objects* is an explicit Mathlib TODO, so that route is no easier.

Decomposed: the remaining `of_shortExact` step is scoped as **#7645** (self-contained, with a
full strategy). Breadcrumb left on #7643.
