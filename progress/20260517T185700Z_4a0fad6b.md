## What landed in this PR

New material in `Chapter6/FieldGenericD5Tilde.lean`:

### Section 5b: `starProj_F ∘ starEmbed_F` left-inverse identities

- `starProj1_F_starEmbed1_F`: `P1 ∘ E1 = id`.
- `starProj1_F_starEmbed2_F`: `P1 ∘ E2 = 0`.
- `starProj2_F_starEmbed2_F`: `P2 ∘ E2 = id`.
- `starProj2_F_starEmbed1_F`: `P2 ∘ E1 = 0`.

These are the basic block-projection / block-embedding identities that
let the γ⁻¹ identity proofs reduce to algebra on the `cumTailSumLin`
component without re-doing entry-by-entry Fin computations.

### Section 5c: `cumTailSumLin` recurrences and the `(I - N)` inverse

- `cumTailSumLin_apply`: closed-form right-tail sum.
- `cumTailSumLin_apply_last`: boundary case `M v ⟨m, _⟩ = v ⟨m, _⟩`.
- `cumTailSumLin_apply_succ`: recursive step
  `M v ⟨i, _⟩ = v ⟨i, _⟩ + M v ⟨i + 1, _⟩` (when `i + 1 < m + 1`).
- `cumTailSumLin_oneSubNilp`: `M (v - N v) = v` (the telescoping
  inverse identity). Proved by reverse induction on `m - i.val` using
  the `_succ` and `_last` recurrences.

### Section 5d: closed-form γ⁻¹ identities on leaf-embedding patterns

- `gammaInv_embed1_plus_embed2_F`:
  `γ⁻¹(starEmbed1_F x + starEmbed2_F x) = starEmbed1_F x`.
- `gammaInv_embed1_plus_embedNshift_F`:
  `γ⁻¹(starEmbed1_F y + starEmbed2_F(N y)) = starEmbed2_F y`.

Both reduce via the projection-embedding identities and (for the
second one) `cumTailSumLin_oneSubNilp`. These are the two identities
the per-(F, Q) D̃₅ indecomposability proof needs for the reversed
`{2, 3}` edge direction case.
