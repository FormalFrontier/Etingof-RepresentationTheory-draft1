## Decisions and patterns

**The crux is injectivity of a determinant pairing, not a bridging construction.** To prove
`ιMulti k N (A ∘ f) = det A • ιMulti k N f` for *arbitrary* `f` (not just a basis), pair against
`D := exteriorPower.alternatingMapLinearEquiv b.det : ⋀[k]^N V →ₗ[k] k` for `b := finBasisOfFinrankEq
k V hN`. Then `Module.Basis.det_comp` closes the goal in one `exact`. `D` is injective because it
is surjective (`D (ιMulti b) = b.det b = 1`) and `finrank (⋀[k]^N V) = N.choose N = 1 = finrank k k`,
via `LinearMap.injective_iff_surjective_of_finrank_eq_finrank`. Reaching for a top-degree
"alternating map is determined by its value on a basis" lemma is a dead end — Mathlib's
`AlternatingMap.eq_smul_basis_det` is `R`-valued only, and there is no module-valued analogue.

**`extPowMap_top` does not need `(2 : k) ≠ 0`, and the issue's suggestion to assume it is
unnecessary.** The identity descends from `exteriorPower_map_top` through
`extPowOfExteriorPower_naturality` and `extPowOfExteriorPower_surjective`, and *surjectivity* of
the comparison map holds over any commutative ring — only *injectivity* (i.e. `exteriorPowerEquiv`)
needs char ≠ 2. `det_comp_of_extPowMap` does still take `h2`, but for a different reason: it needs
`ExtPow k V N` to be nontrivial, which comes from `finrank_extPow`.

**Honest note on circularity.** `Module.Basis.det_comp` is itself proved from `Matrix.det_mul`, so
this derivation of `det (A ∘ₗ B) = det A * det B` is not independent of matrix-determinant
multiplicativity — it cannot be, since Mathlib *defines* `LinearMap.det` through matrices. What is
faithfully recorded here is the book's *structure*: functoriality of `⋀^N` plus one-dimensionality
of the top power. Called out in the PR body rather than left implicit.

**`set` mangles a hypothesis-indexed statement.** A first attempt wrote the lemma about
`finrank k V` directly and used `set N := finrank k V`; that duplicated the argument `f` into a
shadowed `f✝` and left `ιMulti k (finrank k V)` unfolded in the goal, so
`alternatingMapLinearEquiv_apply_ιMulti` never fired. Taking `{N : ℕ} (hN : finrank k V = N)` as
hypotheses instead fixed it and gives a more usable statement.
