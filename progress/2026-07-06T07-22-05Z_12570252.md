`Etingof.Problem_2_3_18`: for a `ℂ`-algebra `A` and an irreducible `A`-module `V` whose
underlying `ℂ`-vector space has at most countable dimension (`Module.rank ℂ V ≤ ℵ₀`), every
`A`-endomorphism `φ : V →ₗ[A] V` is a scalar operator (`∃ c : ℂ, ∀ v, φ v = c • v`).

`sorry`-free, no `native_decide`; `#print axioms` reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice`,
`Quot.sound`. The statement is non-vacuous (e.g. `A = V = ℂ` satisfies all hypotheses).

Proof follows Etingof's hint exactly:
- `D := Module.End A V` is a division ring (`Module.End.instDivisionRing`, i.e. Schur / Prop 2.3.9).
- `D` is at most countably dimensional over `ℂ`: evaluation at a fixed nonzero vector is an
  injective `ℂ`-linear map `D → V`, so `rank ℂ D ≤ rank ℂ V ≤ ℵ₀`
  (`LinearMap.rank_le_of_injective`).
- A non-scalar `φ` is transcendental over `ℂ`: were it algebraic, `minpoly ℂ φ` would have degree
  one (`IsAlgClosed.degree_eq_one_of_irreducible`, using `IsDomain D`), forcing `φ` scalar.
- The field `ℂ(φ)` is realized inside `D` as the **double centralizer**
  `Subalgebra.centralizer ℂ (Set.centralizer {φ})`, a commutative division subring (commutativity
  from `S ⊆ S' ⟹ S'' ⊆ S'`; inverses via `Set.inv_mem_centralizer₀`), upgraded to a field via
  `IsField.toField`. There `Transcendental.linearIndependent_sub_inv` gives the uncountable
  `ℂ`-independent family `{(φ - a)⁻¹ : a ∈ ℂ}`, transported to `D` along the injective inclusion
  (`LinearIndependent.map'`, `map_inv₀`).
- `𝔠 = #ℂ ≤ rank ℂ D ≤ ℵ₀` (`LinearIndependent.cardinal_le_rank`, `Cardinal.mk_complex`)
  contradicts `ℵ₀ < 𝔠` (`Cardinal.aleph0_lt_continuum`).

## Design notes for successors

- The `IsField.toField` route to a subfield of a division ring only needs one commutative
  division subring; the double centralizer `Subalgebra.centralizer ℂ (Set.centralizer {φ})` is
  the cleanest such object (single subalgebra, no nested subtypes) and gives `Algebra ℂ` and an
  injective `AlgHom` to the ambient ring for free via `Subalgebra.val`.
- `RatFunc.liftMonoidWithZeroHom` / `IsLocalization.lift` / `IsFractionRing.lift` all require a
  **commutative** codomain, so they cannot embed a fraction field directly into a noncommutative
  division ring — hence the centralizer construction rather than a fraction-field lift.
- `V` is fixed at universe `Type` so that `#ℂ`, `Module.rank ℂ V`, and `Module.rank ℂ (End A V)`
  all live in `Cardinal.{0}`, letting the same-universe `LinearIndependent.cardinal_le_rank` and
  `LinearMap.rank_le_of_injective` apply without `Cardinal.lift` juggling. `A` stays `Type*`.
