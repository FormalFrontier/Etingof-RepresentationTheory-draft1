## Key proof route discovered (recorded in #4828)
Cleaner than building the degree-0 subfield + its trdeg directly: the
distinguished generator `g* = X t*` is **transcendental** over the image
subfield, because the images are scale-invariant (degree 0) while `g*` has
degree 1 — a Vandermonde argument over the infinite field `k` applied to the
scaling automorphism `scale λ : Frac(k[τ]) ≃ₐ[k] Frac(k[τ])`. Then
`AlgebraicIndependent.option_iff_transcendental` adds `g*` to the (alg.
independent) images, and `AlgebraicIndependent.cardinalMk_le_trdeg` gives
`card σ + 1 ≤ trdeg = card τ`. This avoids defining the degree-0 subfield as an
intermediate field and computing its trdeg via a tower.
