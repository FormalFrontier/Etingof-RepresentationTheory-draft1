## Accomplished

Resolved issue #7436 (Theorem 6.8.1: expose the actual reflection-functor sequence
of an indecomposable representation).

Added `Etingof.Theorem6_8_1_representation` in `Chapter6/CoxeterInfrastructure.lean`:
the source-faithful categorical endpoint of the book's Theorem 6.8.1. For an
arbitrary finite-dimensional indecomposable representation `V` of an orientation `Q`
of a Dynkin diagram, it produces the terminal representation `V⁽ᵐ⁾` — an oriented
quiver `Q_end` (an orientation of the diagram), an indecomposable finite-dimensional
representation `W` on `Q_end` whose dimension vector equals a simple root `αₚ` — plus
the numerical reflection sequence `iteratedSimpleReflection … = αₚ` as its first
conjunct. The numerical `Etingof.Theorem6_8_1` is therefore a consequence, not a
replacement.

Implementation: the reflection-functor sequence was already threaded through the
admissible-ordering walk internally, but the representation at the point a simple
root is reached was discarded (only the numerical equality was exposed). I added a
`TerminalRepData` bundle (orientation + subsingleton-hom + indecomposable rep +
`dim = αₚ`) and carried it through the simple-root (`left`) disjunct of
`walk_admissible_ordering`, `one_round_or_simpleRoot`, and the private
`indecomposable_reduces_to_simpleRoot`. In the base (`IsSimpleAt`) case the terminal
rep is the current rep; in the recursive case it passes through by definitional
equality of `iteratedReversedAtVertices Q_cur (i :: rest.take j)` with
`iteratedReversedAtVertices Q_rev (rest.take j)`. No new mathematics — only exposure
of data the induction already builds via Prop 6.6.5's surjectivity/first-failure
alternative and Props 6.6.7/6.6.8, Lemma 6.7.2.

Updated `Corollary6_8_2`'s and `Corollary6_8_3`'s destructures for the extra
component, the `Theorem6_8_1.lean` module docstring, and the coverage metadata for
`Chapter6/Theorem6.8.1` (`covered_partial` -> `covered`).
