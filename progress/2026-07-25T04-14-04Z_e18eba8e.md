## Decisions and patterns worth keeping

**The generation argument needs no irreducibility input.** The natural-looking route to the `≥`
direction is "`𝔤₁` is the 5-dimensional irreducible `𝔤₀ ≅ 𝔰𝔩₂`-module, so
`⁅𝔤₀·t^{2a}, 𝔤₁·t^{2b+1}⁆ = 𝔤₁·t^{2a+2b+1}`". Proving that irreducibility is real work. It is
entirely avoidable: `ad(gzero 1 · t²)` is *diagonal* on the `gone` basis with eigenvalues
`2, 1, 0, -1, -2`, so it climbs `𝔤₁·t^{2m+1} → 𝔤₁·t^{2m+3}` in four of the five coordinates by a
single division, and the fifth (the zero-weight vector `gone 2`) is reached separately from
`⁅gzero 0 · t², gone 3 · t^{2m+1}⁆ = gone 2 · t^{2m+3}`. The whole induction is then
`𝔤₁·t → 𝔤₀·t² → 𝔤₁·t^{2m+1} → 𝔤₀·t^{2m+2}` with explicit witnesses throughout.

**Don't let a helper lemma conclude at degree `?m + ?n`.** The first version of
`emb_mem_range_of_lie` concluded `emb k (m + n) D ∈ range` with `m`, `n` implicit. Every call site
then asked the unifier to solve `?m + ?n =?= 1` *before* the argument types pinned `m` and `n`
down, and `emb_one_gone_mem_range` died with a 200000-heartbeat `whnf` timeout. Taking the target
degree as a separate implicit `d` with an explicit `hd : m + n = d` (passed as `rfl`, and ordered
*after* `hA`/`hB` so those fix `m`, `n` first) makes it instant. Worth reaching for whenever a
graded-index helper is timing out for no visible reason.

**`ext a b` over `Matrix _ _ (Polynomial k)` introduces three variables, not two** — it keeps
going into `Polynomial.ext`. Use `refine Matrix.ext fun a b => ?_` when the proof is a matrix-entry
calculation whose entries are polynomials.
