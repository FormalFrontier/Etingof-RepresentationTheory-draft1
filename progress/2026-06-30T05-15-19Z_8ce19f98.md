## Accomplished

Resolved fidelity gap #5626 for `Chapter7/Example7.9.5`. The book (Example 7.9.5)
claims the *category* of representations of a finite group is semisimple in the
sense of Definition 7.9.4 (every short exact sequence splits), but the Lean only
proved the ring-level `IsSemisimpleRing (MonoidAlgebra k G)`, never the categorical
statement, and never imported `Etingof.IsSemisimpleCategory`.

Added to `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter7/Example7_9_5.lean`:

* `Etingof.isSemisimpleCategory_moduleCat` — the missing bridge
  `IsSemisimpleRing R → Etingof.IsSemisimpleCategory (ModuleCat R)`. Proof: every
  module over a semisimple ring is a semisimple module, so the range of a mono `S.f`
  has a complement (`exists_isCompl`); `LinearMap.linearProjOfIsCompl` gives a linear
  retraction, and `ShortComplex.Splitting.ofExactOfRetraction` turns the retraction +
  exactness + epi into a splitting.
* `Etingof.maschke_isSemisimpleCategory` — the book's statement:
  `Etingof.IsSemisimpleCategory (ModuleCat (MonoidAlgebra k G))` under the Maschke
  hypothesis, obtained by feeding `maschke_semisimple` into the bridge. `Rep k G` is
  the category of `k[G]`-modules, so this is exactly the representation category.

Existing `Etingof.maschke_semisimple` (ring version) retained.
