> submodule `W` of `⊕ᵢ (Fin nᵢ → Vᵢ)`, there exist `rᵢ ≤ nᵢ` and an

`Pi`-form workhorse (`subrepresentation_of_semisimple_pi`) are exported, plus a
reusable lemma `isIsotypicOfType_fun` (a finite power of a simple module is
isotypic of that type).

Proof strategy (uses Mathlib's `RingTheory.SimpleModule.Isotypic` and
`RingTheory.Length`):
- `W` is the internal direct sum of its `V i`-isotypic components `C i`
  (independence + `⨆ = ⊤` via Schur + pairwise nonisomorphism).
- each `C i ≅ Fin (r i) → V i` (`IsIsotypicOfType.linearEquiv_fun`).
- `r i ≤ n i` by a `Module.length` monotonicity chain: `C i` embeds into the
  `V i`-isotypic component of the ambient `V`, which pairwise nonisomorphism
  confines to the `i`-th summand `Fin (n i) → V i` of length `n i`.
