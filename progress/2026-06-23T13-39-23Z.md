## Key learning (`using!`)
`simpa [lemmas] using h` performs the FINAL unification of `h` against the simplified goal at **reducible** transparency. v4.31 surfaces this as "Type mismatch: After simplification, term h has type A but expected B" when A and B are defeq only **semireducibly** (e.g. `⊥.toSubmodule` vs `⊥`, instance-path or `Function.comp` differences). The faithful fix is `simpa [lemmas] using! h` — the `!` runs that final match at default transparency. Prefer this over `exact h` / hand-added lemmas. (Source: `Lean/Elab/Tactic/Simpa.lean`, `useReducible`.) Use for the remaining bumps.
