**Parts (3)-(4): `End F = A` and `End(𝟭) = Z(A)` had only the determination half.**
`forgetful_natEnd_eq_smul` / `idFunctor_natEnd_eq_smul` say a natural family acts as
multiplication by `η_A 1` — that is injectivity of `End → A`. Nothing stated that every
`a ∈ A` (resp. every central `c`) arises from a natural family, and no ring structure was
transported; `forgetful_smul_comp` is literally `(mul_smul a b m).symm`, independent of
naturality. Three docstrings nonetheless asserted the bijection / ring isomorphism as
established.

Now proved on Mathlib's real categorical `End` (functor categories into a preadditive
category are preadditive, so `End F` is a ring on the nose):

- `Etingof.forgetfulFunctor k A` — `ModuleCat.restrictScalars (algebraMap k A)`;
- `Etingof.forgetfulSmul a` / `Etingof.idSmul c` — the reverse constructions `m ↦ a • m`;
- `Etingof.forgetfulEndRingEquiv : End F ≃+* A`;
- `Etingof.idFunctorEndRingEquiv : End 𝟭 ≃+* Subring.center A`.

`CategoryTheory.End.mul` is in `Function.comp` order (`η * θ = θ ≫ η`), which is exactly
what makes part (3) land on `A` and not `Aᵒᵖ`: `(η * θ)_A 1 = η_A (θ_A 1)`, and
determination turns that into `(η_A 1) * (θ_A 1)`. The file's prose had asserted this;
it is now `map_mul'`.

**Part (2): the field-cardinality hypothesis is gone.** The book states the non-naturality
of `V ↦ V*` for an arbitrary field; the Lean version assumed `∃ l ≠ 0, l² ≠ 1`, which
excludes exactly `𝔽₂` and `𝔽₃` (confirmed by `decide` during the audit). The restriction
was not a fact about those fields but an artefact of testing at the **line** `k`: in
dimension one the scalars exhaust `GL(V)`, and over `𝔽₂`/`𝔽₃` every nonzero scalar
satisfies `l² = 1`, so the obstruction really does vanish there.

Replacing scalars by transvections and `k` by `k³` removes the hypothesis entirely:

- `Etingof.dualTransvection f x hx : V ≃ₗ[k] V`, `v ↦ v + f v • x` for `f x = 0`;
- `Etingof.exists_dual_eq_zero_pair` — once `3 ≤ finrank k V`, any two vectors `u, w` are
  annihilated by a common **nonzero** functional (the evaluation map
  `Dual k V →ₗ[k] (Fin 2 → k)` cannot be injective when `finrank (Dual k V) ≥ 3`);
- `Etingof.dual_gl_natural_eq_zero_of_three_le_finrank` — no hypothesis on `k` at all.

The argument is one line of algebra: pick `f` with `f u = f w = 0` and `p` with `f p = 1`,
then naturality against `T v = v + f v • u` reads `η (p + u) w = η (T p) (T w) = η p w`,
forcing `η u w = 0`. No characteristic split, no transitivity, no basis.
`not_natIso_id_contragredientFunctor` now takes no field hypothesis, and is proved by
extracting the component at `FGModuleCat.of k (Fin 3 → k)`.

The original scalar lemmas (`dual_gl_natural_eq_zero`,
`not_bijective_of_gl_natural_dual`) are kept: they are Etingof's own argument and apply in
dimensions 1 and 2, where the transvection argument does not.

book's claim is actually false without it.
