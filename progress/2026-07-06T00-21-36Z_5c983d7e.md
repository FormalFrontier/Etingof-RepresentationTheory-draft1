`Ind_{S₃}^{S₄}` decomposition isomorphisms of Etingof Discussion 5.11(3), sorry-free, in

Supporting infrastructure added to the same file:

- **Group-generic Frobenius reciprocity** (`frobenius_finrank_gen`, `ind_finrank_eq_scalar_gen`,
  `resObj_character'`): the `Discussion5_11` versions were stated only for `Subgroup S₃`; the
  proofs are group-generic, so restated for any finite `G`.
- **`S₃ ↪ S₄` embedding**: `e3 : Fin 3 ≃ {x : Fin 4 // x ≠ 3}`, `ιHom := extendDomainHom e3`,
  `H := ιHom.range` (the point-stabiliser of `3`), and `φ : S₃ ≃* H` via `MonoidHom.ofInjective`.
- **Transported inducing reps** `indTriv`, `indSign`, `indStd` on `H` (trivial character; `S₄`
  sign restricted; the `S₃` standard rep pulled back along `φ⁻¹`), with `character_comp` to read
  characters of pre-composed reps.
- **Multiplicity lemmas** `ind_mult_triv/sign/std` giving each multiplicity as `⅟6 • ∑_{σ:S₃} …`,
  plus `csum` bridging the complex sum to an integer sum evaluated by `decide`, and the
  character closed forms `trivChar_ιHom`, `signChar_ιHom`, `stdChar_ιHom`, `rotChar_ιHom`,
  `twoDimChar_ιHom` along the embedding.

Each of the fifteen (5 irreducibles × 3 inducing reps) character scalar products is computed by
`decide` over `Perm (Fin 3)` and matched against the biproduct multiplicity from Schur's lemma
(`finrank_hom_biprod` + `finrank_hom_simple_simple`), using the ten pairwise non-isomorphism
lemmas and `S4_simple_iso`.
