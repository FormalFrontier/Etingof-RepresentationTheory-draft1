and the residual was decomposed into open issue #5312. Rather than duplicate

`Chapter2/Problem2_15_1_l.lean`:

- `sl2Pi` — block-diagonal assembly of a finite (arbitrary-index) family of
  sl₂-representations `ρ i` on spaces `W i` into one representation on the
  product `Π i, W i`, with `sl2Pi_apply` / `sl2Pi_e`. Built directly as a
  `LieHom` via `LinearMap.piMap`; `map_lie'` reduces componentwise to each
  `ρ i` being a Lie hom.
- `sl2Transport` — transport of an sl₂-representation across a linear iso
  `e : V ≃ₗ W` by conjugation (`LinearEquiv.lieConj`), with `sl2Transport_apply`.
- `exists_sl2Rep_of_conj_piShift` — combines the two: if a nilpotent `A` is
  conjugate (via `e`) to the block-diagonal sum of standard shifts
  `⨁ᵢ J_{0,nᵢ}` (i.e. `A` has a Jordan basis), then `V` carries an
  sl₂-representation with `E = A`. This is the assembly half of general
  existence, cleanly reduced to the Jordan normal form theorem.

## Current frontier

General existence for arbitrary nilpotent `A` is now reduced to a single
missing input: an explicit Jordan-basis decomposition of a nilpotent
endomorphism (a linear iso `e : V ≃ₗ Π i, Fin (nᵢ) → ℂ` intertwining `A` with
`⨁ᵢ jordanShift nᵢ`). That decomposition is not in Mathlib and must be built
from the PID structure theorem (`Module.equiv_directSum_of_isTorsion`) applied
to the `ℂ[X]`-module `Module.AEval` where `X` acts as `A`.
