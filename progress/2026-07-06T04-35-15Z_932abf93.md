## Accomplished

Landed **piece 1** of the sl₂ Jacobson–Morozov uniqueness program (#5863 / #5867):
the **semisimple decomposition theorem** for finite-dimensional complex `sl(2)`-modules,
in a new file `Chapter2/Sl2SemisimpleDecomposition.lean`.

Deliverable:
```
theorem sl2Module_decomposition (V) [fin-dim complex sl₂-module] :
    ∃ (m : ℕ) (n : Fin m → ℕ),
      Nonempty (V ≃ₗ⁅ℂ,sl2⁆ ∀ i : Fin m, (Fin (n i + 1) → ℂ))
```
Every finite-dimensional `sl(2)`-module is a block-diagonal sum of standard irreducibles.
No new `sorry`; `#print axioms` shows only `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

Supporting infrastructure added (all generic, reusable):
- `instPiLieRingModule` / `instPiLieModule`: componentwise `sl(2)`-action on `∀ i, M i`.
- `instProdLieRingModule` / `instProdLieModule`: componentwise action on `M × N`.
- `prodCongrLie`: assemble two `sl(2)`-equivs into an equiv of products.
- `consBlockLieEquiv`: `(V_{n₀} × ∀ i, V_{n i}) ≃ₗ⁅ℂ,sl2⁆ ∀ j : Fin (m+1), V_{(Fin.cons n₀ n) j}`.
- `lieProdEquivOfIsCompl`: complemented submodules give `↥S × ↥C ≃ₗ⁅ℂ,sl2⁆ V`.
- `exists_atom_lieSubmodule`, `isAtom_isIrreducible`: minimal nonzero submodule is irreducible
  (re-derived from the private versions in `Theorem2_1_1.lean`).

Proof route (issue #5867): strong induction on `Module.finrank ℂ V`. A nonzero `V` has an atom
`S` of the Lie-submodule lattice, irreducible hence `≅ V_{n₀}` (`Problem_2_15_1_f`); complete
reducibility (`complete_reducibility`) splits off a smaller complement `C`; recurse and reassemble
via `consBlockLieEquiv`.

Wired into `Chapter2.lean` aggregator.
