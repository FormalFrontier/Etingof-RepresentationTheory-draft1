## Accomplished

Proved `odd_isSumMatrixAlgebra` (Problem 3.9.5(i), odd case) sorry-free in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Problem3_9_5.lean` (issue #6652). The theorem shows
that for nondegenerate symmetric `B` with `dim V = 2n+1`, `Cl(V) ≃ₐ End(S) × End(S)` with
`dim S = 2ⁿ`.

Key steps of the proof, building on the pre-existing `odd_exists_pi_matrix` infrastructure:

- Took a fresh orthogonal basis `v` and the two-factor Wedderburn decomposition
  `eiso : Cl(V) ≃ₐ P = ∏_{i:Fin 2} M_{dᵢ}(ℂ)`.
- The complementary central idempotents `e0 = (1,0)`, `e1 = (0,1)` pull back to central
  idempotents `g0, g1` of `Cl(V)`; `g0` lies in `span{1, e univ}` (`center_le_span_eUniv`),
  so `g0 = a•1 + b•(e univ)`.
- The idempotent equation, combined with `(e univ)² = μ•1` (`exists_eUniv_sq`) and linear
  independence of `{1, e univ}`, forces `a = 1/2` and `b ≠ 0`.
- The grade involution (`involuteEquiv`) negates `e univ` (odd dimension), hence
  `involute g0 = g1`. Transporting through `eiso` gives an algebra automorphism `ψ : P ≃ₐ P`
  with `ψ e0 = e1`.
- `ψ` maps the coordinate left ideal `e0·P` (finrank `d₀²`) onto `e1·P` (finrank `d₁²`), so
  `d₀ = d₁`; the total dimension `d₀² + d₁² = 2^{2n+1}` then forces `d₀ = d₁ = 2ⁿ`.
- Assembled `P ≃ₐ M_{2ⁿ} × M_{2ⁿ} ≃ₐ End(S) × End(S)` via `RingEquiv.piFinTwo`,
  `reindexAlgEquiv`, `toMatrixAlgEquiv'`, and `AlgEquiv.prodCongr`.

Added `import Mathlib.Tactic.Module` (the `module` tactic closes the linear-combination
identities over the two generators `1, e univ`).
