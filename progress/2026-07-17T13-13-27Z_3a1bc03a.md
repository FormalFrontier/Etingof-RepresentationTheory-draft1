## Notes / patterns discovered

- `omega` does **not** reduce `Fin.val ⟨e, h⟩` to `e` on its own — it treats `↑⟨e, h⟩` as an
  opaque atom. Force the reduction first: `apply Fin.ext; show <lhs> = <rhs>; omega`, or
  `rw [Fin.mk.injEq] at h` to turn `⟨a,_⟩ = ⟨b,_⟩` into `a = b`, or wrap positional
  `(by show … ; omega)` proof args. This bit every index-arithmetic step here.
- Two concrete standard-type adjacency `def`s (matchers with `let`/`min`/`max`) can be equated
  by `show`-ing the fully unfolded `if _ then 1 else 0` on both sides (defeq handles the matcher +
  `let` unfolding) and closing with `split_ifs <;> first | rfl | (exfalso; omega)`. `dtilde_key`
  (~line 140) uses the sibling `= if … := fun _ _ => rfl` restatement trick.
