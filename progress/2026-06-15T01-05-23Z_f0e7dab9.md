## Key patterns discovered

- **smul-instance poisoning by a source-typed carrier.** With `toFun x := x`,
  the equiv's two carriers are defeq, but in `map_smul'` the bound `x` has the
  *source* type `asModule (directSum ρs)`. `HSMul` resolution keys off the
  operand type, so `single g t • x` always picks the *source* `asModule` action
  even when an ascription says the result is the target `DirectSum`. Hence
  `DirectSum.smul_apply` never fires on such a term. Fix: factor the target-side
  fact into a standalone lemma taking a *genuinely target-typed* argument `y`, so
  `single g t • y` resolves to the `DirectSum` action and the componentwise
  rewrites apply.
- **Heterogeneous component coercion.** `DirectSum.ext (β := …)` projects at the
  chosen family; a source-family element under a target-family `(·) i` blocks
  `rw [DirectSum.smul_apply]` (family mismatch in the coe). Routing the source
  side through `Representation.single_smul` + `directSum_apply` to a `lmap` form
  and closing by `rfl` (defeq across the two families) avoids the issue.
