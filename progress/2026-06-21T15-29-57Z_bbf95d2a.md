- `diagTorus k N t` — the diagonal torus element `diag(t) ∈ GL_N(k)` for
  `t : Fin N → kˣ`.

  so the product `diag(t) = ∏ diagUnit` is built one coordinate at a time.

  scalar `∏_i (t i)^{μ i}` (the crux: weight spaces are simultaneous eigenspaces of
  the single-coordinate torus elements).

  `aeval (fun i => t i) (formalCharacter k N M) = trace (M.ρ (diag t))`, proved by
  splitting the trace over the internal weight-space direct sum

  formal character expansion.
- `trace_combination_eq_zero_of_formalCharacter_combination_eq_zero` — payload
  **(B2)**: a vanishing ℚ-combination of formal characters forces the matching
  combination of torus traces to vanish (immediate corollary of B1 via `aeval`).
