2. Main theorem plus reusable helpers `realGEnd_equivariant`,
   `realPlus_eq_complexScalar` (the `ℂ`-linear part of any endomorphism is a complex
   scalar — holds for every irreducible), and `complexToRealGEnd_eq`
   (`z ↦ z.re•1 + z.im•J`).

Key simplification: **injectivity is free** — `Mat₂(ℝ)` is a simple ring
(`IsSimpleRing (Matrix ..)` instance from `DivisionRing.isSimpleRing ℝ`), and a
nonzero ring hom out of a simple ring is injective (`RingHom.injective`). This
avoided a lengthy `1,J,j',Jj'` linear-independence / conjugation argument.
Surjectivity uses the `ℂ ⊕ ℂ·j'` decomposition: `realPlus f` is a complex scalar,
and `realMinus f * j'` is `ℂ`-linear equivariant (composite of two antilinear maps)
hence a scalar by Schur; assemble `f = z•1 + w·j'` and hit the explicit matrix
`!![z.re+w.re, w.im-z.im; z.im+w.im, z.re-w.re]`.
