## Key decisions

- The conjugate-character identity `χ_V(g⁻¹) = conj χ_V(g)` is genuinely new content: the
  project consistently uses `χ_V(g⁻¹)` and never `conj`, and Mathlib has no such lemma. Both
  rigorous routes (charpoly-roots eigenvalue sum, or unitarization + `trace(adjoint f) =
  conj(trace f)` from `LinearMap.toMatrix_adjoint`/`Matrix.trace_conjTranspose`) need
  substantial machinery (eigenvalue-of-inverse multiset lemma, or transporting an
  `InnerProductSpace.Core` to an instance with attendant diamond risk). State-and-sorry +
  decompose was the disciplined one-shot choice.
- Decomposed the two heavy parts into sub-issues rather than attempting heroics:
  - #5180: prove the two §4.4 conjugate-character sorries.
  - #5182: Item 3 — define `V̄` and prove `V̄ ≅ V*` (Discussion after Theorem 4.6.2; needs a
    constructed conjugate-scalar representation, depends on Theorem 4.6.2's unitary structure).
  - #5183: Item 4 — Remark 4.5.3 Frobenius convolution algebra + primitive idempotents (heavy).
