Approach (swap-operator route, no Sym²/Λ² functors):

- Added a reusable swap-trace lemma `trace_comm_comp_map`:
  `trace (comm ∘ map A B) = trace (A ∘ B)` on `W ⊗ W` for finite-dim `W`,
  proved by a tensor-basis (`Module.Basis.tensorProduct`) diagonal computation
  plus `Matrix.trace` of the matrix product. Helper `trace_eq_sum_repr_diag`
  expresses `trace f = ∑ i, b.repr (f (b i)) i`.
- Worked on `Bil = V →ₗ Dual V`, the representation space of `Λ = linHom ρ ρ.dual`.
  `τ` = the `ℂ`-linear flip (`LinearMap.lflip`), `P = averageMap Λ` the averaging
  projector onto `Bil^G = invariants Λ`.
- Per-`g` trace identity `trace (τ ∘ Λ g) = χ(g⁻¹g⁻¹)` via conjugating `τ ∘ Λ g`
  by the iso `E = dualTensorHomEquiv ℂ V (Dual V) : Dual V ⊗ Dual V ≃ Bil`
  (intertwining `(τ∘Λg)∘E = E∘(comm∘map(ρ.dual g)(ρ.dual g))`, then
  `trace_comp_comm'` + the swap-trace lemma). Summed and reindexed `g ↦ g⁻¹` to
  hit `frobeniusSchurIndicator ρ = 1`, giving `trace (τ ∘ P) = 1`.
- Symmetric-part projector `Pₛ = ½(P + τ∘P)` is idempotent and projects onto
  `sym ∩ Bil^G`; `IsProj.trace` gives `2·finrank(sym∩Bil^G) = finrank Bil^G + FS`.
  With `FS = 1`, `2s = d + 1` forces `s ≥ 1`, so a nonzero invariant **symmetric**
  form exists. Extracted and shown symmetric + invariant.

Notable: this existence half needs **no** simplicity hypothesis (it is pure trace
counting); `hρ` is therefore unused here (simplicity only enters the nondegeneracy
bridge). Kept `hρ` in the signature to match the issue and the caller.
