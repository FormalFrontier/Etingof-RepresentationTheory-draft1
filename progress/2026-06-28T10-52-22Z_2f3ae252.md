(iii) the character `formalCharacter M = schurPoly n ν` from #5553
(`formalCharacter_detTwist_linearDual_eq_schurPoly`); plus the `finrank` match via
`finrank_eq_of_formalCharacter_eq`. The FDRep iso is converted to the bundled
intertwining `LinearEquiv` via `intertwines_of_fdRepIso` (#5543's helper).

Key technique: the native `schurModuleRep`/`SchurModuleSubmodule` instances vs the
`FDRep`/`ModuleCat` instances on the eigenbasis `v` form a carrier-alias diamond. Per
the #5563 skill note, `change` (not `rw`) unfolds `M.ρ` across the alias, and the whole
`h_span` derivation is phrased through `(SchurModule k n lz).ρ` (defeq to
`schurModuleRep`, but carrying the same instances as `v`).
