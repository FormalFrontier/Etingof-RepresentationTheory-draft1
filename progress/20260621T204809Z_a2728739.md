### Key facts discovered

- The decomposition's clean meeting point is the single number
  `∏_j C(μ_j+N-1, N-1)`: it is both `coeff μ (formalCharacter A_d)` (= weight-space
  dimension, via `formalCharacter_coeff` + the new eigenbasis) and
  `coeff μ (∑_ν s_ν(1^N) s_ν)` (the Cauchy coefficient). So A and B are
  independent; assembly is `MvPolynomial.ext`.
- The **power-sum** Cauchy identity IS fully proven and sorry-free in the repo
  (`powerSum_bilinear_coeff_gen`, `fullCauchyProd_coeff_eq_card_gen`,
  `PowerSumCauchyBilinear*.lean`), as is Frobenius `p_μ = ∑_λ χ_λ(μ) s_λ`
  (`Proposition5_21_1_univ`) and the hook-length dimension
  (`Proposition5_21_2_dimension`). The **Schur-form** Cauchy identity is *not* yet
  assembled; the likely missing piece is `S_n` character column-orthogonality
  (`∑_μ z_μ^{-1} χ_λ(μ)χ_ρ(μ) = δ_{λρ}`) — flagged in #4958 to check/sub-split.
- `rTransAlgHom_diagonal_X` had to be stated over a single pair `p : Fin N × Fin N`
  (not `(i j : Fin N)`): the `X (i,j)` pattern won't unify with `X x` for a bound
  variable `x` inside a `Finsupp.prod`, so the simp lemma wouldn't fire.
