Approach (matches the book's "enumerate types of roots" hint):
- Roots split into **integer** roots `±eᵢ±eⱼ` (`intVec i j a b`, backed by an ℤ-valued
  `coordZ`) and **half-integer** roots `½(±1,…,±1)` (`halfVec s`).
- Classification lemmas `intShape`/`halfShape`: a root with all-integer coords has exactly
  two `±1` entries (support-card = 2 argument); a root with all-half-integer coords has every
  entry `±½` (sum-of-nonnegatives-is-zero argument).
- Each root family is realized as the injective image of a **decidable finite index set**
  (`Fin 8 × Fin 8 × Bool × Bool` filtered by `i<j`; `Fin 8 → Bool` filtered by even weight),
  so cardinalities fall to `decide` (112 + 128 = 240, etc.).
- E₇/E₆ reuse the same machinery, adding the coordinate constraints `x₀=x₁` (resp. `x₀=x₁=x₂`)
  as ℤ/Bool conditions on the index sets, giving 62+64=126 and 40+32=72.

Key formalization notes for successors:
- `decide` chokes on ℚ coordinate conditions but handles `Fin`/`Bool`/`ℤ`; hence `coordZ`
  is ℤ-valued and all index-set constraints are stated over ℤ, cast to ℚ via `Int.cast_inj`.
- `decide` over `Fin 8 → Bool` (256 elts) needs `set_option maxRecDepth 10000` (else recursion
  depth error).
