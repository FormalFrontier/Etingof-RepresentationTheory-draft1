## Accomplished

Closed the last `sorry` blocking issue #6658 (Ch5 Problem 5.11.1 part (d), order-`12`
conjugacy infrastructure): proved `exists_conj_H12` in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter5/Problem5_11_1.lean` sorry-free. This makes
`indA4_triv` (`Ind_{A₄}^{A₅} 1 ≅ 1 ⊞ 4`) and its helper `countH_eq12` non-vacuous.

Rather than the Sylow-`2`-normalizer route sketched in the issue, I used a cleaner
point-stabilizer / orbit argument:

- `H12_fixes_point`: an order-`12` subgroup `H ≤ A₅` fixes a point of the natural `5`-point
  action `natHom`. Proof by orbit-stabilizer on the `H`-orbit `O` of `0`: `|O| ∣ 12` (via the
  existing `orbit_fiber_card` fiber-count helper from Chapter4), and `1 ≤ |O| ≤ 5`. Size `5` is
  ruled out by divisibility; sizes `2`/`3` put all `12` elements of `H` inside the setwise
  stabilizer of `O`, which has only `6` even permutations (`setwise_le6`, a `decide` — this is
  the parity obstruction to a `2 + 3` orbit split); size `4` forces a fixed point in the
  singleton complement; size `1` gives `0` as a fixed point.
- `exists_conj_H12`: from the fixed point `i`, `H = stabSub natHom i` (both order `12`, via
  `Subgroup.eq_of_le_of_card_ge` + `card_stab_i`); transitivity (`natHom_trans`) gives `d` with
  `natHom d i = 0`, conjugating `stabSub natHom i` to `A4std = stabSub natHom 0`.

New supporting lemmas added just above `exists_conj_H12`: `natHom_trans`, `stab_filter_card_i`,
`card_stab_i`, `setwise_le6`, `H12_fixes_point`.
