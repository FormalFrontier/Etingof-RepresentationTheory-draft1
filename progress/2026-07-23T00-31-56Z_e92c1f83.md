## Accomplished

Worked issue #7443 (Fix Definition 9.6.2: distinguish arbitrary from finite progenerators).
The defect: `Etingof.IsProgenerator` only formalized the *finite*-biproduct variant, so the
book's general Definition 9.6.2 (projective + every object a quotient of an arbitrary,
possibly infinite, coproduct of copies of P) and its regular-module example were not
formalized; the module documentation silently narrowed the example to finitely generated
modules.

Changes (all in Chapter 9, sorry-free, full project `lake build` green):

- `Chapter9/Definition9_6_2.lean`: added the faithful general notion
  `Etingof.IsProjectiveGenerator P := Projective P ∧ IsSeparator P`, with accessors
  (`projective`, `isSeparator`) and `IsProjectiveGenerator.epi_coproduct`, which uses
  Mathlib's `isSeparator_iff_epi` to show the notion is exactly "P projective and every X is
  the epimorphic image of a coproduct of copies of P" — the book's "quotient of a multiple of
  P". Retained `Etingof.IsProgenerator` unchanged but rewrote its docstring to document that it
  is the finite/finitely-generated variant, not the general definition. Kept this file's
  imports lightweight (no ModuleCat) so downstream generic `Projective` typeclass search in the
  finite-abelian-category development is not slowed.

- `Chapter9/Definition9_6_2_Example.lean` (new): `Etingof.moduleCat_regular_isProjectiveGenerator` —
  the regular module `ModuleCat.of R R` over an arbitrary ring `R` is a general projective
  generator. Projectivity is the free-rank-one instance; the separator half is proved directly
  via `LinearMap.toSpanSingleton`. Registered in `Chapter9.lean`.

- `Chapter9/Theorem9_6_4.lean`: `Etingof.IsProgenerator.isProjectiveGenerator` proves finite ⟹
  general (reusing the existing `IsProgenerator.isSeparator`), justifying that every finite
  progenerator consumer is a valid witness of the book definition. Used the structure's
  `toProjective` projection rather than `inferInstance` to avoid the known slow `Projective`
  search in this import-heavy file.

- `progress/items.json`: Definition 9.6.2 metadata updated (fidelity `partial`→`faithful`,
  coverage `covered_partial`→`covered_full`, notes rewritten).
