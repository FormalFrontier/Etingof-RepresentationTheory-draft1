`ℤ`, `k[x]` are commutative, cyclic modules get their `Aᵐᵒᵖ`-action via
`Module.compHom _ ((RingHom.id _).fromOpposite mul_comm)` (`local instance`s). The `k[x]`
answer `k[x]/gcd(f,g)` is written `k[X] ⧸ Ideal.span {f, g}`, avoiding any `GCDMonoid` /
`DecidableEq` instance (in a PID `(f)+(g) = (gcd)`). `HasExt (ModuleCat.{0} ℤ)` resolves via
`Small.{0} ℤ`.
