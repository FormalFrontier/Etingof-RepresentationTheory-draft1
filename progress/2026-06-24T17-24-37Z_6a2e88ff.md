## Accomplished
One-shot session on #5153 (`progenerator_decomposition`, the Krull–Schmidt forward
direction of the Etingof §9.7 projective-generator classification). After investigating
the available infrastructure, I determined the issue is the **full Krull–Schmidt theorem
for finite abelian categories**, which is far larger than a single session, and
**decomposed it into a five-link dependency chain** of self-contained sub-issues with
precise Lean signatures and proof sketches:

- #5205 — Krull–Schmidt 1/5: composition-length function `Etingof.clength : C → ℕ` with
  short-exact additivity (`KrullSchmidt/Length.lean`). Foundation/measure for all later
  links.
- #5206 — Krull–Schmidt 2/5: existence — every object is a finite biproduct of
  indecomposables (`KrullSchmidt/Existence.lean`), by strong induction on `clength`.
  Includes the projective-preserving variant. Depends on #5205.
- #5207 — Krull–Schmidt 3/5: Fitting's lemma + endomorphisms of an indecomposable are
  nilpotent-or-iso + `End X` is a local ring (`KrullSchmidt/Fitting.lean`). The hard core.
  Depends on #5205.
- #5208 — Krull–Schmidt 4/5: exchange/uniqueness — an indecomposable summand of a finite
  biproduct of indecomposables is iso to a factor; full uniqueness companion
  (`KrullSchmidt/Exchange.lean`). Depends on #5206, #5207.
- #5209 — Krull–Schmidt 5/5: assembly that discharges the `sorry` in
  `Introduction_9_7.lean` and closes #5153. Depends on #5206, #5208.

Dependency chain wired via `coordination add-dep`: #5205 → {#5206, #5207} → #5208 → #5209.
Left a `Decomposed into …` breadcrumb on #5153 and `coordination skip`-ped it to `replan`.

## Overall project progress
§9.7 classification: backward direction proved sorry-free; forward direction
(`progenerator_decomposition`) remains the single documented `sorry`, now scoped into the
#5205–#5209 chain. Confirmed via search that Mathlib provides **none** of the needed
pieces for abstract abelian categories: no categorical Krull–Schmidt, no categorical
Fitting decomposition (only for Artinian modules, `RingTheory/Artinian/Module.lean:314`),
and no Jordan–Hölder/length wiring for the subobject lattice (only for `Submodule R M`,
`RingTheory/SimpleModule/Basic.lean:552`). The project has only the `Prop`-valued
`Etingof.HasFiniteLength`, no numeric length.
