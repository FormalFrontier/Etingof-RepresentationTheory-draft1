Key lessons recorded for successors:
- FDRep carriers (`↑(FDRep.of ρ).V`) do **not** accept a direct
  `(u : MvPolynomial …)` coercion; extract underlying data with explicit
  subtype maps (`polyOf`-style) and prove the action/inclusion facts by `rfl`.
- `let`-bound linear maps hide `LinearMap.restrict` from `rw`; use `rfl`-backed
  local `have`s about the let-names instead of `LinearMap.restrict_coe_apply`.
- A term-mode `calc … := (lemma …).symm` step over `glWeightSpace` of FDReps can
  trigger a non-terminating `isDefEq` (it whnf's the FDRep carriers). Use `rw`
  with an explicit weight argument instead — syntactic matching avoids the blow-up.
