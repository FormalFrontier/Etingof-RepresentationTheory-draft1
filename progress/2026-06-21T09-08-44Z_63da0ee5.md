## Key findings / patterns

- The crux (g transcendental over `adjoin k (range (f∘X))`) is the scaling-action
  argument: from `aeval g p = 0` with coeffs in the scale-fixed subalgebra, apply
  every `scale μ` to get `∑_d (c_d g^d) μ^d = 0` for all `μ ∈ kˣ`; the
  single-variable polynomial `pK.comp (C g * X) ∈ K[X]` then has infinitely many
  roots (`{μ ≠ 0}` infinite, `k` infinite, `algebraMap k K` injective ⟹
  `Set.infinite_of_injOn_mapsTo`), so `Polynomial.eq_zero_of_infinite_isRoot`
  kills it; undo the substitution `comp (C g⁻¹ * X)` to recover `pK = 0`, then
  `Polynomial.map_eq_zero_iff` (inclusion injective) gives `p = 0`.
- Assembly: `AlgebraicIndependent.option_iff_transcendental` +
  `AlgebraicIndependent.cardinalMk_le_trdeg` give `card σ + 1 ≤ trdeg k K`.
- Lean detail: pushing `scale μ` through `aeval g p` needs
  `Polynomial.hom_eval₂` at the **RingHom** coercion `(scale μ).toRingHom`; bridge
  `⇑(scale μ)` to `⇑(scale μ).toRingHom` with a `show … from rfl` (defeq). The
  CI runs only `lake build` (no separate linter), so the `show`-tactic style
  warnings on `from rfl` terms are harmless.
- `scale μ` fixes the whole image subalgebra via `Algebra.adjoin_induction`
  (mem → `hscale_f`, algebraMap → `AlgEquiv.commutes`, add/mul → `map_add`/`map_mul`).
