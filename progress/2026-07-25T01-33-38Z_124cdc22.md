## Accomplished

Issue #7423 (feature, claimed via `/work`): Corollary 9.7.3's public API is now coherent —
the algebra handed out by the existence theorem satisfies the hypotheses of the uniqueness
and dimension theorems.

The gap was real and exactly as the issue described. `Etingof.Corollary_9_7_3_i` returned
`IsBasicAlgebra k B` (the book's literal Definition 9.7.2: `B/Rad(B)` commutative) plus a
bare `Etingof.MoritaEquivalent A B`, while `Corollary_9_7_3_i_unique` and
`Corollary_9_7_3_ii` consume `IsBasicAlgebraSplit` (every simple `B`-module is
one-dimensional over `k`) plus `KLinearMoritaEquivalent`. So the `B` you were handed could
not be fed to either advertised consequence.

Five deliverables, all landed:

1. **Strong existence.** `Etingof.exists_basicSplit_klinearMorita`
   (`Infrastructure/BasicAlgebraExistence.lean`) returns `IsBasicAlgebraSplit ∧
   IsBasicAlgebra ∧ KLinearMoritaEquivalent`. This is pure re-exposure, no new mathematics:
   `exists_full_idempotent_basic_corner` already proved *both* basic predicates for the
   corner ring and `klinear_morita_equiv_of_full_idempotent` already proved `k`-linearity;
   `exists_basic_morita_equivalent` simply dropped two of the three. Surfaced in Chapter 9
   as `Etingof.Corollary_9_7_3_i_strong`.

2. **The literal/split bridge** — the one piece that needed actual proving. New file
   `Chapter9/BasicAlgebraSplitBridge.lean`:
   - `IsBasicAlgebraSplit.isBasicAlgebra` (split ⟹ literal). Needs **neither**
     `IsAlgClosed k` **nor** finite-dimensionality, which I had not expected going in.
     `Rad(A)` is the intersection of the maximal left ideals `m`; each `A ⧸ m` is a simple
     module, hence one-dimensional over `k` by hypothesis, so every `a : A` acts on it as a
     `k`-scalar and the commutator `xy - yx` kills the class of `1`, i.e. lies in `m`.
   - `IsBasicAlgebra.isBasicAlgebraSplit` (literal ⟹ split), for `Module.Finite k A` over
     `IsAlgClosed k`. Commutativity of `A/Rad(A)` puts each commutator in `Rad(A)`, which
     annihilates a simple (hence semisimple) module by Mathlib's
     `IsSemisimpleModule.jacobson_le_annihilator`; so the `A`-actions on `M` commute,
     `m ↦ r • m` is an `A`-endomorphism, and Schur over an algebraically closed field
     (`IsSimpleModule.algebraMap_end_bijective_of_isAlgClosed`) makes it a `k`-scalar.
   - `isBasicAlgebra_iff_isBasicAlgebraSplit`.

3. **Bundled theorem.** `Etingof.Corollary_9_7_3` states existence, `dim B ≤ dim A`, and
   uniqueness in one statement using the strong notions throughout, so the `B` produced by
   the existence clause demonstrably meets the uniqueness clause's hypotheses.

4. **Categorical form with uniqueness.** `Etingof.Corollary_9_7_3_i_categorical_strong`
   adds both basicness predicates, the `k`-linear Morita witness, the dimension bound, and
   uniqueness to the `𝒞 ≌ FGModuleCat B` conclusion. See "Blockers" for why uniqueness there
   is stated relative to `(End P)ᵐᵒᵖ` rather than to `𝒞`.

5. **Weak forms preserved.** `Corollary_9_7_3_i`, `_i_fmod`, `_i_categorical` and
   `_i_categorical_fgModule` are untouched. The bridge also gave literal-hypothesis variants
   `Corollary_9_7_3_i_unique'` and `Corollary_9_7_3_ii'`, which take the book's
   `IsBasicAlgebra` directly and lose no strength over an algebraically closed field.

## Blockers

None for this issue. One scope note, recorded in the docstring of
`Corollary_9_7_3_i_categorical_strong` rather than left implicit:

The book phrases part (i)'s uniqueness relative to `𝒞` — any basic `B'` with
`𝒞 ≌ B'`-fmod is isomorphic to `B`. Stating it that way needs the *converse* of
`Etingof.MoritaEquivalent.fgModuleCatEquiv`: an equivalence
`FGModuleCat A ≌ FGModuleCat B'` of finitely generated subcategories must be promoted back
to a `k`-linear Morita equivalence of the full module categories. The project does not have
that yet — it is open issue #7689. So uniqueness is stated relative to the endomorphism
algebra `A = (End P)ᵐᵒᵖ` of the progenerator, which is what the available machinery
supports and is the whole content once #7689 lands, since `𝒞 ≌ FGModuleCat A` by
Theorem 9.6.4.
