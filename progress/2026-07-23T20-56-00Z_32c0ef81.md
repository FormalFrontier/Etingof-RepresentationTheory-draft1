## Accomplished

Closed issue #7564 — "fix(Ch3 Problem 3.3.3(a)): classify product-algebra simples by
factor simples". `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Problem3_3_3.lean` previously stated
part (a) only as a criterion about modules over the *full* product algebra
`∀ i, 𝒜 i`; it never equipped the summand `1ᵢ V` with its factor `𝒜 i`-action, and it
supplied no inflation, simplicity correspondence, or isomorphism-class classification. This
turn added the genuine factor-algebra content (all axiom-clean, no `sorry`):

- `Inflate 𝒜 i W` — a factor `𝒜 i`-module `W` viewed as a representation of the product
  algebra via restriction of scalars along the surjective `Pi.evalRingHom 𝒜 i`
  (`Module.compHom`). Instances for `AddCommGroup`, `Nontrivial`, `Module (𝒜 i)`,
  `Module (∀ j, 𝒜 j)`, plus `prod_smul_def`/`single_smul`.
- `Inflate.isSimpleModule_iff` — `W` is simple over `𝒜 i` iff its inflation is simple over
  the product (submodule lattices correspond), via
  `LinearMap.isSimpleModule_iff_of_bijective` and the identity `toFactor` semilinear map.
- `Inflate.congr` / `Inflate.ofCongr` — inflation is functorial: `𝒜 i`-linear equivalences
  of factor modules correspond to product-linear equivalences of the inflations.
- `Inflate.index_eq_of_equiv` — the factor index is an isomorphism invariant (inflations of
  nontrivial modules from different factors are never isomorphic).
- `factorSummandModule` — the summand `1ᵢ V = range (idemProj i)` as a genuine
  `Module (𝒜 i)`, with `isSimpleModule_summand_iff` (product-simple iff factor-simple) and
  `prod_smul_summand_eq` (the product action on `1ᵢ V` is the inflation of the factor action).
- `simpleModule_prod_iff_factor` — part (a) restated honestly: `V` is simple over the product
  iff for exactly one `i` the summand `1ᵢ V` is simple over the factor `𝒜 i` and `1ⱼ V = ⊥`
  otherwise.
- `exists_isSimpleFactor` / `exists_inflate_of_isSimple` — every simple product-algebra
  representation is (isomorphic to) the inflation of a simple factor representation from a
  unique index. `simpleModule_prod_iff` retained as the idempotent-range corollary.
