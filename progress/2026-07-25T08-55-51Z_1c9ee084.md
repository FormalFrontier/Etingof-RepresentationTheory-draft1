Closed the classification arm of Problem 2.7.4(c). The project already had the dimension result

**The normal form, factored out** (`Problem2_7_4.lean`). `exists_normalForm` now exposes what was
previously buried inside the proof of `finrank_irreducible_charP`: a basis `b : Module.Basis
(Fin p) k V` together with the two structure relations

```
x • b i     = (if (i + 1 : Fin p) = 0 then α else 1) • b (i + 1)
y • b (i+1) = c • b (i + 1) + ((i + 1 : ℕ) : k) • b i
```

built from an eigenvector `v` of `y` (eigenvalue `c`) via `bᵢ = xⁱ · v`, with `α` the scalar by
which the central `xᵖ` acts. Both relations are stated so that only `i + 1` appears, never
`i - 1`: the wraparound cases are then uniform, and the family-side computation reindexes by a
single `Equiv.addRight 1` instead of doing `Fin` value arithmetic. `finrank_irreducible_charP` is
now two lines on top of it. Supporting `Fin`-arithmetic helpers `val_add_one` and `add_one_cases`
were added because Mathlib's `Fin.val_add_one` family is stated for the literal shape
`Fin (n + 1)` and does not apply to a variable modulus `p`.

**Exhaustiveness** (`Problem2_7_4_Family.lean`). `exists_toFamEquiv`: those two relations are
exactly the matrices of `Xlin α` and `Ylin c`, so the coordinate map `b.equivFun.symm` intertwines
the generators, and `smul_comm_of_gens` (new, reusable) promotes that to `WeylAlgebra k`-linearity
because `x` and `y` generate the algebra. The `y` case is where the bookkeeping lives: both sides
split as a `c`-part plus a lowering part, the lowering parts agree termwise once `wY_eq` is
applied, and the `c`-parts differ by exactly one index shift.

**Public endpoint.** `existsUnique_toFamEquiv`: for every finite dimensional simple `V`, there is
a *unique* pair `(α, c)` with `V ≅ V(α,c)` as `WeylAlgebra k`-modules. Existence is
`exists_toFamEquiv`; uniqueness reuses `famEquiv_nonempty_iff` by composing the two isomorphisms.
The conclusion carries the isomorphism itself, not a dimension.

**Dimension recovered from the classification.** `finrank_eq_of_classification` derives
`dim V = p` from the classification plus `famModule_finrank`, routed through
`toFamEquivToLinearEquiv` (an `A`-linear equivalence onto a family member is automatically
`k`-linear, `toFamEquiv_map_smul_field`). `finrank_irreducible_charP` is retained unchanged in
statement.
