  of an iso along a `K`-algebra hom of commutative `K`-algebras. Built by viewing `T` as an
  `S`-algebra through `f`, base-changing `φ` over `S`, and transporting across
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.cancelBaseChange`'s `T ⊗[S] (S ⊗[K] ·) ≅ T ⊗[K] ·`. The `T⊗A`-linearity
  is recovered from `T`-linearity of the base change plus a check that the map commutes with the
  `1⊗a` action (`hcomm`, via `cancelBaseChange` intertwining the `A`-action — lemma `key`).
