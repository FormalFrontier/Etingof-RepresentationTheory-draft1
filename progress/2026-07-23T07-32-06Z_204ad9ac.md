## Accomplished

Restored fresh-buildability of `Chapter5/Theorem5_4_6.lean` (issue #7515).

The file failed a direct source check at line 80 inside `trivialFDRep_simple`: the call
`is_simple_module_of_finrank_eq_one (Module.finrank_self ℂ)` could not synthesize
`IsScalarTower ℂ (MonoidAlgebra ℂ G) (Representation.trivial ℂ G ℂ).asModule`.

Root cause is an instance diamond specific to the trivial representation, whose carrier
is literally `ℂ`. Passing `Module.finrank_self ℂ` pins `Module ℂ V` to
`Complex.instModule` (the self-module), whereas Mathlib's
`IsScalarTower k k[G] ρ.asModule` instance (Basic.lean:262, defined with
`backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false`) is built on the canonical *transferred*
`Module ℂ asModule`. Under reduced transparency these two `Module ℂ ℂ` structures no
longer unify, so the tower synthesis fails.

Fix: fix `V := (Representation.trivial ℂ G ℂ).asModule` explicitly and prove
`Module.finrank ℂ V = 1` via `asModuleEquiv.finrank_eq` + `Module.finrank_self`. This
keeps the canonical transferred `Module ℂ asModule` in play throughout, matching the
scalar-tower instance. No hypotheses or conclusions changed; the public endpoint
`Etingof.Theorem5_4_6` is untouched.

Added `Chapter5/Theorem5_4_6_Test.lean`, a downstream import/`#check` regression test
pinning the public endpoint's signature, and registered it in `Chapter5.lean` (mirrors
the `Theorem5_4_4_Test.lean` convention from sibling issue #7514).
