  `cast`-composition round-trips between `revOut` and `reversedArrow_ne_eq`.

  (the ne_eq map read backwards).
- `Etingof.AdjReducedData.ext` and `Etingof.QuiverRepresentationHom.ext` (both `@[ext]`).

The equivalence itself: `toFun f` restricts `f.app v` through `equivAt_ne⁻¹` (constraint (C)
comes from `f.naturality` on reversed arrows + `mkQ_kills_sourceMap`); `invFun r` uses
`Submodule.liftQ (g r) ∘ equivAt_eq` at `i` (well-definedness = `r.constraint`) and
`r.h v ∘ equivAt_ne` off `i`; `left_inv`/`right_inv` by the two `ext` lemmas, with the
`i`-vertex direction closed by surjectivity of `reflFunctorMinus_mkQ` + `DirectSum`
induction on generators.
