Extended `Chapter2/Problem2_15_1_l.lean`:

- **Block coordinate identification `blockQuotEquiv e : (ℂ[X] ⧸ (Xᵉ)) ≃ₗ[ℂ] (Fin e → ℂ)`**.
  Built via a reversed low-degree coefficient map (`blockCoeffMap`, `blockPolyOf`), with
  both round-trips proved by pure coefficient arithmetic + `Polynomial.X_pow_dvd_iff` (no
  `aeval`, no `finrank`, and handles `e = 0` uniformly). The ℂ-linear map out of the
  ℂ[X]-quotient is obtained by `Submodule.liftQ` over the restricted-scalars submodule —
  the resulting ℂ-quotient type is defeq to the ℂ[X]-quotient the PID theorem produces.
- **`blockQuotEquiv_X_smul`**: multiplication by `X` on `ℂ[X] ⧸ (Xᵉ)` becomes the standard
  nilpotent shift `jordanShift e` under the identification (via `coeff_X_mul`).
- **`aeval'_isTorsion`**: nilpotency of `A` makes `Module.AEval' A` (X acts as A) a torsion
  `ℂ[X]`-module for the prime `X`.
- **`exists_sl2Rep_of_nilpotent`**: for any finite-dimensional `V` and nilpotent `A`, there
  is `ρ : sl2 →ₗ⁅ℂ⁆ End ℂ V` with `ρ sl2_e = A`. Route: `torsion_by_prime_power_decomposition`
  (prime `X`) → per-block `blockQuotEquiv` → `DirectSum.linearEquivFunOnFintype` →
  `LinearEquiv.piCongrRight`, giving a ℂ-linear Jordan basis intertwining `A` with
  `⨁ᵢ jordanShift eᵢ`; then the existing `exists_sl2Rep_of_conj_piShift` finishes. The
  intertwining follows because all intermediate equivs are ℂ[X]-linear, so they commute with
  the `X`-action, and `X` acts as `A` on the source and as the block shifts on the target.

Updated the module + mid-file docstrings to reflect that general existence is now complete.
