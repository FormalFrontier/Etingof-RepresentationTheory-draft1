## Design note (hypotheses)
The map's well-definedness w.r.t. the inner `k`-balancing genuinely needs `k` to act through each
`Aᵢ`, i.e. `IsScalarTower k Aᵢᵐᵒᵖ Pᵢ` and `IsScalarTower k Aᵢ Nᵢ` (the "`k → Z(Aᵢ)`" compatibility),
not merely `SMulCommClass`. These are threaded as hypotheses and documented in the file header.
The external `(A₁⊗A₂)`-actions are taken as parameters pinned on simple tensors (`hM`/`hN`), mirroring
`Problem_8_2_8_tor`.
