## Decisions

* **The intertwiner is just `Xᵐ`.** The issue sketched a diagonal rescaling `eⱼ ↦ cⱼ·eⱼ` with a
  gauge factor chosen to commute with the twisted shift. That works but needs a wraparound case
  analysis to solve the gauge recurrence `γₖ·wX(k-s) = wX(k)·γ(k-1)`. It is unnecessary: `Xᵐ`
  commutes with `x` for free, and conjugating `y` by `xᵐ` rescales `β` by `q^{-m}` straight from the
  defining relation `y x = q·x y`. Concretely `Xᵐ` shifts the index by `m` and the `y`-weights
  `β·qʲ` of `V(α,β)` are exactly the weights `β'·qʲ` of `V(α,β')` read `m` steps later, so no gauge
  factor is needed at all. This removed an entire construction from the file.
* **Type synonym for the carrier.** `V(α,β)` and `V(α',β')` share the underlying space
  `Fin n → ℂ`, so two `Module (qWeylAlgebra ℂ q)` structures cannot both be instances on it and
  `letI` twice does not work (the second shadows the first, and `≃ₗ` would silently use one
  structure on both sides — a vacuous statement). `Fam q α β` fixes this. Baking `N := orderOf q`
  into the synonym rather than carrying an `orderOf q = N` hypothesis is what lets the module
  structure be a real `instance` (the hypothesis becomes `rfl`), which keeps every downstream
  statement free of `letI`.
* **Verified the statement is not vacuous.** Checked with `pp.explicit` that the two sides of
  `fam_nonempty_linearEquiv_iff` really elaborate to `@famQWeylModule q α β` and
  `@famQWeylModule q α' β'`. This is the one thing that could have made the whole theorem
  meaningless, and it is worth re-checking in #7700.
