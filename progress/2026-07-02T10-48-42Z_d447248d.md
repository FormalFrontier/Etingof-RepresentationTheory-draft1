## Notes for future agents

- Instance synthesis over the thrice-nested eigenspace subtype
  (`↥(ker(z−μ⁺) ⊆ Λ²(ℂ⁴) ⊆ W4 ⊗ W4)`) is expensive: `LinearMap.IsProj.trace` needs
  `set_option synthInstance.maxHeartbeats 400000`, an explicit
  `FiniteDimensional.of_finrank_pos` (via `lam2_finrank`), and `Module.Free` supplied by hand as
  `Module.Free.of_divisionRing ℂ (↥…)` with **both** `K` and `V` explicit (the automatic search
  will not select it, and a metavariable `V` leaves the `Semiring ℂ` instance ambiguous).
- `rw [mul_sub]` / `rw [map_sub]` fail to keyed-match on `Module.End` / bundled `trace`; pass the
  operands explicitly (`mul_sub a b c`, `LinearMap.map_sub f a b`) or use `simp only`.
