## Decisions made / patterns discovered

- **Prove `IsEquivalence` from Full + Faithful + EssSurj, not from unit/counit.** Building the
  inverse functor of `ModuleCat (R⧸I) ≌ 𝒞ₖ` by hand means carrying a `letI`-bound module instance
  through `obj`, `map`, `map_id`, `map_comp` and two natural isos. Instead: `ObjectProperty.lift`
  the restriction functor into the full subcategory (fullness/faithfulness are then inherited
  automatically), and supply only `EssSurj`. `Functor.IsEquivalence` is a class whose three fields
  default to `by infer_instance`, so `instance : F.IsEquivalence where` closes it and
  `F.asEquivalence` produces the `≌`.
- **`Module.IsTorsionBySet.module` is the bridge to a quotient ring's module category**, and its
  `mk_smul` is `rfl`, so `(restrictScalars (Ideal.Quotient.mk I)).obj (of _ M)` and `M` are related
  by the *identity* linear equivalence (`LinearEquiv.toModuleIso` with `toFun := id` and all proof
  fields `rfl`).
- **Type ascribe isos through `ObjectProperty.ι`.** In the `EssSurj` proof for the finite length
  version, the goal's source object is `(ι P).obj ⟨…⟩`, and instance synthesis does not see the
  `Module R` structure through it. Stating the iso as a `have` with its unfolded type and closing
  with `exact` works, where inlining the `LinearEquiv.toModuleIso` term fails.
- **`include k` is required for definitions whose *type* does not mention the field.**
  `simpleIdempotent : {e // …}` mentions only `R`, so without `include k` the section variables
  `k`, `[Field k]`, `[Algebra k R]`, `[FiniteDimensional k R]` are silently dropped and the body
  fails with "Unknown identifier `k`". Conversely `omit [Small.{v} R] in` is needed on the few
  declarations that do not use it, or the unused-section-variable linter fires.
- **`set … with h` cannot be followed by a structure instance literal**: in
  `set ψ := LinearMap.id - { toFun := …, … } with hψ`, the `with hψ` is parsed as the
  structure-instance `with`. Use a plain `let` for the map instead.
