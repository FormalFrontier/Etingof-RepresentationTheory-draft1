## Key decision: how the trace was computed

Mathlib has no character formula for induced representations that applies here, and the
existing `Theorem5_25_2.lean` computes principal-series characters one conjugacy class at
a time through a hand-built evaluation equivalence (hundreds of lines, `maxHeartbeats
1600000`). That approach does not scale to a general subgroup.

Instead, the whole character is obtained in one shot from the averaging idempotent

```
(P f) g = |H|⁻¹ * ∑ h : H, (lam h)⁻¹ * f (h * g)
```

on `G → ℂ`. `P` is idempotent with image the covariance submodule and commutes with right
translation `R a`, so writing `P ∘ₗ R a = subtype ∘ₗ S a` with `S a : (G → ℂ) →ₗ V` and
applying `LinearMap.trace_comp_comm'` moves the trace off the submodule onto all of
`G → ℂ`, where the standard delta-function basis makes the diagonal entry a one-term sum
(`h * g * a = g` forces `h = g a⁻¹ g⁻¹`). Reindexing `g ↦ g⁻¹` produces exactly the shape
`complementarySeriesChar` uses. `finrank = |G|/|H|` then falls out of `FDRep.char_one`
applied to the same formula at `a = 1` — no coset transversal needed anywhere.

This pattern is worth reusing: any future "what is the character of this induced thing"
question in the project should go through `Etingof.InducedChar` rather than a bespoke
basis.

## Gotcha worth recording

`FDRep ℂ G` forces the carrier into `Type 0`, so `InducedChar` is stated for
`{G : Type}`, not `{G : Type*}`.

Instance-mismatch on `Fintype ↥K` bit twice: a lemma stated with `Fintype.card ↥K` under
`open scoped Classical in` will not `rw` into a goal whose `Fintype ↥K` came from a
different elaboration. Stating `card_ellipticSubgroup` with `Nat.card` and going through
`← Nat.card_eq_fintype_card` at the use site sidesteps it, because `Nat.card` carries no
instance.
