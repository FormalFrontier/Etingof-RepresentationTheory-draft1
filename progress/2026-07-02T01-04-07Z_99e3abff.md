## Accomplished

Proved the **transvection-connectivity** ingredient for the symmetric half of
Problem 4.12.3 / Example 5.19.3 (issue #5754, sub-issue of #5638), sorry-free and
axiom-clean (`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound` only). New file
`Chapter5/SymmetricTransvection.lean`, added to the `Chapter5.lean` aggregator.

Deliverables (all in `namespace Etingof`, over `symmetricPowerMonomialBasis`):

- `transvectionMap` / `transvection` — the transvection `1 + Eᵢⱼ` as a linear map and
  (for `i ≠ j`) a `V ≃ₗ[k] V`, with `transvectionMap_apply_basis` and `transvection_coe`.
- `symmetricPowerMap_transvection_eq_sum` — the action of `1 + Eᵢⱼ` on a monomial basis
  vector of `SⁿV`, expanded via `MultilinearMap.map_add_univ` into a sum over subsets `s`
  of the `j`-positions of the re-labelled monomials (positions in `s` sent from `j` to `i`).
- `transvection_repr_ne_zero` — the coordinate of `(1+Eᵢⱼ)·e^α` at the single-unit-move
  monomial `e^{α−eⱼ+eᵢ}` is the (positive) count of movable `j`-positions, hence nonzero in
  char 0 (via `Finset.sum_boole` + a `{m₀}` witness). No diagonal machinery needed.
- `MonomialAdj` + `monomialAdj_connected` — single-unit moves connect any two degree-`n`
  monomials (strong induction on the differing-position finset).
- `monomialAdj_step_mem` — the `hstep` hypothesis of
  `DiagonalCoordinate.eq_bot_or_eq_top_of_connected`: given the diagonal `T` facts (`hT`,`hw`,
  `hWT`) and `GL(V)`-stability (`hWG`), a transvection realises each `Adj`-step inside `W`.
