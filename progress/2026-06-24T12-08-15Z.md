- `Etingof.coreOfLinearEquiv (e : V ≃ₗ[ℂ] F)` — transport an inner product from an

  a starting positive definite Hermitian form on any finite-dim complex space (via a
  basis equiv to `EuclideanSpace`).
- `Etingof.avgCore ρ c` — Weyl's averaging trick: `B̄(v,w) = Σ_{g∈G} c(ρ g v, ρ g w)`
  is again a positive definite Hermitian form. Definiteness uses that the real part
  of the sum is a sum of nonnegatives, so the `g = 1` term `re c(v,v)` vanishes, and
  Hermitian symmetry makes `c(v,v)` real, hence `0`, hence `v = 0`.
- `Etingof.Theorem4_6_2_existence` — every finite-dim complex rep of a finite group
  admits a `G`-invariant positive definite Hermitian form. **Sorry-free**
