## Accomplished

Formalized **Krull–Schmidt link 2/5** (issue #5206): the existence half of the

`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter9/KrullSchmidt/Existence.lean`.

New declarations:

- `Etingof.clength_eq_of_iso` — composition length is an isomorphism invariant
  (via `Subobject.mapIsoToOrderIso` + `Order.height_orderIso`). Needed for the
  well-founded induction measure to descend across the splitting iso `X ≅ Y ⊞ Z`.
- `Etingof.biproductSumIso` — `(⨁ f₁) ⊞ (⨁ f₂) ≅ ⨁ (Sum.elim f₁ f₂)`, the
  reindexing iso that concatenates two finite indecomposable families. Mathlib has
  no direct biproduct-over-a-sum lemma, so this is built explicitly with
  `biproduct.desc`/`biprod.desc` and discharged by `hom_ext` + `simp`.
- `Etingof.exists_indecomposable_biproduct` — every object is a finite biproduct of
  indecomposables. Strong induction on `clength X` (`Nat.strong_induction_on`):
  zero ⇒ empty `Empty` family; `Indecomposable X` ⇒ `PUnit`; otherwise unfold the
  negation to get `X ≅ Y ⊞ Z` with `Y, Z` nonzero, recurse via `clength_biprod` +
  `clength_pos_of_not_isZero`, concatenate over `κ_Y ⊕ κ_Z`.
- `Etingof.exists_indecomposable_projective_biproduct` — the projective-preserving
  variant consumed by the §9.7 assembly. Each summand `f k` is a retract of `X`
  through `biproduct.ι`/`biproduct.π`, and `Retract.projective` carries projectivity.
