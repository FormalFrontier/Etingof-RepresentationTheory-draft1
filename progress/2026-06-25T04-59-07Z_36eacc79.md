## Accomplished

One-shot feature session `36eacc79` (`/once 5247`): proved the keystone lemma
`Etingof.charEq_iso` — for finite-dimensional complex representations of a finite
group, equal characters imply isomorphism (the converse of `FDRep.char_iso`).

New file `Chapter5/CharEqIso.lean` (sorry-free, `#print axioms` =
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`), added to the `Chapter5.lean`
aggregator.

The proof stays entirely inside the semisimple ℂ-linear category `FDRep ℂ G`,
reusing Mathlib's categorical Schur lemma and the character/scalar-product bridge:

- `finrank_hom_eq_of_character_eq`: equal characters force
  `finrank ℂ (S ⟶ V) = finrank ℂ (S ⟶ W)` for every `S`, via
  `FDRep.scalar_product_char_eq_finrank_equivariant`.
- `iso_of_forall_finrank_hom_eq` (the core): two objects with equal
  `finrank ℂ (S ⟶ -)` for all simple `S` are isomorphic. Strong induction on
  `finrank ℂ V`: peel a simple subobject `S₀ ↪ V` (exists since FDRep objects are
  Artinian), which splits off because every FDRep is injective (Maschke /
  `FinGroupCharZero`), then match the complement by induction.

Supporting infrastructure built here (all reusable):
- `instIsArtinianObjectFDRep`: every `FDRep ℂ G` is an `IsArtinianObject`. Proved
  via the strictly-monotone length `finrank ℂ` on the subobject lattice (mono ⟹
  underlying injective ⟹ `finrank ≤`, with equality forcing an underlying iso that
  the forgetful functor reflects). This was the crux missing-Mathlib piece.
- `finrank_biprod_obj` / `finrank_hom_biprod`: `finrank ℂ` additivity on binary
  biproducts (objects and hom-spaces).
- `splitSummand`: a split mono splits off a biproduct summand.
- `homCongrRight`, `homBiprodEquiv`, `isZero_of_finrank_eq_zero`, etc.

## Key decisions

- Chose the fully-categorical route (induction in `FDRep`) over module-level
  decomposition. It reuses `finrank_hom_simple_simple` and
  `scalar_product_char_eq_finrank_equivariant` directly with no `asModule`
  bridges. The only genuinely missing Mathlib fact was `IsArtinianObject (FDRep)`,
  proved here from the finrank length function.
