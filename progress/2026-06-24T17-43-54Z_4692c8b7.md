## Accomplished

Formalized the **discussion after Theorem 4.6.2** (issue #5182): the complex
conjugate representation `V̄` of a finite-dimensional complex representation `V`
of a finite group is isomorphic to the dual `V*`. New file
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Discussion_after_Theorem4_6_2.lean`
(added to the `Chapter4` aggregator), building cleanly with **no sorries**
(`#print axioms` shows only `propext`/`Classical.choice`/`Quot.sound`).

Genuine constructed objects:
- `Etingof.Conjugate V` — the conjugate `ℂ`-vector space: a type synonym of `V`
  carrying the conjugate scalar action `z • v = z̄ • v` via
  `Module.compHom V (starRingEnd ℂ)`. The `smul_def` reduction lemma is `rfl`
  (using `show V from v` to pick the underlying `V`-action, not the `(v : V)`
  ascription which loops/mis-resolves).
- `Etingof.conjugateRep ρ` — the conjugate representation `V̄`, a real
  `Representation ℂ G (Conjugate V)` with the same underlying `G`-action as `ρ`
  (a `ℂ`-linear map is automatically linear for the conjugate action, so each
  `ρ g` lifts unchanged).
- `Etingof.conjEquivDual c` — the `ℂ`-linear equivalence `V̄ ≃ₗ[ℂ] V*` attached
  to a positive definite Hermitian form `c`, repackaging the conjugate-linear
  `V ≃ₛₗ[starRingEnd ℂ] V*` of Theorem 4.6.2 as genuinely `ℂ`-linear out of the
  conjugate space.

Proved:
- `conjEquivDual_intertwines` — `conjEquivDual c` is `G`-equivariant for an
  invariant form `c` (intertwines `conjugateRep ρ` with `Representation.dual ρ`).
- `conjugate_iso_dual` — **main result**: for a finite-dimensional complex rep of
  a finite group there is a `G`-equivariant `ℂ`-linear isomorphism `V̄ ≅ V*`,
  supplied by the Theorem 4.6.2 unitary structure. Fully sorry-free.

Supporting edit: de-privatized `Theorem4_6_2.innerEquivDual` and its
`@[simp]` apply lemma (previously `private`) so the new file can reuse the
nondegenerate conjugate-linear `V ≃ₛₗ V*` instead of duplicating ~60 lines of
nondegeneracy/surjectivity plumbing. No signature changes; purely additive to
the public API.

## Key decisions

- Stated `V̄ ≅ V*` as the existence of an intertwining `ℂ`-linear equivalence
  (`∃ e : Conjugate V ≃ₗ[ℂ] Module.Dual ℂ V, ∀ g v, e (conjugateRep ρ g v) =
  dual ρ g (e v)`). This is the standard, non-vacuous meaning of a representation
  isomorphism and avoids bundling a bespoke iso type.
- Used the physics-convention `InnerProductSpace.Core` (antilinear in the first
  argument) directly: `v ↦ c(v, ·)` is conjugate-linear out of `V`, hence
  `ℂ`-linear out of `V̄`, landing in `V*` (linear in the second argument).
- Skipped the **optional** character identity `χ_{V̄}(g) = conj χ_V(g)`: proving it
  cleanly needs trace-over-conjugate-module machinery plus `char_inv_eq_conj`
  (still sorried in `Discussion_4_4.lean`), so it would add risk/sorry-dependence
  for an explicitly optional deliverable.
