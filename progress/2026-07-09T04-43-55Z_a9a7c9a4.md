  `2·r(1) = 1` in `ℤ` (impossible). Built via `ShortComplex.moduleCatMk`;
  exactness through `moduleCat_exact_iff_ker_sub_range`, mono/epi through
  `mono_iff_injective`/`epi_iff_surjective`.
