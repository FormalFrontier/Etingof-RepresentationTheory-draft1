## Accomplished

Worked issue #6714 (Frobenius: `dim` of a complex irreducible divides `|G|`),
which covered two remaining sorries A2/A3 in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter6/DimDvdCard.lean`.

- **A3 proved sorry-free:** `finrank_dvd_card_of_irreducible`. Assembly:
  - Schur orthogonality (`FDRep.char_orthonormal V V`, `[IsAlgClosed ℂ]`, chosen
    over `simple_iff_char_is_norm_one` because the latter forces `k` and `G` into
    the same universe, incompatible with `G : Type*`) gives
    `∑_g χ(g)χ(g⁻¹) = |G|`.
  - Reindexed the group sum over conjugacy classes with
    `Finset.sum_fiberwise_of_maps_to` along `ConjClasses.mk`; each fiber is
    constant (character is a class function, on `g` and `g⁻¹`) with cardinality
    `Nat.card {x // IsConj g₀ x}`, giving `|G| = ∑_C |C|·χ(g_C)·χ(g_C⁻¹)`.
  - `|G|/d = ∑_C ω_C·χ(g_C⁻¹)` is a sum of products of algebraic integers
    (`ω_C` from A2, `χ(g_C⁻¹)` from A1 `isIntegral_character`), hence integral
    over `ℤ`; being rational it is an integer (`isIntegral_algebraMap_iff` +
    `IsIntegrallyClosed.isIntegral_iff`), so `d ∣ |G|`.
- **A2 decomposed into #6723** (`isIntegral_classSum_scalar`), the substantial
  central-character/class-sum machinery. Mathlib has no class-sum or
  central-character infrastructure (verified), so the sub-issue carries a
  detailed from-scratch route: class sum in `MonoidAlgebra ℤ G`, module-finite
  center over `ℤ` ⇒ integral, trace central character as a ring hom on the
  center (Schur scalar from `finrank (V ⟶ V) = 1`), value `|C|·χ(g₀)/d`.
