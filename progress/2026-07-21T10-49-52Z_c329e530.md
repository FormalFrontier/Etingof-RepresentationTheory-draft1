## Accomplished

Issue #7211: exposed Problem 4.12.2 part (d)'s full irrep classification of the
Heisenberg group `H_p` as reusable headline theorems in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Problem4_12_2.lean`.

- New headline `simple_iso_charRep_or_rhoHom`: every finite-dimensional simple
  complex `Heisenberg p`-representation `U` is isomorphic either to a
  `1`-dimensional character `FDRep.of (charRep χ)` (`χ : Heisenberg p →* ℂˣ`) or
  to a `p`-dimensional `FDRep.of (rhoHom z hz)` for a `p`-th root of unity
  `z ≠ 1`. This lifts the family construction + completeness argument (family
  `E`, pairwise non-iso `hEinj`, sum-of-squares `hEsum`, Wedderburn injection,
  pigeonhole surjectivity `hcsurj`) that previously lived *inside* the
  `irreducible_dim` proof.
- Uniqueness/distinctness companions: `charRep_iso_iff` (characters isomorphic
  iff equal), `rhoHom_iso_iff` (`R_z` isomorphic iff the roots agree — separated
  by the central character `z^{(-1).val}·p`), and `charRep_not_iso_rhoHom` (the
  two branches are disjoint, dimension `1 ≠ p`). Together these pin down the
  `χ`/`z` uniquely, matching the book's "unique" classification.
- Refactored `irreducible_dim` to *derive* from `simple_iso_charRep_or_rhoHom`:
  it keeps the transport of `σ` to a `FDRep` `U` in `ℂ`'s universe, applies the
  classification, and reads off the dimension (`1` or `p`). Signature unchanged,
  so downstream users are unaffected.
- Updated the module docstring and `progress/items.json` coverage note.
