## Accomplished

Closed issue #7434 (`Problem 9.4.6(ii): certify A·eᵢ as the vertex-simple projective covers
before calling the matrix Cartan`).

The complaint was real and the audit accurate: `Etingof.algebraCartanMatrix` accepts *any*
indexed family of modules, so `cartanMatrix_pathAlgebra_eq_pathCount'` computed a Hom-dimension
matrix for `A·eᵢ` while the docstrings called those modules "indecomposable projective covers"
with nothing proved. New file `Chapter9/PathAlgebraVertexCovers.lean` (sorry-free, axiom-clean)
supplies the missing certification:

* `augModule_isSimpleModule` — the vertex module `Sᵢ` is a simple `A`-module.
* `augModule_not_isomorphic_of_ne` — distinct vertices give non-isomorphic simples (`eᵢ` acts as
  the identity on `Sᵢ` and as zero on `Sⱼ`).
* `exists_augModule_iso` — every simple `A`-module is some `Sᵢ`. Together with the previous two,
  the vertices index the simple modules exactly, which is what makes the matrix's index type
  correct.
* `pathAlgebraProj_projective` — `Pᵢ = A·eᵢ` is projective (`Module.Projective.of_split` on the
  retraction `a ↦ a·eᵢ` of `Pᵢ ↪ A`).
* `pathAlgebraProj_isIndecomposable` — for acyclic `Q`, `End_A(Pᵢ) ≅ (paths i → i) →₀ k` is
  one-dimensional over `k`, so the idempotent coming from any decomposition is `0` or the
  identity.
* `projToSimple` / `projToSimple_surjective` / `projToSimple_essential` — the augmentation
  `π : Pᵢ ↠ Sᵢ` has superfluous kernel.
* `vertexProjectiveCover` — the above packaged as `Etingof.ProjectiveCover`, with `.P` reducing
  to `pathAlgebraProj k Q i` by `rfl`.
* `Problem946.cartanMatrix_pathAlgebra_eq_pathCount_of_projectiveCovers` — the connection: the
  five conjuncts assert simplicity, pairwise non-isomorphy, exhaustiveness, that the cover of
  `Sᵢ` is the very `Pᵢ` the matrix is built from, and the path-count equality.

Two pieces the issue asked to construct already existed and were reused rather than rebuilt:
`augHom` (the vertex character `A →+* k`) and `augModule` (the vertex simple module), both in
`PathAlgebraLowerBound.lean`, where they were introduced for the homological-dimension lower
bound. Grepping first turned deliverable 1 from a construction into a simplicity proof.

Unsupported docstring claims in `PathAlgebraProjectiveCover.lean` reworded to describe `A·eᵢ` as
the principal left ideal, pointing at the new file for the cover statement.
`progress/items.json` for `Chapter9/Problem9.4.6` moves `covered_partial → covered`, the
`followup_issue: 7434` field is dropped, and the `coverage_note` records the certification.

## Current frontier

Mathematical content of the issue is complete. Both halves of Problem 9.4.6 are now closed:
part (i) (homological dimension 1) was already sorry-free, and part (ii)'s Cartan matrix is
certified against Definition 9.3.1.

Acyclicity is used in exactly two places, both through "the only path `i → i` is trivial": it
makes `eᵢ A eᵢ` the line `k·eᵢ`, which gives both the one-dimensionality of `End_A(Pᵢ)` and the
essentiality of `π`. Projectivity and simplicity of `Sᵢ` need no acyclicity.

## Next step

No follow-up is needed for this item. A reusable observation for whoever next audits a

projective covers of the simples, indexed without repetition or omission. `Chapter9/Problem9_3_2`
