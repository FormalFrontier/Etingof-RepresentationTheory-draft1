  for every parameter pair `(α, β) ∈ ℂˣ × ℂˣ`.

Proof follows the book's "similar to Problem 2.7.4(c)" hint:

1. A nonzero `A`-submodule `W` is closed under the ambient `ℂ`-action, because `ℂ` acts through
   `qWeylAlgebra ℂ q` (`IsScalarTower`, via `c • v = (c • (1 : A)) • v`).
2. Take `w ∈ W`, `w ≠ 0`, and `k` with `w k ≠ 0`. Applying the Lagrange-style separator
   `∏_{j ≠ k} (y - β·qʲ)` to `w` gives `(∏_{j ≠ k}(β·qᵏ - β·qʲ)) · (w k) • e_k`. The separator was
   built as an explicit `Finset.induction_on` over the product set rather than through polynomial
   API: at each step the operator is `Ylin _ - wY a • _`, which is visibly a `Submodule.sub_mem`.
   Distinctness of the eigenvalues makes the leading scalar nonzero, so `e_k ∈ W`.
3. `Xlin (e_m) = wX (m+1) • e_{m+1}` with `wX ≠ 0`, so `W` is closed under `m ↦ m + 1` on indices.
   Sweeping the cycle from `k` gives every `e_i ∈ W`, and the `e_i` span, so `W = ⊤`.
