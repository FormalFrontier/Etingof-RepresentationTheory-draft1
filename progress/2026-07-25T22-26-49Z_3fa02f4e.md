* **Reduction.** `nonempty_homotopyEquiv_homologyZeroComplex` upgrades Problem 7.8.7(iii) plus

  `homologyZeroComplex C ≃ C`: in `C ≅ E ⊞ homologyZeroComplex C` the composite
  `biprod.fst ≫ biprod.inl` is null-homotopic because `𝟙 E` is, so `biprod.total` makes
  `𝟙` homotopic to `biprod.snd ≫ biprod.inr`. `tensorHomotopy` (via
  `HomologicalComplex.mapBifunctorMapHomotopy₁`/`₂`) transports homotopies through the tensor
  product, so `kunnethSource i` and `kunnethTarget i` both send that homotopy equivalence to an
  isomorphism (`kunnethSource_map_eq_id`, `kunnethTarget_map_eq_id`,
  `isIso_map_of_homotopyEquiv`). The naturality square of `kunnethNatTrans` then yields
  `isIso_kunnethNatTrans_app` and `isIso_kunnethMap` for arbitrary `C`, `D`.

## Deviation from the issue's proof plan

Deliverable 1 of #7830 asked for additivity of `kunnethSource`/`kunnethTarget` and the
compatibility of `κ` with `Functor.mapBiprod`, in order to run an induction over the four
summands of `(E ⊞ H_C) ⊗ (F ⊞ H_D)`. That bookkeeping is not needed on the route taken here:
replacing the abstract biproduct splitting by an explicit homotopy equivalence
`homologyZeroComplex C ≃ C` lets the naturality square of `kunnethNatTrans` transport the
zero-differential case directly. With `κ` known to be an isomorphism in all cases, the
requested conclusion `IsIso κ_{X ⊞ Y, D} ↔ IsIso κ_{X,D} ∧ IsIso κ_{Y,D}` is vacuous, so it was
not added. Deliverable 2 (the acyclic case) *was* added, in the informative form "both sides
are zero objects" rather than the vacuous "κ is invertible there". This is documented in the
module docstring.
