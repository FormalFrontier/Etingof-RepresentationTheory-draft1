- `Etingof.HasFiniteLength : C → Prop` — inductive predicate encoding "finite filtration

- `Etingof.IsFiniteAbelianCategoryOverField k C` — a **new** class extending the existing
  `[IsFiniteAbelianCategory C]` with `[Linear k C]`, the finite-length condition, and
  `End(X) ≅ k` (`Nonempty (End X ≃ₐ[k] k)`) for every simple `X`.
- `Etingof.IsFiniteAbelianCategoryOverField.finiteDimensional_hom (X Y) :
  FiniteDimensional k (X ⟶ Y)` — the "check it!" remark. Proof is Etingof's elementary
  double induction on lengths of `X` and `Y`: Schur's lemma
  (`isIso_of_hom_simple` + `End ≅ k`) for the simple/simple base, and left-exactness of
  `Hom(X,-)` / `Hom(-,Y)` (via `ShortExact.fIsKernel` / `gIsCokernel` and the
  `Module.Finite.of_submodule_quotient` extension lemma) for the filtration steps.

`#print axioms` of `finiteDimensional_hom` shows only `propext, Classical.choice,
Quot.sound` — no `sorryAx`.
