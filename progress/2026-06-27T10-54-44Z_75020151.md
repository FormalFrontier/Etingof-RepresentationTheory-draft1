One-shot dispatch on #5529 (`exists_common_schurModule_model_detTwist_dual`, the sole
analytic sorry behind the GL_n contragredient identity, `Chapter5/ContragredientIdentity.lean`).
After mapping the full infrastructure (exact signatures of `iso_of_formalCharacter_eq_schurPoly`,
the Prop 5.22.2 shift/det-twist lemmas, the `IsAlgebraicRepresentation` toolkit, the

sorry-free landing, and decomposed it.

Key findings:
- The two analytic facts the proof rests on — (a) `formalCharacter (Representation.dual ρ)`
  at inverse weights, and (b) the inverted-variable Schur identity
  `s_λ(x⁻¹)·det^s = s_{w₀λ+s}` — have **no support in the repo or Mathlib**. There is also no
  `charTwistRep` composition lemma yet.
- `formalCharacter` is *truncated* to ℕ-weight spaces, so fact (a) cannot be a naive Laurent
  substitution; stating it wrong would be a landmine (per the skill's counterexample-first rule).
- `SchurModule k N lam` is definitionally `FDRep.of (schurModuleRep k N lam)` (Theorem5_22_1.lean:321),
  so the keystone's FDRep iso connects cleanly to the goal's `schurModuleRep`/`SchurModuleSubmodule`
  carrier — the FDRep→≃ₗ extraction is plumbing, not the obstruction.
- The two halves share `s` and `ν`, so they are coupled (pick `s ≥ (lam.w0Twist).shift`, then `ν`
  is forced); this coupling is why a clean code-level split with sorried sub-statements is awkward.
