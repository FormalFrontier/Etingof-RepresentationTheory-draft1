## Decisions

* **Matrix entries are endomorphisms, not scalars.** `Proposition3_1_4.lean` is stated over
  an arbitrary ring `A` with arbitrary simple `V i`, so Schur's lemma gives entries in the
  division ring `Module.End A (V i)`, not `k`. Over the book's algebraically closed base
  these coincide. Keeping the general setting avoids weakening the existing theorem.
* **Independence is over the opposite ring.** The book's convention
  `φ(v₁, …, v_r) = (v₁, …, v_r) Xᵢ` puts scalars on the right, so the relevant relation among
  the rows is `∑ a, X a l * c a = 0`. Over a noncommutative `Module.End A (V i)` there is no
  left/right symmetry to appeal to, so `LinearIndependent Dᵐᵒᵖ` is the honest statement, and
  it matches the convention `Remark3_1_5.lean` had already chosen.
* **Block-diagonality is proved from Schur, not read off the construction of `e`.** For any
  `A`-linear map into `⊕ⱼ nⱼVⱼ` out of the `i`-th block, the `j`-th coordinate is a map
  `V i →ₗ V j`, hence zero for `j ≠ i`. This is `hom_eq_zero_of_ne` and needs nothing about
  how `e` was built, so it also holds for any other choice of `e`.
* **The proof duplicates a little of `Remark3_1_5.lean`'s `homMatrixEquiv` machinery**
  (~15 lines). `Remark3_1_5.lean` imports `Proposition3_1_4.lean`, so reuse in the other
  direction is impossible; the assembled `subrepresentation_blocks` corollary lives in
  `Remark3_1_5.lean` where the reuse is available.
