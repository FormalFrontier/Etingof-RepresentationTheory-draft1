  propagation along the connected graph forces `z = 0`, i.e. `y ∝ w`; `w v > 0` and
  `y v = 0` force the constant to be `0`.

  lemma; nonzero `x` with vanishing form would give `xhat` a nonzero off-`v` kernel
  vector, contradicting `affine_kernel_off_vertex_eq_zero`.

  `IsDynkinDiagram` (symmetry/zero-diag/0-1/connectivity inherited; posDef from above).
