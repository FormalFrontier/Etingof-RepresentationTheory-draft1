## Accomplished

Fixed and proved `one_dim_reps_card` in `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Problem4_12_6.lean`
(issue #6295). The previous statement `Nat.card (Affine K →* ℂˣ) = Fintype.card K - 1` was
**false for `K = 𝔽₂`** (there `Kˣ` is trivial, `G ≅ ℤ/2` is abelian of order 2, giving 2 characters
but `q - 1 = 1`).

- Chose issue's Option 1: added the hypothesis `hK : 3 ≤ Fintype.card K` (equivalently
  `Nontrivial Kˣ`) and kept the `= Fintype.card K - 1` conclusion, matching Etingof's "`q - 1`
  one-dimensional representations".
- Proof: built the projection `projA : Affine K →* Kˣ` (`⟨a,b⟩ ↦ a`) and its section
  `s : Kˣ →* Affine K` (`a ↦ ⟨a,0⟩`). Every character `φ` kills translations `⟨1,c⟩` because each is
  a single commutator `⟨1,(a-1)c⟩ = [⟨a,0⟩,⟨1,c⟩]` (needs a unit `a ≠ 1`, hence `3 ≤ q`), and `ℂˣ` is
  abelian. This makes precomposition with `projA` a bijection `(Kˣ →* ℂˣ) ≃ (Affine K →* ℂˣ)`. Then
  `Nat.card (Kˣ →* ℂˣ) = Nat.card Kˣ = q - 1` via
  `CommGroup.card_monoidHom_of_hasEnoughRootsOfUnity` (duality for finite abelian groups; `ℂ` has
  enough roots of unity since it is algebraically closed) and `Fintype.card_units`.
- Updated the module docstring bullet to state the `3 ≤ q` hypothesis and note the `q = 2` failure.
- `#print axioms one_dim_reps_card` → `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (axiom-clean).
