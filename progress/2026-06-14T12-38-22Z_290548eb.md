## Current frontier

#4598's actual deliverable (`polynomial_homog_rep_equivariant_embedding`, feeding
`polynomialRep_embeds_in_tensorPower'`) reduces to producing **bare-entry,
homogeneous-degree-`n`** matrix-coefficient polynomials (`hpoly'`) from
`IsAlgebraicRepresentation` (which only gives `GLCoordVars`-polynomials *with a
det⁻¹ variable*). That "det⁻¹ elimination" is the genuine work, and it factors
into three pieces, NOT one:

1. **General weight-space spanning for algebraic reps:**
   `⨆ μ, glWeightSpace k N M μ = ⊤`. Only proven for `SchurModule`
   (`Proposition5_22_2.lean:304`), used as a *hypothesis* `h_top` everywhere else
   (`FormalCharacterIso.lean:264`). For an arbitrary algebraic rep this is the
   simultaneous diagonalizability of the commuting torus operators — a real
   theorem (rational one-parameter subgroups are diagonalizable). Substantial.
2. **Homogeneity operator:** `M.ρ (scalarUnit t) = (t^n) • LinearMap.id`. **Now
   easy** given (1) + the landed `rho_scalarUnit_apply_of_mem_glWeightSpace` +
   `h_homog`. This is the small remaining glue.
3. **det⁻¹ elimination / polynomiality:** given homogeneity + `IsAlgebraic`,
   show each matrix coefficient is a genuine polynomial of degree `n` in the bare
   entries (no det⁻¹). Scaling at `t • 1` forces `entry-deg − N·(detVar-deg) = n`
   per monomial, but that alone is **insufficient** (det⁻¹·(homog deg n+N) is also
   "degree n"). The missing ingredient is **non-negativity of weights**
   (`μ : Fin N → ℕ`), i.e. the rep extends to the matrix monoid `M_N(k)` /
   `det^F | numerator`. This is polynomial-functor theory — the hard crux the
   issue's "bounded det⁻¹-degree" hint understates.
