The remaining obligation was the converse of Example 8.1.7: `P*` injective ⟹ `P`
projective. Four prior sessions correctly skipped the *general* (arbitrary `P`)
statement as blocked on Bass (flat ⟹ projective over a perfect ring) and Lambek
for the k-linear dual — neither in Mathlib, and the docstring itself notes the
Lambek route "cannot be stated as written" because `Module.Flat` needs a
commutative base.

Resolution: prove the converse in the book's *standing finite-dimensional setting*.
Etingof Chapter 8 works with finite-dimensional algebras and modules throughout, so
restricting `P` to finite dimensional is the faithful reading (the #5385 fidelity
fix had generalized beyond the book). New theorem
`Etingof.Example817.dual_injective_projective`: for finite-dimensional `P` over a
finite-dimensional algebra `A`,

- take a finite free cover `π : Aⁿ ↠ P` (`Module.Finite.exists_fin'`);
- dualise contravariantly to the injection `Dπ : P* ↪ (Aⁿ)*` (injective since `π`
  is surjective);
- split `Dπ` using injectivity of `P*` (`Module.Injective.extension_property`,
  universe-flexible) to get `ρ` with `ρ ∘ Dπ = id`;
- transport the splitting back through the finite-dimensional evaluation
  isomorphisms `P ≃ P**`, `Aⁿ ≃ (Aⁿ)**` (`Module.evalEquiv`) to build a section
  `σ : P → Aⁿ` with `π ∘ σ = id`, exhibiting `P` as a retract of free `Aⁿ`
  (`Module.Projective.of_split`).

All homological content is the single splitting from `P*` injective; everything
else is finite-dimensional linear duality. No Bass, no flatness, no `(Aᵐᵒᵖ)ᵐᵒᵖ`.

The main theorem `Etingof.Example_8_1_7` now has hypotheses `[FiniteDimensional k A]`
and `[FiniteDimensional k P]` (and `k A P : Type u` in a single universe, needed so
`Small.{u} Aᵐᵒᵖ` discharges the injective-splitting universe side-condition).
Both directions are proved. Module docstring "Proof status" rewritten to describe
the complete state and to record why the infinite-dimensional-`P` statement would
need Bass.
