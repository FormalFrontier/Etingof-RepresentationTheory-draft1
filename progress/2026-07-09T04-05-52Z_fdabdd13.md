## Accomplished

Discharged **Problem 2.7.5(b)** (issue #5999), the determinant obstruction for
finite-dimensional representations of the `q`-Weyl algebra, in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter2/Problem2_7_5.lean`:

- `Etingof.Problem2_7_5.q_pow_finrank_eq_one` — on any finite-dimensional module `V`,
  `(q : ℂ) ^ finrank ℂ V = 1`. Proof: represent `A` on `V` via
  `Algebra.lsmul ℂ ℂ V : (qWeylAlgebra ℂ q) →ₐ[ℂ] End ℂ V`. The generators
  `x = qMono (1,0)`, `y = qMono (0,1)` are units in `A` (`qMono_x_mul_inv`,
  `qMono_y_mul_inv`), so `det (ρ x)`, `det (ρ y)` are nonzero. The relation
  `yx = q·xy` (`qMono_relation_yx`) pushes through the algebra hom to
  `ρy·ρx = q•(ρx·ρy)`; taking `LinearMap.det` (a `MonoidHom`, so `map_mul` applies)
  and using `LinearMap.det_smul` gives `det(ρy)det(ρx) = q^{finrank}·det(ρx)det(ρy)`,
  and cancelling the nonzero product yields `q^{finrank} = 1`.
- `Etingof.Problem2_7_5.isOfFinOrder_of_nontrivial_finrep` — a `Nontrivial`
  finite-dimensional `V` has `finrank ℂ V > 0` (`Module.finrank_pos`); lifting the
  power identity to `ℂˣ` via `Units.ext`/`push_cast` and applying
  `isOfFinOrder_iff_pow_eq_one` gives `IsOfFinOrder q`.

Both `#print axioms` show only `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound` (no `sorryAx`).
