## Accomplished

One-shot session on #5207 (Krull–Schmidt 3/5: Fitting's lemma and local endomorphism rings).
Created `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter9/KrullSchmidt/Fitting.lean` with all three deliverables
stated, and the two reductions proved **unconditionally** from the Fitting decomposition:

- `Etingof.fitting_decomposition` (deliverable 1) — stated in block-conjugation form
  `f = e.hom ≫ biprod.map fK fI ≫ e.inv` with `IsNilpotent fK`, `IsIso fI`. **Body is a single
  documented `sorry`**: its proof needs stabilisation of the `im (f^n)`/`ker (f^n)` chains, i.e.
  finiteness of `clength` in a finite abelian category — the still-`sorry`-d categorical
  Jordan–Hölder content of `KrullSchmidt/Length.lean` (#5205, closed with documented sorries).
- `Etingof.isNilpotent_or_isIso_of_indecomposable` (deliverable 2) — **fully proved** from (1):
  indecomposability forces `IsZero K` or `IsZero I`; zero `K` ⇒ both blocks iso ⇒ `f` iso, zero
  `I` ⇒ both blocks nilpotent ⇒ `f` nilpotent. Transport uses `Iso.conj` (a `MulEquiv` sending the
  zero morphism to `0`).
- `Etingof.isLocalRing_End_of_indecomposable` (deliverable 3) — **fully proved** from (2) via
  `IsLocalRing.of_isUnit_or_isUnit_one_sub_self`: iso ⇒ `IsUnit a`, nilpotent ⇒ `IsUnit (1 - a)`
  (`IsNilpotent.isUnit_one_sub`). `Nontrivial (End X)` from `𝟙 X ≠ 0`.

Helper lemmas proved sorry-free: `biprodMap_comp`, `biprodMap_pow` (block maps respect powers),
`biprodMap_isNilpotent`, `isNilpotent_conj`.

`lake build EtingofRepresentationTheory.Chapter9.KrullSchmidt.Fitting` succeeds. `#print axioms` on
both downstream theorems shows `sorryAx` only via the single `fitting_decomposition` sorry.
