Closed the fidelity gap on `Chapter4/Theorem4.2.1` (issue #5380). The book
claims irreducible characters form a **basis** of `Fc(G, k)` (spanning AND
linearly independent), but the Lean development only asserted the spanning
half (`Etingof.Theorem4_2_1`).

Added `Etingof.Theorem4_2_1_linearIndependent` in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Theorem4_2_1.lean`: the characters of
simple representations are k-linearly independent. The proof applies the
orthogonality functional `f ↦ ∑ x, f x * χ_{V}(x⁻¹)` to a vanishing linear
combination and uses `FDRep.char_orthonormal` (Theorem 4.5.1) to collapse it
to the diagonal term `g i₀ * |G| = 0`, then `|G| ≠ 0` (Invertible) forces the
coefficient to zero. Only the easy direction of `FDRep.char_iso`
(iso ⇒ equal character) is needed for the off-diagonal vanishing, so this works
for general algebraically closed `k`, not just `ℂ`.

Spanning (`Etingof.Theorem4_2_1`) + independence
(`Etingof.Theorem4_2_1_linearIndependent`) is exactly the book's basis claim.
Updated the module docstring and set `fidelity: verified` in
`progress/items.json`.
