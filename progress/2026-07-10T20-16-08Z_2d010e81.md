Added four private helpers (all sorry-free) implementing the strategy from the prior
handoff (`progress/2026-07-10T19-53-29Z_9896f456.md`):

- **`eigen_smul_eq`** — if every `Xᵢ` acts on `m₀` by scalar `cᵢ`, then `p • m₀ = aeval c p • m₀`
  (MvPolynomial `induction_on`, mirrors `Vrep_smul_eq`).
- **`oneDim_iso_Vrep`** — a 1-dim `A`-module with a scalar-eigenvector `m₀` is `≃ₗ[A] Vrep c`,
  via `φ = toSpanSingleton A M m₀`, `ker φ = 𝔪_c`, surjectivity from `finrank = 1`, then
  `quotKerEquivOfSurjective` ∘ `quotEquivOfEq`.
- **`oneDim_common_eigen`** — on a 1-dim module every generator acts by a scalar.
- **`exists_common_eigenvector`** — the crux: over `ℂ` a 2-dim rep has a simultaneous
  eigenvector. `by_cases` on whether every `Xᵢ` is scalar; otherwise take a non-scalar `X_j`,
  an eigenvalue `μ` (`Module.End.exists_eigenvalue`, needs `IsAlgClosed ℂ`), whose eigenspace
  `E` is proper hence 1-dim (`finrank_lt_finrank_of_lt` both sides + `omega`); the commuting
  generators preserve `E`, so any nonzero `v ∈ E` is a common eigenvector.

Main proof: `S := span_{polyAlg} {v}`; `S.restrictScalars ℂ = span_ℂ {v}` gives `finrank ℂ S = 1`
and `S ≅ Vrep b`; `Submodule.Quotient.restrictScalarsEquiv` + `finrank_quotient_add_finrank` give
`finrank ℂ (U ⧸ S) = 1`, and `oneDim_common_eigen` + `oneDim_iso_Vrep` give `U ⧸ S ≅ Vrep a`.
