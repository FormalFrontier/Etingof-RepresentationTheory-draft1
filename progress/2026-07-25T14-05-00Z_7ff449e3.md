## Design note (deviation from the issue text)

The issue asked to build the isomorphism out of Mathlib's `OnCycleFactors.toPermHom` /
`Basis.toCentralizer` / kernel-and-range parametrization. I did not use that route. Assembling
it requires (a) a split-extension lemma, (b) identifying the kernel as
`Perm (fixedPoints g) × ∏_c zpowers c` and the range as `∏_m Perm (cycles of length m)`,
(c) transporting the conjugation action of the range on the kernel through both identifications
into a coordinate permutation, and (d) regrouping `(A × ∏ N_m) ⋊ ∏ G_m` into
`A × ∏ (N_m ⋊ G_m)` — with the fixed-point factor sitting in the *kernel* while all other
`S_{i_m}` sit in the range, so the regrouping is not uniform in `m`.

Conjugating `g` into the standard shift instead makes the whole thing one construction plus two
structure lemmas, and gives the isomorphism in the wreath → centralizer direction, which is the
useful one. Mathlib's `Equiv.Perm.nat_card_centralizer` is still used, as the issue required, for
the cardinality reconciliation. Nothing in Mathlib was rebuilt.
