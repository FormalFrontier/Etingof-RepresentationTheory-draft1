## Accomplished

Feature session on #7298 (isomorphism-level tensor decompositions for Problem 4.12.6).
Landed the first headline isomorphism `χ ⊗ V ≅ V` plus a reusable character-theory lemma, in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Problem4_12_6.lean` (sorry-free, builds clean):

- `equiv_of_character_eq`: two **irreducible** complex representations of the affine group with
  equal characters are isomorphic (`Nonempty (ρ.Equiv σ)`). Proof via
  `Representation.char_orthonormal`: `⟨χ_ρ, χ_σ⟩ = ⟨χ_ρ, χ_ρ⟩ = 1` forces `ρ ≅ σ`.
- `smulChar χ ρ` (character twist `g ↦ (χ g)·ρ g`) + `smulChar_apply`, `charRep_apply`.
- `subrepEquivSmulChar` (order-iso of subrepresentation lattices of `ρ` and `smulChar χ ρ`) and
  `smulChar_isIrreducible`.
- `tprodCharRepEquivSmulChar`: `charRep χ ⊗ ρ ≅ smulChar χ ρ` via `TensorProduct.lid`.
- `IsIrreducible.of_equiv`: irreducibility transfers across `Representation.Equiv`
  (built from `equivLinearMapAsModule` + `IsSimpleModule.congr`).
- `charRep_tprod_Vrep_character_eq_Vrep`: `χ_{χ⊗V}(g) = χ(g)·χ_V(g) = χ_V(g)` pointwise
  (either `χ_V(g)=0` when `g.a≠1`, or `χ(g)=1` when `g` is a translation, needing `3 ≤ q`).
- `charRep_tprod_Vrep_equiv_Vrep`: **`χ ⊗ V ≅ V`** — assembled from the above.

Worked at the `Representation.Equiv` level (not `FDRep`) deliberately: `FDRep ℂ G` fixes the
carrier universe to `Type 0`, but our `zeroSum K` lives in `K`'s universe;
`Representation.Equiv` and `Representation.char_orthonormal` are universe-flexible.
