  `A`-linear endomorphism (`A`-linearity is exactly centrality).

  `z ↦ (v ↦ z • v)` as a `k`-algebra homomorphism.
- `endScalarEquiv : k ≃ₐ[k] Module.End A V` — Schur's lemma (Corollary 2.3.10) as a
  `k`-algebra isomorphism, from `IsSimpleModule.algebraMap_end_bijective_of_isAlgClosed`.
- `centralCharacter : Subalgebra.center k A →ₐ[k] k` — **the central character** `χ_V`,
  defined as `endScalarEquiv.symm ∘ centralActionHom`. Being a composite of algebra
  homomorphisms, it *is* a homomorphism (the second half of part (a)).
- `centralCharacter_smul`, `exists_central_scalar` — `z` acts on `V` by the scalar `χ_V(z)`.
