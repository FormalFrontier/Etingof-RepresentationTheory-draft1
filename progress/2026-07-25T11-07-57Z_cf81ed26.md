nonzero, the target component is everything). Step `m` lands in the unique vertex block
containing `m`, and the functional is the corresponding basis coordinate.

## Current frontier

Issue #7359 is complete; every acceptance criterion in its body is met.

Design note for successors: the one deliberate deviation from the issue's wording is that the
successive subquotient is expressed as "surjection onto `S i` with kernel `W`" rather than as an
explicitly constructed quotient object. `QuiverRepresentation` bundles `AddCommMonoid` carriers,
and `Submodule.Quotient` needs `AddCommGroup`, so building the quotient means threading
`Module.addCommMonoidToAddCommGroup` through every carrier and fighting instance diamonds. The
chosen form is equivalent and, more importantly, provably not vacuous
(`IsSimpleStep.eq_or_eq`, `IsSimpleStep.ne`). Anyone who later wants literal quotient objects
should first change Definition 2.8.3 to bundle `AddCommGroup`, not work around it locally.

`Infrastructure/QuiverCompositionSeries.lean` is general-purpose: any later Jordan–Hölder or
finite-length statement about quiver representations (Chapter 6 Gabriel-theorem work, Chapter 3
`Problem3_9_3`) should build on it rather than re-derive filtrations.
