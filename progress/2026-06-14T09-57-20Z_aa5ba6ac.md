### Difficulty analysis (key handoff content)

The 32 orientations split into two genuinely different regimes:

- **Non-mixed** (each center has *both* or *neither* leaf reversed;
  central edge either direction): mechanical. The matching pull
  projections / push embeds line up, so containments follow from the
  `*_proj1/2` siblings (reversed leaves) or `core_F`/`core3_F`
  (canonical leaves), plus `gamma_from_embed*_F` (forward central) or
  `gammaInv_embed*_F` (reversed central). Combo D and the all-canonical
  case are the two done so far; ~6 more non-mixed orientations remain,
  scattered across the branch `sorry`s.

- **Mixed-at-a-center** (exactly one leaf reversed at some center —
  combos B/C and many sub-cases of the e02/e12/e23 branches): HARD.
  A canonical push-leaf adjacent to a reversed pull-leaf yields only an
  `(I − N)`-twisted relation. Concretely in combo B (4 canonical, 5
  reversed): `x ∈ W(0) ⟹ x ∈ W(5)` holds (matching leaf-0↔5 pull),
  but `y ∈ W(1) ⟹ N y ∈ W(5)` (twisted) and `w ∈ W(4) ⟹ w − M w ∈
  W(0), M w ∈ W(1)` via `γ⁻¹` (`M = cumTailSumLin = (I − N)⁻¹`). The
  literal leaf equality requires untwisting, which appears to need a
  new **N-invariance structural lemma** (the invariant leaf subspaces
  are stable under the nilpotent shift `N`). This is research-level and
  should be built once, then reused across all mixed sub-cases.
