### Facts established (validated, not hand-checked)

1. **The `ih` parameter is redundant.** Every peeled column-standard polytabloid
   `ψ_{β'}` lies in `V` *unconditionally* by the already-proven, sorry-free
   `generalizedPolytabloidTab_mem_span_polytabloidTab` (`SpechtModuleBasis.lean:2846`).
   So the outer/inner `(srRank, rowInvCount')` IH threaded through #4593/#4605 is not
   needed to discharge the peeled terms — the base lemma covers them regardless of
   rank. (The existing `r2b-peeling-validation.py` "FAIL"s only because it demanded
   IH-dischargeability; with the base lemma those cases are fine.)

2. **No S_n-stability shortcut.** `f_w(σ) = w·ψ_σ` as a *left* translation, but
   tabloids correspond to **right** cosets of `RowSubgroup` (`toTabloid_eq_iff`:
   `σ₁σ₂⁻¹ ∈ Row`). Left translation `[τ] ↦ [wτ]` is **ill-defined** on tabloids
   (`w·Row·w⁻¹ ≠ Row`), so the tempting "`V` is S_n-stable ⟹ `f_w(σ) ∈ V`" route does
   not exist at the tabloid level. The Garnir/peeling argument is genuinely required.

3. **Termination measure for the peel.** `card(support)` provably does NOT decrease
   (subtracting `c·ψ_{β'}` can introduce new lower-dominance support). The correct
   well-founded measure is `M(Δ) = Σ_{t ∈ supp Δ} B^{srRank t}` with base `B = |U|+1`
   (`U` = tabloids `≼ σ`), peeling a dominance-**maximal** element: removing the top
   `B^{srRank β}` term strictly exceeds the total `< B^{srRank β}` mass introduced
   strictly below it. Works with *maximal* (not global-maximum) elements.

4. **THE GAP — col-std-izability of maximals is not what sub-A provides.** The
   leading-term-elimination recursion needs: *every dominance-maximal tabloid of every
   intermediate residual is column-standardizable* (has a col-std rep with the SAME
   tabloid). New probe `r2b-peel-stuck-probe.py`:
   - Garnir residuals **and their intermediate peels**: **0 / 1668** stuck, 0 nonterm,
     1144 multi-step peels (up to 14 steps), over shapes (2,2),(3,2),(2,2,1),(3,3),(3,2,1).
     So the property holds for the recursion. ✓
   - **Arbitrary** support-bounded-by-σ vectors: **2400 / 2400** stuck.
   Therefore col-std-izability of maximals is **special to Garnir-derived residuals via
   an invariant preserved under peeling** — it is NOT "support-bounded-by-σ", and it is
   NOT captured by sub-A #4630, which only asserts col-std-izability of the **global
   max of the original Δ**. Intermediate residuals are not Garnir residuals, yet the
   recursion needs the property for them. The #4593 split (sub-A = "max col-std-izable"
   + sub-B = "assembly given sub-A") thus has a hole: sub-B's recursion needs strictly
   more than sub-A delivers, and the missing invariant is currently uncharacterized.

### Why no assembly was written

Writing #4605 "given sub-A" would require sorry-ing a lemma strictly stronger than
sub-A (#4630) — col-std-izability of maximals of *all* intermediate residuals under an
unknown preserved invariant. That is not "use sub-A as a sorry'd dependency"; it is
inventing a new, harder, uncharacterized hypothesis, which would make the assembly's
correctness illusory. Per the worker-flow escalation ladder ("ordering/decomposition
mistake in the plan → request a replan"), this is routed to replan.
