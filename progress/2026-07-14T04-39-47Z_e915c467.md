## Accomplished

Proved `highest_weight_eigenvalue_of_not_isOfFinOrder` (issue #6552) sorry-free in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter2/Problem2_16_5.lean` — the non-root-of-unity classification:
on an `(n+1)`-dimensional irreducible `U_q(sl₂)`-module, `K` acts on a highest weight vector by
`ε·qⁿ` with `ε² = 1`.

New reusable infrastructure landed in the file:
- Scalar facts: `qpow_ne_one`, `q_sq_ne_one`, `q_sub_inv_ne` (from `¬ IsOfFinOrder q`).
- The `f`-ladder `ladder q V v i = fⁱ • v` with `ladder_succ`, `ladder_zero`, and its
  `K`-eigenvalue `mu q lam i = lam·q^{-2i}` (`K_ladder`), `L`-eigenvalue (`L_ladder`),
  `mu_ne_zero`, `mu_zero`.
- `ef_action`: the commutator action `e•(f•x) = f•(e•x) + (q-q⁻¹)⁻¹(a-a⁻¹)•x` on a `K`-eigenvector.
- `dcoef` (telescoping quantum-bracket sum) and `e_ladder`: `e•fⁱ⁺¹v = dcoef q lam i · fⁱv`.
- `mu_inj`: injectivity of the eigenvalue chain when `q` is not a root of unity.
- `dcoef_eq_zero_imp`: vanishing of the top coefficient forces `lam² = q^{2N}` (geometric-sum
  reflection identity `SA·q^{2N} = SB`, both sums nonzero).
- `smul_mem_of_generators`: a `ℂ`-submodule closed under `e,f,K,L` is a `U_q(sl₂)`-submodule
  (via `FreeAlgebra.induction` on a `mk`-preimage).

The main theorem assembles: highest weight vector (from parent #6551), `lam ≠ 0`, termination
(`Nat.find` on the first vanishing ladder vector, using would-be infinite independence), the
`ℂ`-span of the finite ladder is a `U_q`-submodule hence `⊤` by `IsSimpleModule`, giving
`finrank ℂ V = N+1`, then `dcoef N = 0 ⇒ lam² = q^{2N}`, and `ε = lam·(qᴺ)⁻¹`.
