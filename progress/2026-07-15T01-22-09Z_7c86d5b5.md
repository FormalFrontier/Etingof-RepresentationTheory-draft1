## Accomplished
Proved `Etingof.Problem3_9_5.even_isMatrixAlgebra` sorry-free (issue #6607): the even nondegenerate
Clifford algebra `Cl(V)` (`dim V = 2n`) is a matrix algebra `End ℂ S` with `dim S = 2ⁿ`.

Rather than the book's explicit spinor construction, the proof goes through the **center**:
- `gen_comm_notMem` / `gen_conj`: for an orthogonal basis, `gen i * e S = (-1)^{|S \ {i}|} • (e S * gen i)`
  (reuses the existing monomial machinery `gen_mul_mem`, `e_minPeel`, `gen_anticomm`, `isUnit_e`).
- `central_of_commutes_gen`: commuting with all generators ⇒ central (via `CliffordAlgebra.induction`).
- `center_le_bot`: expanding a central `x = ∑ c_S e_S` in `cliffBasis`, commutation with `gen i`
  forces `c_S ((-1)^{|S\{i}|} - 1) = 0`; for `S ≠ ∅` some `i` makes `|S \ {i}|` odd (uses `N` even),
  so `c_S = 0`. Hence `center ℂ (CliffAlg B) = ⊥` (1-dimensional).
- Assembly: `IsSemisimpleRing.exists_algEquiv_pi_matrix_of_isAlgClosed` gives `Cl(V) ≃ₐ ∏ᵢ M_{dᵢ}(ℂ)`;
  the coordinate units `Pi.single i 1` are central and independent, so `#factors ≤ finrank center = 1`;
  the single factor collapses via `RingEquiv.piUnique` to `M_k(ℂ) ≃ₐ End ℂ (Fin k → ℂ)`
  (`LinearMap.toMatrixAlgEquiv'`); dimension count `k² = 2^{2n}` gives `k = 2ⁿ`.

`#print axioms even_isMatrixAlgebra` = `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (no `sorryAx`).
Full project builds clean. Added imports: `RingTheory.SimpleModule.IsAlgClosed`,
