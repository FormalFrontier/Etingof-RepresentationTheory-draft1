- `homologyTensorHomologyZeroIso` (zero-differential tensor homology, from #6345 / PR #6349) —
  identifies `Hⁱ(H_C ⊗ H_D)` with `∐_{j+m=i} Hʲ(C) ⊗ Hᵐ(D)`.

Key steps: the homology functor `Hi = homologyFunctor _ _ i` is additive, so
`preservesBinaryBiproducts_of_preservesBiproducts` gives `PreservesBinaryBiproducts Hi` and
`Hi.mapBiprod` distributes `Hⁱ` over `⊞`. Three of the four summands are acyclic (part (ii),
`Problem7_8_7_ii`), so their `Hⁱ` is a zero object
(`exactAt_iff_isZero_homology`); `biprod_isZero_iff` + `isoZeroBiprod.symm` collapse them,
leaving `Hⁱ(H_C ⊗ H_D)`.
