Route (mirrors the Heisenberg classification in `Problem4_12_2`):

- Added reusable infrastructure to the file:
  - `charRep` / `charRep_character` / `charRep_asModule_simple` / `charRep_simple`: the
    1-dimensional representation attached to a character `χ : G →* ℂˣ`, and its simplicity
    (via the universe-general bridge `Etingof.simple_fdRepOf_of_isSimpleModule` — the
    character norm-one criterion `FDRep.simple_iff_char_is_norm_one` could **not** be used
    because it forces `k` and `G` into the same universe, and `Affine K : Type u₁`).
  - `permRep`: the permutation representation of `G` on `K → ℂ`.
  - `Vsub` / `Vrep_isSimpleModule`: the zero-sum subrepresentation `V` (dim `q-1`), proved
    simple as a `ℂ[G]`-module by repackaging the already-proved `zeroSum_irreducible`
    through the `Subrepresentation ↔ Submodule ℂ[G] asModule` order isomorphism.
  - `exists_simpleFDRep`: universe transport — any finite-dim rep with simple `asModule`
    gives a simple `FDRep ℂ G` object of equal dimension on a `Type 0` carrier
    (`transportModule` / `repOfModule`), so the Wedderburn enumeration applies to an
    arbitrary-universe `W`.
- Main proof: exhibit the `q` explicit simples (the `q-1` characters + `V`), pairwise
  non-isomorphic (characters by `FDRep.char_iso`; `V` by dimension since `q-1 > 1` for
  `q ≥ 3`), squared dims summing to `(q-1) + (q-1)² = q(q-1) = |G|`; inject into the
  complete family `exists_simples_sum_finrank_sq_eq_card` and force surjectivity by
  pigeonhole (every `dim² > 0`), so every simple — in particular `σ` — is one of them.
- `q = 2` edge case (`G` abelian): handled directly via `Example4_3_FiniteAbelianGroups`
  after building a local `CommGroup (Affine K)` instance (all units are `1`).
