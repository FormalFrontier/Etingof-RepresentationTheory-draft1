## Accomplished

One-shot feature session `79edd803` (`/once 5251`): worked the single remaining
`sorry` in Example 5.1.3 — `Etingof.isRealType_of_A5_even_standard` (the
even-dimensional / 4-dim standard A₅ irrep, real type).

Investigation established that filling it honestly is blocked on **three**
foundational results, all confirmed missing from Mathlib by a thorough source
search:
1. sum of squared dimensions of complex irreps `= |G|` (Artin-Wedderburn count),
2. the Frobenius-Schur involution count `#{g : g²=1} = ∑ FS(Vᵢ)·dim Vᵢ`,
3. `#irreps = #conjugacy classes` (`= 5` for A₅).

The original plan routed through Frobenius **divisibility** (`dim ∣ |G|`, needing
character integrality / algebraic integers). This session found a cleaner route
that **avoids divisibility entirely** and never has to exhibit the `{1,3,3,4,5}`
dimension list: a Cauchy-Schwarz counting argument.

**Landed (sorry-free, axioms clean):** `Etingof.A5_frobeniusSchur_all_pos` in
`Chapter5/Example5_1_3.lean` — the verified arithmetic endgame. Given the five A₅
irreps' positive integer dims `d` and ±1 indicators `ε`, from `∑ d² = 60 = |A₅|`
and `∑ ε·d = 16 = #{g : g²=1}` plus `(∑ d)² ≤ 5·∑ d² = 300`
(`sq_sum_le_card_mul_sum_sq`), every `ε i = 1`. Insight: a single `+1 → −1` flip on
a dim `≥ 1` raises `∑ d` by `≥ 2` to `≥ 18`, whose square `324 > 300`.
