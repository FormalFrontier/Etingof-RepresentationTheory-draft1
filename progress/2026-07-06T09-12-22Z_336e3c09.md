  bilinear form, modelled as `CliffordAlgebra B.toQuadraticMap`. Statements: nondegenerate

  `Ring.jacobson`); the `B = 0 ⟹ Cl = ⋀V` parenthetical.
- **Problem 3.9.4** (`Problem3_9_4.lean`): formal deformations. A genuine
  `FormalDeformation` structure (coefficient maps `ρₙ`, base `ρ₀ = ρ`, Cauchy-product
  multiplicativity), the constant deformation, and the `IsIsomorphic` relation via a power
  series `b(t)`. (a) `Ext¹(V,V) = 0 ⟹` trivial (reusing `Problem3_9_1.Ext1`); (b) the
  converse question posed as a `Prop`-def against the dual numbers `DualNumber k`.
- **Problem 3.9.2** (`Problem3_9_2.lean`): `Ext¹` for `ℂ[x₁..xₙ]`. 1-dim reps `Vₐ` as
  `A ⧸ 𝔪ₐ` (genuine A- and ℂ-module with scalar tower). `Ext¹(Vₐ,Vₐ) ≅ ℂⁿ`,
  `Ext¹(Vₐ,V_b)` subsingleton for `a ≠ b`, every 2-dim rep is an extension of two 1-dim
  reps. Part (b): `B = ℂ⟨x⟩/(xᵢxⱼ)` built via `RingQuot`, infinitely many nonisomorphic
  indecomposables for `n > 1`.
- **Problem 3.8.5** (`Problem3_8_5.lean`): `A` = period-1 continuous functions (subalgebra
  of `C(ℝ,ℝ)`), `M` = antiperiodic ones (an `A`-submodule). (i) both indecomposable;
  (ii) `A ≇ M` but `A ⊕ A ≅ M ⊕ M` (Möbius line bundle on the circle).
- **Problem 3.9.3** (`Problem3_9_3.lean`): path-algebra representations, modelled as
  `Etingof.QuiverRepresentation` (reps of `P_Q` = quiver reps of `Q`). Reuses
  `Problem6_9_3`'s `simpleRep`, `Ext1Vanishes`, `dimVec`. `NoOrientedCycles`, `IsIrreducible`,
  simples `S_i` are the irreducibles, `Ext¹(S_i,S_j) = 0 ↔ IsEmpty (i ⟶ j)`, 2-dim
  classification dichotomy.
