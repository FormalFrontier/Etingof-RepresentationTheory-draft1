## Current frontier

`QWeylAlgebraUniversal.lean` has the reordering law but not yet the algebra hom. Remaining
construction (fully designed, ready to implement):

- `gMono q p : qWeylAlgebra k q := ⟨qMono k q p, qMono_mem k q p⟩`, with `coe_gMono` (rfl),
  `gMono_one` (`= 1` via `qMono_zero`), and `gMono_mul'` (`gMono p * gMono r = q^(p.2 r.1) • gMono (p+r)`
  via `qMono_mul`).
- `qWeylBasis : Basis (ℤ×ℤ) k (qWeylAlgebra k q) := Basis.mk hli hsp`, where
  `hli := (qMono_linearIndependent k q).of_comp (qWeylAlgebra k q).subtype` (since
  `subtype ∘ gMono = qMono` by rfl) and `hsp : ⊤ ≤ span k (range gMono)` from
  `qWeyl_toSubmodule_eq_span` pushed through the injective inclusion (`Submodule.map_span` +
  `Subalgebra.range_val` + `Submodule.map_injective_of_injective`).
- `toEndHom (hrel) : qWeylAlgebra k q →ₐ[k] End k V := AlgHom.ofLinearMap (qWeylBasis.constr k (fun p => op X Y p.1 p.2)) map_one map_mul`.
  `map_one` from `1 = qWeylBasis (0,0)` + `constr_basis` + `op_zero_zero`. `map_mul` by proving it
  on basis pairs (`gMono_mul'` + `constr_basis` + `op_mul`) then lifting to all elements via
  bilinear `Basis.ext` (two nested `Basis.ext`, using `LinearMap.compr₂`/`compl₁₂` with
  `LinearMap.mul`).
- Module wrapper: `Module.compHom V toEndHom.toRingHom` gives `Module (qWeylAlgebra k q) V`, plus
  the matching `IsScalarTower ℂ (qWeylAlgebra k q) V` (both k-actions agree because `toEndHom` is a
  k-algebra hom), and `toEndHom (gMono (1,0)) = X`, `toEndHom (gMono (0,1)) = Y`.
