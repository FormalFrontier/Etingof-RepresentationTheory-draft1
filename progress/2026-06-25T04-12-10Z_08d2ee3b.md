## Current frontier

The four induced-rep isomorphisms are stated (3 of 4) but unproved. Proving them needs a
keystone lemma missing from both Mathlib and this project: **for a finite group over ℂ,
equal character ⟹ isomorphic** (`char_iso` gives only the forward direction;
`scalar_product_char_eq_finrank_equivariant` + `char_orthonormal` are the building blocks).
