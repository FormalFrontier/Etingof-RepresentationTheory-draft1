## Result

Eighteen items are proof-complete or have no formal proof obligation. Example 2.9.13's former
gap is closed: the free algebra on `x`, `y`, `c` modulo exactly the three Heisenberg relations is
proved algebra-equivalent to `U(ℋ)`. The construction supplies maps in both directions from the
two universal properties, proves both composites are identities, and matches all three
generators.

Remark 2.9.3 remains partial. Its arbitrary-field Ado–Iwasawa statement is a genuine theorem-level
obligation recorded by `proof_wanted`; it has not been reclassified or certified as proved.
This pass proves the constructive last step in two forms:

- `ado_of_finiteAssociativeEmbedding`: an injective Lie map into a finite-dimensional associative
  algebra gives a faithful finite-dimensional representation by left multiplication;
- `ado_of_finiteEnvelopingTarget`: it therefore suffices to construct a finite-dimensional target
  of `U(L)` whose composite with the canonical map `L → U(L)` is injective.

Both reduction lemmas are sorry-free and depend only on the standard axioms reported by Lean.
The remaining construction is substantial. Mathlib contains neither Ado–Iwasawa nor a general PBW
theorem for `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra`; in particular it does not currently prove injectivity of
the canonical map `L → U(L)`. In positive characteristic the finite-quotient route additionally
needs the commutator `p`-power identity, central `p`-polynomials, and the bounded-exponent PBW
spanning/basis argument. In characteristic zero, a separate structural construction such as
Iwasawa's finite splitting algebra (or equivalent Levi/nilradical machinery) is needed; the generic
filtration API alone does not supply it. These are the exact prerequisites still blocking
`Etingof.ado`.

All 19 tracker records now have `stage3_3` data. Eighteen say `complete`; the Ado item says
`partial` with `proof_integrity: proof_wanted` and names its remaining obligation.
