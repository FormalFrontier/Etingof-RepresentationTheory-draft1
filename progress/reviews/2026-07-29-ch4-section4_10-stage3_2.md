## Frobenius factorization

Wedderburn block. The proof of `Etingof.Theorem4_10_2` establishes:

- factorization of the group determinant into these block polynomials with exponent equal to
  total degree;
- irreducibility of every adjusted block polynomial;
- pairwise non-association, which is stronger than nonproportionality;
- equality between the number of factors and the number of conjugacy classes.
