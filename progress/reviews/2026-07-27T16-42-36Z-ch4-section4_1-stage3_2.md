## Definition integrity, fidelity, and nonvacuity

The representations are genuine group homomorphisms into linear automorphisms, group algebras
are actual monoid algebras, the irreducible family is finite, pairwise non-isomorphic and
exhaustive, and all algebra/representation decompositions are actual equivalences rather than
dimension-only shadows. The averaged-projection claims use the established Maschke machinery,
not an assumption of the conclusion.

Example 4.1.3 assumes simplicity of the associated group-algebra module—not simplicity merely
as a vector space—and proves every group element acts by the identity. Problem 4.1.4 first
constructs a nonzero fixed vector for every finite `p`-group action in characteristic `p`, then
uses simplicity to prove the whole representation is trivial. Thus neither result assumes
one-dimensionality or triviality in disguise.

The proposition provider proves the source conclusion through the equivalent nonzero central
sum/idempotent obstruction: it constructs Λ, proves its left/right invariance and centrality,
derives Λ² = |G| · Λ, and obtains a contradiction from the idempotent-generated ideal when
|G| vanishes. It does not define the augmentation, classify the two Hom spaces, or formalize the
source's splitting/no-left-inverse route, so those claims are recorded as intentional omissions.

The cyclic example proves the actual generator-power and operator-nilpotence route: every
`g` satisfies `g^p = 1`, `(ρ(g) - 1)^p = 0`, and Schur's lemma forces that self-intertwiner to
vanish. The binomial identity is covered by `sub_pow_char_of_commute`. The source's preliminary
one-dimensional/scalar-root formulation is not separately formalized and is recorded as an
intentional omission.
