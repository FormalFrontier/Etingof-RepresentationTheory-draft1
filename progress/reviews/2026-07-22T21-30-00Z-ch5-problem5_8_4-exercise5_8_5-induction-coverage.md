## Exercise 5.8.5 — `covered_full`, fidelity verified (fresh)

No prior fidelity review existed; applied the Stage 3.2 step 6-7 fidelity tests,
judged as a different model than formalized it.

Headline `Etingof.ind_chiRep_iso_charLeftIdeal`
(`Chapter5/Exercise5_8_5.lean:239`):
`∃ e : IndV K.subtype (chiRep χ) ≃ₗ[ℂ] ↥(charLeftIdeal χ), ∀ (g:G) x,
↑(e (Definition5_8_1 K (chiRep χ) g x)) = of(g) * ↑(e x)`.

Fidelity checks (all pass):

- **Genuine `G`-equivariant iso** — an honest ℂ-linear `≃ₗ[ℂ]` (not merely an
  additive/ℂ-linear bijection asserted at a weaker level) *plus* the
  intertwining stated on all group elements `g : G`. Left multiplication by all
  of `ℂ[G]` is generated from the group elements by ℂ-linearity, so this is the
  full `ℂ[G]`-module iso.
- **Idempotent is the real one** — `idempotentOfChar χ = (Nat.card K : ℂ)⁻¹ •
  ∑ g:K, ((χ g : ℂˣ)⁻¹ : ℂ) • of(g:G)`: the `χ(g)⁻¹` inverse and the `|K|⁻¹`
  normalization are both literally present; `K ↪ G` via `(g : G)`. Idempotence
  `e_χ * e_χ = e_χ` is proved (`idempotentOfChar_mul_self`), and
  `of k * e_χ = χ(k) • e_χ` for `k∈K` (`of_smul_idempotentOfChar`).
- **Honest left ideal** — `charLeftIdeal χ = Submodule.span ℂ[G] {e_χ}`, the
  `ℂ[G]`-submodule generated by `e_χ`, i.e. `ℂ[G]·e_χ`; the target action is
  left multiplication `g·x = of(g)*x` (matches the intertwining RHS).
- **Correct induction** — source action is
  `Etingof.Definition5_8_1 K (chiRep χ) = Representation.ind` on
  `Representation.IndV K.subtype (chiRep χ)` (Definition 5.8.1).
- **Non-degenerate** — for `χ` trivial, `e_χ = |K|⁻¹ ∑_{g∈K} g` is the standard
  nonzero averaging idempotent, so neither the ideal nor the iso collapses.
- **Axiom-clean** — `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`, no `sorryAx`.

The Lean statement is not strictly weaker than the book's claim (the "naturally
isomorphic … as a `G`-representation" qualifier is realized by the ℂ-linear equiv
+ full group-element intertwining). Passed through as `covered_full`; no
follow-up feature issue.
