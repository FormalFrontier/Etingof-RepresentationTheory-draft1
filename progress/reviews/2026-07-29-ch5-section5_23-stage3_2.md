# Stage 3.2 review — Chapter 5, §5.23

Section 5.23 is complete. Algebraicity is defined for a bundled representation, so the action
laws cannot be lost by treating an arbitrary regular coefficient family as a representation.
Dominant integer weights are realized by determinant twists of Schur modules; the resulting
representations are simple, pairwise nonisomorphic, and exhaustive among simple algebraic
representations.

Theorem 5.23.2(i) gives complete reducibility with named `L_λ` constituents and uniqueness of
their highest weights. Part (ii) is a genuine `GL_N × GL_N`-equivariant Peter–Weyl equivalence:
the direct-sum matrix-coefficient map is proved equivariant, injective, and surjective.

Remark 5.23.3 is complete at the group level. Determinant shifts become trivial after restriction
to `SL_N`; the converse is proved; and intrinsically algebraic simple `SL_N` representations are
exhausted and parametrized by dominant weights modulo simultaneous shift. The source explicitly
says it will not prove the corresponding `sl_N` Lie-algebra classification, which remains recorded
in `skipped-exercises.md` rather than counted as a gap.

Fresh source checks pass for the definition, highest-weight construction, nonisomorphism,
classification, Peter–Weyl, `SL_N` restriction, and `SL_N` exhaustiveness providers. The stale
#7534, #7400, and #5384 references are removed; all three issues are closed.
