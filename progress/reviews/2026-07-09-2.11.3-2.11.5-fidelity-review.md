## Per-item findings

### Problem 2.11.3

- **(a) `exists_bilinear_equiv_linear` — FAITHFUL.** Bilinear `V × W → U` is modelled as
  `V →ₗ[k] W →ₗ[k] U` (curried, correct). Crucially the statement carries the characterizing
  equation `e f (v ⊗ w) = f v w`, which pins `e` down to *the* canonical lift on generators — so
  naturality ("without choosing bases") is genuinely captured, not just existence of some bijection.

- **(c) `exists_dualTensor_equiv_hom` — FIDELITY WEAKENING (main finding).**
  Stated as `Nonempty (V* ⊗ W ≃ₗ[k] (V →ₗ[k] W))` for finite-dimensional `V`. Any two vector spaces
  of equal dimension are linearly isomorphic, and `dim(V*⊗W) = dim V · dim W = dim Hom(V,W)`, so this
  `Nonempty` is **automatically true by a dimension count** and says nothing about the *natural* map.
  The book asks for the natural iso `V* ⊗ W → Hom(V,W)`, `f ⊗ w ↦ (v ↦ f(v)·w)`. The proof already
  uses `dualTensorHomEquiv k V W` (which *is* that natural map), so the statement should be
  strengthened to assert `dualTensorHom k V W` is a linear equiv, characterized on pure tensors via
  `dualTensorHom_apply : dualTensorHom k V W (f ⊗ₜ w) v = f v • w`. Confirmed available in Mathlib
  (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Contraction.lean`).

- **(b) `exists_basis_tensorProduct` — FIDELITY WEAKENING (minor).**
  Stated as `Nonempty (Module.Basis (ι × κ) k (V ⊗ W))`. This asserts *a* basis indexed by `ι × κ`
  exists (a dimension fact); it does not assert that the specific family `vᵢ ⊗ wⱼ` is a basis, which
  is what the book asks. The proof uses `b.tensorProduct c`; strengthen the statement to name that
  basis and pin its vectors with `Basis.tensorProduct_apply : (b.tensorProduct c) (i,j) = b i ⊗ₜ c j`
  (Mathlib `LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Basis.lean`).

- **(g) `det_comp` — FAITHFUL.** `det(A ∘ₗ B) = det A * det B` via `LinearMap.det_comp`, matching
  the book's `det(AB) = det(A)det(B)`. Note the book's `∧ᴺ A = det(A)·Id` half (the pedagogical
  content) is not formalized here; it is legitimately deferred with parts (d)–(f) (exterior powers).
  Acceptable as a recorded fact, but the "one-line proof via ∧ᴺ" is not what this file demonstrates.

### Exercise 2.11.5 — FAITHFUL and HONEST

- `instRightAlgebra : Algebra L (A ⊗[K] L)` — real instance via
  `Algebra.TensorProduct.rightAlgebra`. `algebraMap_right_apply` correctly identifies the structure
  map `l ↦ 1 ⊗ₜ l` by `rfl`.
- `exists_module_baseChange` — the constructed `(A ⊗_K L)`-module structure has the correct action on
  pure tensors `(a ⊗ l) • (v ⊗ l') = (a • v) ⊗ (l * l')`, matching the book's natural action. The
  transport of the `L`-action across `TensorProduct.comm` and assembly via
  `TensorProduct.Algebra.module` (guarded by an explicit `SMulCommClass A L` proof) is sound. No
  hypothesis is strong enough to make it vacuous.
- Minor suggestion (not a bug): the module structure is packaged inside an existential *theorem*
  rather than exposed as a global `instance`, so downstream files cannot yet `•` on `V ⊗_K L` over
  `A ⊗_K L`. If later work needs the action, promote it to an `instance`. Honest and correct as-is.
