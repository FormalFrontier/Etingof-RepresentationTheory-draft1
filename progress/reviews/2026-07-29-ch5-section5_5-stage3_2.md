# Stage 3.2 review — Chapter 5, §5.5

Section 5.5 consists entirely of the historical interlude on William Burnside: his career,
the development and reception of finite-group representation theory, his relationship to
Frobenius's work, the history of the `p^a q^b` theorem, and his influence on Philip Hall.
It contains no mathematical definition, theorem, or exercise endpoint requiring Lean code.

Both source records are therefore covered as historical context, with complete Stage 3.2
ledgers. The mathematical Burnside theorem discussed here is already formalized in §5.4 as
`Etingof.Theorem5_4_3`.
