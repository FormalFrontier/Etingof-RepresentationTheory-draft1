## Non-vacuity of the hypotheses

The universally-quantified `act` hypotheses are jointly satisfiable, so the theorems are
not vacuously true:

- **Vertices**: `A₅` acting by left multiplication on the 12 cosets of a Sylow-5 subgroup

transitive action all point stabilizers are conjugate, hence share the stated order — so

any action satisfying the hypotheses. This is a deliberate, conservative modeling choice,
