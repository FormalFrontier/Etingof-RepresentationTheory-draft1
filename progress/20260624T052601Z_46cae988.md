## Key decisions

- The project's `IsFiniteAbelianCategory` is a *ring*-level abstraction (abelian +
  enough projectives + finitely many simples; **no `k`-linear structure**). So
  `B_𝐧` is a ring and the comparisons are ring isomorphisms `≃+*`, not the
  `≃ₐ[k]` the issue text suggested. This is the faithful statement at the available
  level of abstraction; a `k`-algebra upgrade would require `𝒞` to carry `Linear k`.
- Stated the biconditional at the "endomorphism algebra of a progenerator" level
  rather than for an arbitrary algebra `D` with `ModuleCat D ≌ 𝒞`. The forward
  reduction (`D`-mod ≌ 𝒞 ⟹ `D ≅ (End Q)ᵒᵖ` for a progenerator `Q`) needs the
  regular-module-is-progenerator transport across an equivalence, which is not yet
  in the project. Combined with Theorem 9.6.4 (which supplies the reverse), the
  proved biconditional is the precise mathematical content of the book's claim.
