## Accomplished

Issue #4781 (Ch6 #4777 S1), **deliverable 1** of 2 — sorry-free.

New file `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter6/DecompositionExistence.lean`:

- `Etingof.QuiverRepresentation.exists_decomposition`: every finite-dimensional
  quiver representation over a field (`Module.Finite k (V.obj v)` at each vertex)
  is `AreIsomorphic` to an iterated `directSumList` of `IsIndecomposable`
  representations. Proof by strong induction on `∑ v, finrank (V.obj v)`:
  indecomposable ⟹ singleton; all spaces trivial ⟹ empty list; otherwise the
  negation of `IsIndecomposable` yields two complementary invariant
  sub-representations, each of strictly smaller total dimension, and we recurse.
  **Existence only — uniqueness/Krull–Schmidt deliberately avoided** (per #4777
  step 1).

Supporting infrastructure (all sorry-free):
- `AreIsomorphic.refl/.symm/.trans/.intertwine` and `.directSum` (congruence).
- `zeroRep`, `directSumList`, with unit/associativity/append isomorphisms.
- `subRep` (sub-representation from an arrow-invariant family of submodules) and
  `areIsomorphic_subRep_directSum` (the complementary-decomposition splitting).

Wired into `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter6.lean`.

### Key patterns / decisions

- `QuiverRepresentation.obj` carries only `AddCommMonoid`, so the public
  hypothesis is `Module.Finite` (not `FiniteDimensional`, which needs
  `AddCommGroup`). `AddCommGroup` is added locally via
  `Etingof.addCommGroupOfRing` only inside the splitting branch, where complements
  and `finrank` additivity are needed.
- `Etingof.QuiverRepresentation.directSum` returns obj-universe `max u₁ u₂`. To
  avoid a spurious free universe in the `directSumList` fold, `zeroRep` is fixed to
  `PUnit : Type 0` and `exists_decomposition` is stated at obj-universe `0`
  (`QuiverRepresentation.{uk,0,0,uh}`) — exactly the setting the orbit-counting
  application uses (`V.obj v ≃ Fin (d v) → k`).
