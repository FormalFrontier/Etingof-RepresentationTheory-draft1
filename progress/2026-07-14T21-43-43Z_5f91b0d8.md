Proof outline (as implemented):

  summand `P₀` (project onto the unlinked summand; the image has both linked and unlinked factors,
  hence is trivial). This makes `P₀`, `Q₀` stable under every `R`-linear endomorphism.
- Right multiplications `Ta a` (transported to `Shrink R` through `Shrink.linearEquiv`) are
  `R`-linear, so `P₀`, `Q₀` are two-sided; the projection onto `P₀` commutes with them.
- `c := sh (Π (sh.symm 1))` is right-multiplication by a central idempotent (`hPiR`, `hidem_c`,
  `hcentral_c`); `c' := 1 - c` is the complementary one.
- `c` acts as the identity on `P₀` (hence on `S`, a factor of `P₀`); `c'` acts as the identity on
  `Q₀` (hence on `T`). If `T` is a factor of `P₀` we get `AreLinked S T` immediately; otherwise
  `f = f·c + f·c'` splits `f` into two nonzero orthogonal central idempotents, contradicting
  `IsIndecomposableCentralIdempotent`.
