  `edgesAct` are the constructed actions, with transitivity and stabilizer-order lemmas.
* `quotStabEquivFin` — every transitive `A₅`-action on `Fin N` *is* a coset action (the orbit map
  descends to an equivariant bijection from `A₅ ⧸ stabSub act i₀`).
* `quotientEquivOfConjSubgroup` — conjugate subgroups have equivariantly isomorphic coset spaces.
* `verticesAct_unique`, `facesAct_unique`, `edgesAct_unique` — combining the previous two with the
  conjugacy results already in `Problem4_12_5.lean` (Sylow for orders 5 and 3, conjugacy of
  involutions for order 2): any transitive `A₅`-action of the right degree and stabilizer order is
  equivariantly isomorphic to the constructed model. This is what makes the coset model *the*
  icosahedral action rather than one arbitrary choice.
* `vertices_decomposition_icosahedral`, `faces_decomposition_icosahedral`,
  `edges_decomposition_icosahedral` — the three decompositions with no hypotheses.
