## Accomplished

Issue #7911 (child of #7882, child of #5723): built
`Chapter8/KoszulHomotopy.lean`, the characteristic-free contracting homotopy of
the augmented Koszul complex. No `sorry`, no `native_decide`; all five main
results are axiom-clean (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only).

New public API, all as specified in the issue:

* `Etingof.IsKoszulPivot α s a` — the pivot condition: `a ∈ supp α`, `a` is `≤`
  everything in `supp α`, and `a <` everything in `s`. Decidable.
  `IsKoszulPivot.unique` shows a pair has at most one pivot.
* `Etingof.koszulHFun` / `Etingof.koszulH b i : koszulX k V i →ₗ[k] koszulX k V (i+1)`.
* `Etingof.koszulEta : KoszulAugModule k V →ₗ[k] koszulX k V 0`, `1 ↦ 1 ⊗ 1`.
* `Etingof.koszulAug_koszulEta` — `ε ∘ η = id`.
* `Etingof.koszulD_koszulH_add_koszulH_koszulD` — `d h + h d = id` on `C_{i+1}`.
* `Etingof.koszulD_koszulH_add_eta_aug` — `d h + η ε = id` on `C₀`.
* `LinearMap`-level restatements of the last two, plus the basis-vector versions
  they are derived from.

Supporting material worth knowing about:

* `Etingof.insertElem` — the counterpart of the existing `Etingof.eraseElem`.
* `Etingof.finsetPos_insert_self` / `_insert_of_mem` / `finsetPos_min'` — the sign
  bookkeeping: inserting a new minimum puts it in position 0 and shifts every
  other position up by one.
* `Etingof.sub_single_add_single`, `sub_single_add_single_comm`,
  `add_single_sub_single`, `mem_support_add_single` — `Finsupp`-exponent arithmetic.
* `instance : IsScalarTower k (SymmetricAlgebra k V) (KoszulAugModule k V)` —
  needed before `(koszulAug k V).restrictScalars k` will even elaborate.
* `Etingof.koszulDTerm` + `koszulD_koszulKBasis'` — the differential as a sum over
  the plain finset `s` rather than `s.attach`, which is what makes the `a = p`
  term splittable with `Finset.sum_insert`.

## Next step

Claim #7912 and prove exactness. The two identities in this file are exactly the
input: a `k`-linear contracting homotopy makes the augmented complex acyclic as a
complex of `k`-modules, and the terms of a chain complex of `SV`-modules have the
same underlying homology whether computed over `SV` or over `k`, since the
forgetful functor is exact and faithful. Concretely, for `x ∈ ker (koszulD b i)`,
`x = d (h x) + h (d x) = d (h x)`, so `ker d ⊆ im d` directly — no derived-functor
machinery needed. The degree-0 identity gives `ker ε = im d₀` the same way.
