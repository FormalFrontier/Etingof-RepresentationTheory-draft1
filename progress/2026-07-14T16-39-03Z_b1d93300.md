## Accomplished
Formalized Problem 9.5.3(i)-B (issue #6597): the **primitive central idempotent decomposition**
of a finite dimensional algebra, in the new file
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter9/Problem9_5_3_PrimitiveIdempotents.lean`. All new
declarations are sorry-free (`#print axioms` = `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`).

Key results (namespace `Etingof.Problem953`):
- `isIndecomposableCentralIdempotent_comm_iff` — in a commutative ring, `e` indecomposable ⇔
  nonzero idempotent with every idempotent below it equal to `0` or `e`.
- `IsIndecomposableCentralIdempotent.ringEquiv` — transfer along a `CommRing` isomorphism.
- `isIndecomposable_pi_single` — `Pi.single i 1` is indecomposable in a finite product of fields.
- `exists_completeOrthogonal_isIndecomposable_of_commArtinian` — a commutative Artinian ring has a
  finite complete orthogonal family of indecomposable idempotents, and every indecomposable
  idempotent is one of them. Proof: pull the standard idempotents of `A ⧸ nil(A) ≅ ∏ᵢ (A ⧸ mᵢ)`
  (`IsArtinianRing.quotNilradicalEquivPi`) back along the nilpotent-kernel quotient
  (`CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents.lift_of_isNilpotent_ker`).
- `isIndecomposableCentralIdempotent_center_iff` — indecomposability of a central idempotent of
  `R` matches indecomposability in the center `Z(R) = Subalgebra.center k R`.
- `exists_completeOrthogonal_isIndecomposableCentral` — the decomposition `1 = Σ eₖ` for a
  finite dimensional algebra `R` (deliverable 1), plus uniqueness (every indecomposable central
  idempotent is one of the `eₖ`).
- `finite_isIndecomposableCentralIdempotent` — the subtype is finite (deliverable 2).

The predicate `IsIndecomposableCentralIdempotent` was **moved** from `Problem9_5_3.lean` into the
new lower file (same full name), and `Problem9_5_3.lean` now imports it. This keeps the heavy
ring-theory development in a lightweight file while remaining usable by the block-assembly theorem
`blocks_equiv_indecomposableCentralIdempotents` (still sorried, issue #6598) inside
`Problem9_5_3.lean`.
