## Accomplished

Fixed the fresh-build regression in `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Problem3_8_5.lean`
(issue #7533). The projection construction inside `antiperiodic_isIndecomposable` used
`LinearMap.ofIsCompl hC W₁.subtype 0`, which no longer elaborates: the non-`@`
application fails to infer the ring/module (`R = ↥periodicSubalg`, `E = ↥antiperiodicSubmod`)
from `W₁`, leaving `hC`'s type `IsCompl W₁ W₂` unable to unify against `IsCompl ?p ?q`.

Replaced it with `@Submodule.projection (periodicSubalg) _ (antiperiodicSubmod) _ _ W₁ W₂ hC`,
pinning `R` and `E` explicitly, and proved `hleft`/`hright` from
`Submodule.projection_apply_of_mem_left`/`_right` (also in `@`-explicit form fed through
`simpa [hπ] using`). `hmem` reverted to the linear-decomposition derivation from `hleft`/`hright`.

## Key findings

- The regression is an elaboration-order issue, not a missing API. `Submodule.projection`
  is the clean self-map projection along a complement; its `_apply_of_mem_left/right` and
  `_apply_mem` lemmas replace the `ofIsCompl_apply_left/right` route directly.
- `exact @Submodule.projection... ` (defeq) times out on heartbeats because it forces
  whnf of the projection term plus instance synthesis; `simpa [hπ] using (@...)` succeeds
  because the simplified goal guides instance synthesis. Use `simpa`, not `exact`, here.
- The shell's `grep` is a wrapper that errors with `unknown option '-G'` in this Bash tool;
  use `command grep` or `awk`.
