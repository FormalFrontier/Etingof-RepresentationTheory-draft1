`t125Rep_kQ_leaf_equalities` envisioned by the issue (concluding
concrete-element memberships `(x : Fin (k·(m+1)) → F) ∈ W₁ ⟨v⟩`) **cannot

`(t125Rep_kQ …).obj ⟨v, …⟩` of the `by letI := Q; exact {…}` representation
