Added `Etingof.Theorem_8_1_1_i_iff_iv` in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter8/Theorem8_1_1.lean`. Exactness of `Hom_A(P, ?)`
is formalized as preservation of short exact sequences: for every short exact
sequence `0 → K →[ι] M →[π] N → 0`, the image sequence
`0 → Hom(P,K) → Hom(P,M) → Hom(P,N) → 0` is short exact (post-composition with `ι`
injective, range = kernel of post-composition with `π`, post-composition with `π`
surjective). The left-exact conjuncts hold for every `P`; the surjectivity conjunct
is the lifting property, proved equivalent to `Module.Projective` via
`Module.projective_lifting_property` and `Module.Projective.of_lifting_property''`.
With the existing pairwise-with-(i) theorems this establishes the full four-way
equivalence transitively.
