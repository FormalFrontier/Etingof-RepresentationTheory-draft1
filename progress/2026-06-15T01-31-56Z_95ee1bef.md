  whichever of `e02/e12/e43` are reversed (reversed leaf edge ⟹ push the

  edge ⟹ fill the complement). Branch 1 (this PR) is one of them; the natural
  next step is `e43`-reversed-`e53`-reversed (combo D) and the `e12`/`e02`
  reversed variants that keep `e23` canonical & `e53` reversed.
