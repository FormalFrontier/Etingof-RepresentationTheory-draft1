- **`repC3plus_simple`, `repC3minus_simple`**: simplicity of the two golden-ratio
  rotation representations `ℂ³₊`, `ℂ³₋` via `FDRep.simple_iff_char_is_norm_one`.
  The 60-term norm sum `∑_g χ(g)·χ(g⁻¹)` is reduced fiberwise over the five
  conjugacy classes: each `g` is conjugate to `classRepA5 (classIdxA5 g)`
  (`classIdxA5_spec`) and each rep to its inverse (`classRepA5_inv_conj`), so each
  summand is `χ(classRepA5 (classIdxA5 g))²`. Collecting fibers
  (`Finset.sum_fiberwise'`, `classIdxA5_card`) gives
  `1·9 + 20·0 + 15·1 + 12·φ² + 12·φ'² = 60`, with `φ² + φ'² = 3` closed by

  integer helper table `tblA5`; rows 1/2 are `repC3plus/minus_character`), and
