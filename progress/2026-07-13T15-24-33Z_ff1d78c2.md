- `periodicSubalg_idempotent_eq_zero_or_one`: an idempotent element of the period-1
  continuous function algebra `A` is `0` or `1`. Pointwise `e(x)²=e(x)` gives `e(x)∈{0,1}`;
  the set `{x | e(x)=1}` is clopen (both it and its complement are closed preimages of
  singletons), so by `PreconnectedSpace ℝ` it is `∅` or `univ`, making `e` constant.
- `periodic_isIndecomposable`: `A` indecomposable — a complemented pair of ideals gives
  an idempotent `e₁` (with `e₁·e₂ ∈ W₁ ⊓ W₂ = ⊥`), forcing one summand to `⊤`, the other `⊥`.
- `antiperiodic_isIndecomposable`: `M` (antiperiodic functions) indecomposable. New infra:
  `cosπ`, `sinπ` (frame sections, both antiperiodic), `cosπ_sq_add_sinπ_sq` (c²+s²=1),
  `antiMul` (product of two antiperiodics is periodic → element of `A`), `smul_apply_coe`,
  and `antiperiodic_frame_decomp` (every antiperiodic `f = (c·f)·c + (s·f)·s`). A complemented
  decomposition yields the A-linear idempotent projection `π = LinearMap.ofIsCompl`; the frame
  decomposition shows `π f = e • f` with `e = c·π(c) + s·π(s) ∈ A`; idempotence of `π` plus
  `c²+s²=1` (tested against `c` and `s`) gives `e²=e`, so `e∈{0,1}` reusing the A-lemma.
