Closed the fidelity gap on `Chapter5/Corollary5.12.4` (issue #5381, wave-1 epic #5338).

- **Gap:** Book claim "All irreducible representations of S_n can be given by matrices with
  rational entries." The old `Etingof.Corollary5_12_4` only asserted `∃ c_int : ℤ[S_n],
  cast c_int = YoungSymmetrizer` (integrality of the Young symmetrizer) — strictly weaker
  than the corollary's conclusion; it never asserted that the irreducibles are realizable
  over ℚ.

- **Fix:** Rewrote `Corollary5_12_4` to assert the full claim. For every simple
  `ℂ[S_n]`-module `M` it now provides:
  1. the classification `M ≅ V_λ` (via `Theorem5_12_2_classification`);
  2. simplicity of the rational form `V_λ^ℚ = ℚ[S_n]·c_λ` as a `ℚ[S_n]`-module (via
     `SpechtModuleK_isSimpleModule`) — an irreducible representation defined over ℚ; and
  3. an `S_n`-equivariant `ℂ`-linear isomorphism `ℂ ⊗_ℚ V_λ^ℚ ≃ V_λ`, exhibiting `V_λ` as
     the complexification of a rational representation (= realizable by rational matrices).

  The core is a new lemma `SpechtModule_complexification`. The map
  `T : ℂ ⊗_ℚ (ℚ[S_n]·c_λ) → ℂ[S_n]` is the base change of the inclusion along ℚ→ℂ followed
  by `TensorProduct.finsuppScalarRight` (module-level base change of monoid algebras — works
  for the noncommutative `S_n`, unlike `MonoidAlgebra.scalarTensorEquiv` which needs
  `CommMonoid`). `T` is injective because ℂ is flat (free) over ℚ
  (`Module.Flat.lTensor_preserves_injective_linearMap`), and its image is exactly `V_λ`, so
  it corestricts to an isomorphism. `T(z ⊗ w) = z · cast w` makes the iso `S_n`-equivariant.
