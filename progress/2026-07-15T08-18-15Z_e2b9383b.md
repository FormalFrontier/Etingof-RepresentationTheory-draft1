new `decide` lemma `A5_no_order_four`), lying in the single `2a` class. So the Frobenius

plugs in `∑σ = 4`; the nontrivial case plugs in `∑σ = 0` (`indV4_nontriv_charSum_zero`:
a nontrivial simple character of the abelian order-4 group sums to zero, via multiplicativity
of the 1-dim character — `CommGroup ↥H` from `IsPGroup.commGroupOfCardEqPrimeSq`, dim 1 from
`Example4_3_FiniteAbelianGroups`).
