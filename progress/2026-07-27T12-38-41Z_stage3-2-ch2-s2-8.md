## Result

whereas the book writes `ab` for “first `b`, then `a`.”  To preserve downstream
users while presenting the source convention exactly, the audit adds
`BookPathAlgebra k Q := (PathAlgebra k Q)ᵐᵒᵖ` and proves the book-facing path
multiplication rules.

In particular, `Problem2.8.6` now proves the printed relations

- `a_h * p_source = a_h` and `p_target * a_h = a_h`;
- the corresponding annihilation relations at all other vertices;
- `1 = ∑ᵢ p_i` for finite quivers;
- generation by the vertex idempotents and arrow elements; and
- the universal property with precisely those relations.

The pass also adds the explicit three-arrow diagram data for `Example2.8.2`, a
dependent arrow type recording source and target, restriction of arrow maps to
quiver subrepresentations, precise zero/nonzero/irreducible/indecomposable
predicates, the book-facing module-to-representation construction, and locally
finite graded/Hilbert-series definitions with the degree pieces for the free
and path algebras.
