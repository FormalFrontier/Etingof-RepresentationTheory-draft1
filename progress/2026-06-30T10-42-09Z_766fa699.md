## Accomplished
Closed conjunct (1) of the fidelity gap in `Chapter5/Example5.19.3` (issue #5638):
**GL(V)-equivariance** of the two isomorphisms `L_{(n)} ≅ SⁿV` and `L_{(1ⁿ)} ≅ ∧ⁿV`.

The previous statements (`Example5_19_3_symmetric`, `Example5_19_3_exterior`) only
asserted `Nonempty (… ≃ₗ[k] …)` — bare `k`-linear isomorphisms, dropping the book's
claim that these are isomorphisms of `GL(V)`-representations. New material in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter5/Example5_19_3.lean`:

- `symmetricPowerMap g : SⁿV →ₗ SⁿV` — the functorial action of `g : V →ₗ V` on the
  symmetric power, built by descending the diagonal action `g ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ g` through the
  quotient `mk` (`AddCon.lift`), with `@[simp] symmetricPowerMap_mk` characterizing it.
- `symInvariants_map_mem` / `symAntisymmetric_map_mem` — the diagonal action preserves
  the symmetric / antisymmetric subspaces (it commutes with permutations, via the
  existing `symGroupAction_comm_diagonalAction`).
- `π_map` — the antisymmetrizing projection `π` is natural in `V`, intertwining the
  diagonal action with `exteriorPower.map n g`.
- Refactored the two existence theorems to expose concrete isomorphisms
  (`Example5_19_3_symmetricEquiv`, `Example5_19_3_exteriorEquiv`) plus `_apply` simp
  lemmas; the original `Example5_19_3_symmetric` / `_exterior` keep their signatures.
- `symInvariantsMap` / `symAntisymmetricMap` (the restricted diagonal actions) and the
  equivariance theorems `Example5_19_3_symmetric_equivariant`,
  `Example5_19_3_exterior_equivariant`, each stating the iso intertwines the diagonal
  `GL(V)`-action with the functorial action on the target. Restricting `g` to `GL(V)`
  gives the equivariance the book asserts.

Module builds cleanly, no sorries.
