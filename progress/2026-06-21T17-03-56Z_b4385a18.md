  general lemma is *false* (det⁻¹ twists are algebraic but carry negative weights,
  so ℕ-indexed weight spaces cannot span). Instead routed through the *polynomial*

  against a coordinate functional `φ` with `φ s = 1`, `s` a basis vector of `S i`).
  Then `⊤ = q ⊤ = q (⨆_μ wtₘ V^{⊗n}) = ⨆_μ q(wtₘ) ≤ ⨆_μ wtₘ(L i)`.

  in `Chapter5/FormalCharacterIso.lean`: the weight spaces of `V^{⊗n}` span (every
  standard tensor basis vector is a weight vector). Proven from the in-file private
  `tensorStdBasis_mem_glWeightSpace` + `Basis.span_eq`.

  ℂ-linear map sends the μ-weight space of its source into the μ-weight space of its
