## Accomplished

Implemented issue #6790 (feature): `affineNullVector_pos` in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter6/Problem6_1_3_continued_tildeE.lean`.

Sorry-free proof that the Cartan form `A = 2·Id − adj` of an affine Dynkin
diagram has a strictly-positive integer null vector `w` with `A ·ᵥ w = 0`
(discrete Perron–Frobenius / step 1 of #6785). Structure:

- `hMij` / `Bform` / `hdotcomm`: entry formula and symmetric bilinear expansion
  `uᵀ M v = ∑ᵢ∑ⱼ uᵢ Mᵢⱼ vⱼ`.
- Sign-folding: `w = |x|` from a degenerate null vector `x`; termwise
  `wᵢMᵢⱼwⱼ ≤ xᵢMᵢⱼxⱼ` gives `A(w) ≤ A(x) = 0`, and semidefiniteness gives
  `A(w) = 0`.
- Polarization at each `eₖ`: `A(t·w + eₖ) = 2t(A·ᵥw)ₖ + 2 ≥ 0` for all integer
  `t`; the vanishing `t²` term (since `A(w)=0`) forces `(A·ᵥw)ₖ = 0`
  (evaluated at `t = ±2`, closed by `omega`). Hence `A ·ᵥ w = 0`.
- Strict positivity: the zero-set of `w` is closed under adjacency
  (`2wₚ = ∑ⱼ adjₚⱼ wⱼ` with `wₚ = 0` and nonnegative terms), and the
  `SimpleGraph`/`ReflTransGen` connectivity (reused from
  `isDynkinDiagram_isTree`) propagates `w = 0` from any zero entry to the
  nonzero entry witnessing `x ≠ 0`, a contradiction.
