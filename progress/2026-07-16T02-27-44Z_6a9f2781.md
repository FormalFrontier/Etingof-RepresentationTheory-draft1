### Key idea (the native-vs-restricted `Module k` obstacle)

`extTensorFunctorObj` bakes the free carriers' `k`-module structure as restriction of scalars along
`k → A₁ᵐᵒᵖ` (the `restrictModule` local instances), which is **not** defeq to the standard `Finsupp`
`k`-module, so `finsuppTensorFinsupp` does not apply on the nose (confirmed: `rfl` fails to identify
the two tensor types). Bridge:

1. `freeCastEquiv₁`/`freeCastEquiv₂`: identity function as a `k`-linear equiv restricted-→-native
   (`map_smul'` discharged by `algebraMap_smul`, since the two `k`-actions agree).
2. `freeExtTensorEquivK`: `TensorProduct.congr` of those, then `finsuppTensorFinsupp`, then
   `Finsupp.mapRange.linearEquiv` of `Algebra.TensorProduct.opAlgEquiv` — a `k`-linear equiv to
   `I₁ × I₂ →₀ (A₁ ⊗ A₂)ᵐᵒᵖ`.
3. `freeExtTensorEquiv`: promote to `(A₁ ⊗ A₂)ᵐᵒᵖ`-linear by checking the external simple-tensor
   action. Reduced to the generator identity `freeExtTensorEquivK_op_smul_tmul` (proved
   coordinatewise via `Finsupp.ext` + `opAlgEquiv` multiplicativity), then `MulOpposite.rec'` +
   `TensorProduct.induction_on` for the general case.
4. `ModuleCat.projective_of_free (Module.Basis.ofRepr freeExtTensorEquiv)`.

Gotcha worth recording: coordinate access (`x i`) on a `↑(ModuleCat...)`-typed Finsupp does not
elaborate; route it through the `freeCastEquiv` coercions. And `rw`/`simp` under the
`↑(extTensorFunctorObj ...)` coercion fails on `smul_zero`/`add_tmul` (instance mismatch), so
`freeExtTensorEquiv` is stated over the raw `↑F₁ ⊗[k] ↑F₂` carrier (defeq, same `extModule`
instance) rather than `↑(extTensorFunctorObj ...)`.
