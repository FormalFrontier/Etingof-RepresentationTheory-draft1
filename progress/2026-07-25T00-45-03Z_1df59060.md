### Scope finding

`𝔤₄ = ⟨x, y | ad(x)²y = ad(y)⁵x = 0⟩` has Cartan matrix `[[2,-1],[-4,2]]`, determinant `0`: it is
the nilpotent positive part `𝔫₊` of the twisted affine Kac–Moody algebra of type `A₂⁽²⁾`. Its
graded dimensions by `x`-degree are `1, 5, 3, 5, 3, …`. Producing a `Module.Basis` (the issue's
stated acceptance bar — an infinite independent family is explicitly not enough) requires both a
lower bound (the loop realization) and a matching upper bound (a Serre-relations spanning
argument). That is several sessions of Lean, not one, so #7394 was decomposed:

- **#7717** the twisted loop subalgebra `𝔫₊ ⊆ 𝔤𝔩₃(k[t])` — **done this session**
- **#7718** its `Module.Basis`, and `range matHom₄ = loopPos` (blocked on #7717)
- **#7719** the upper bound: an explicit spanning family for `𝔤₄` closed under `ad x`, `ad y`
  (independent of the other two; this is the crux, and the book's own "(Hard!)")
- **#7720** assembly into `Module.Basis … (g k 4)` plus the items.json / skipped-exercises fix

All structure constants the successors need are worked out in the sub-issue bodies (verified
numerically before writing them): the `σ`-eigenspace bases, `[𝔤₁,𝔤₁] = 𝔤₀` (rank 3),
`[𝔤₀,𝔤₁] = 𝔤₁` (rank 5), the five `ad(NY)ⁱ NX` with their `3` and `6` scalars, and the
degree-2 relation table for `𝔤₄` derived from `⁅a₀,a₁⁆ = 0` by applying `ad(y)`.

### #7717 delivered (all in `Chapter2/Problem2_16_3.lean`, sorry-free)

- `sigInv`: the involution `σ(A) = -J Aᵀ J`, entrywise `σ(A)ᵢⱼ = -A_{rev j, rev i}`. Involutive,
  additive, `k`-linear, `σ(A)·σ(B) = -σ(B·A)`, hence `σ⁅A,B⁆ = ⁅σA, σB⁆`. Plus `sigInv_single`.
- `gzero : Fin 3 → …` and `gone : Fin 5 → …`, bases of the `+1`- and `-1`-eigenspaces of `σ` in
  `𝔰𝔩₃`, with eigenvalue, trace and linear-independence lemmas. Independence needs **no**
  characteristic hypothesis: the members have pairwise disjoint supports, so reading off one
  entry per index suffices.
- `twPoly` (the algebra involution `t ↦ -t` of `k[t]`) with `twPoly_coeff`, and its entrywise
  matrix versions `twMat`, `constMat`, `coeffMat`.
- `loopPos k : LieSubalgebra k (Matrix (Fin 3) (Fin 3) k[X])`, the positive part of `A₂⁽²⁾`.
- `NX_mem_loopPos`, `NY_mem_loopPos`.
- `sigInv_eq_twMat_iff`: `σ(P(t)) = P(-t)` iff the coefficient of `tⁿ` is in the
  `(-1)ⁿ`-eigenspace of `σ` — the bridge #7718 needs to turn `loopPos` into the graded span.

## Decisions made

**Cut `𝔫₊` out by intrinsic conditions, not as a span.** `loopPos` is defined as
`{P | trace P = 0 ∧ σ P = twMat P ∧ P(0) ∈ k·(E₀₁ - E₁₂)}`. Each condition is visibly stable under
the bracket (trace of a commutator vanishes; `σ` and `t ↦ -t` are both automorphisms;
`⁅P,Q⁆(0) = ⁅P(0),Q(0)⁆` and the bracket of two multiples of one element is `0`), so the
`LieSubalgebra` closure proof is four short lines. Defining it as the span
`k·NY ⊕ ⨁_{n≥1} 𝔤_{n mod 2}·tⁿ` instead would have forced a 49-entry table of generator brackets.
The span equality is deferred to #7718, which now has `sigInv_eq_twMat_iff` to do it with.

The trace condition is not decorative: the `-1`-eigenspace of `σ` in `𝔤𝔩₃` is 6-dimensional
(it contains `I`), and only its traceless part is the 5-dimensional `𝔤₁` that `range matHom₄`
actually meets.

## Key patterns discovered

- **Don't pass `Matrix.single` to `simp`.** Unfolding it to `of fun i' j' => if … then c else 0`
  makes simp rewrite diagonal sums into `Finset.card` of set comprehensions, which then will not
  close. Leaving it folded lets the existing `single_apply_same` / `single_apply_of_ne` simp
  lemmas fire with `decide` discharging the `Fin` side conditions.
- **Prove `sigInv_single` once instead of `ext`-ing nine entries per matrix.** The naive
  `fin_cases i <;> (ext a b; fin_cases a <;> fin_cases b <;> simp [...])` over five 3×3 matrices
  hit the `whnf` heartbeat limit. With `sigInv_single` plus `@[simp]` structural lemmas
  (`sigInv_add/sub/neg/smul`), each eigenvector check is `simp [gone] <;> abel`.
- Write scalar multiples inside `Matrix.single` (`Matrix.single 1 1 (2 : k)`) rather than as
  `(2 : k) • Matrix.single 1 1 1`; simp normalizes the two differently and the smul form stops
  `sigInv_smul` from firing.
- `ext a b` on an equality of polynomial matrices applies `Polynomial.ext` too — use `ext a b n`,
  not a follow-up `apply Polynomial.ext`.
