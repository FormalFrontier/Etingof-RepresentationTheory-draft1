* `CategoryTheory.projectiveResolutions_additive` — taking projective resolutions is an
  *additive* functor `C ⥤ HomotopyCategory C (down ℕ)`. Mathlib had the functor laws but not
  this one. Proof: `lift f + lift g` is a lift of `f + g`, so `ProjectiveResolution.liftHomotopy`
  identifies it with `lift (f + g)` in the homotopy category.

  additive functor is additive (composite of three additive factors).
* `CategoryTheory.NatTrans.mapHomologicalComplex_add` and
  `CategoryTheory.NatTrans.leftDerived_add` — `NatTrans.leftDerived` is additive in the natural
  transformation. Mathlib had only `leftDerived_id` and `leftDerived_comp`.

## Key findings

* **`Ext` additivity is already in Mathlib, in both variables.** `Abelian.Ext.biprodAddEquiv`,
  `Ext.biproductAddEquiv` (first variable), `Abelian.Ext.addEquivBiprod`,
  `Ext.addEquivBiproduct` (second variable), in
  `Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/DerivedCategory/Ext/Basic.lean`. `Etingof.Ext` is a reducible
  abbreviation for `Abelian.Ext`, so they apply verbatim — no project-side work needed. The
  `Ext` half of Problem 8.2.7 therefore only waits on the structure theorem.
* **Second-argument functoriality of `Tor` already existed in the project**, as
  `Etingof.torSndMap` in `Chapter8/Problem8_2_6_Core.lean`. That is what made second-argument
  additivity tractable in one session; without it the second argument would have needed a
  biproduct-of-functors argument.
* `ModuleCat` finite biproducts need the explicit import
  `Mathlib.Algebra.Category.ModuleCat.Biproducts`; `AddCommGrpCat` gets them transitively.
