## Accomplished
Closed the fidelity gap in `Chapter9/Theorem9.6.4` (issue #5665). `Etingof.Theorem_9_6_4`
(Morita equivalence) previously carried an extra `[IsNoetherianRing (End P)ᵐᵒᵖ]` hypothesis
that the book never states. Etingof's Theorem 9.6.4 assumes only a finite abelian category
*over a field `k`*, in which `End P` is finite dimensional over `k` (already proven in the
project as `Etingof.IsFiniteAbelianCategoryOverField.finiteDimensional_hom`), hence
Noetherian. So the hypothesis was an over-hypothesis, making the stated theorem weaker and
less reusable than the book's.

Fix:
- Added `Etingof.isNoetherianRing_endOp_of_overField`: in a `k`-linear finite abelian
  category over a field, `(End P)ᵐᵒᵖ` is a Noetherian ring (finite-dimensional over `k` via
  `finiteDimensional_hom`, transported to the opposite, then `isNoetherian_of_tower`).
- Kept the original Noetherian-based proof as a strictly-more-general ring-level engine,
  `Etingof.Theorem_9_6_4_of_isNoetherian` (no `k`-linear structure needed).
- Restated `Etingof.Theorem_9_6_4` in the book's setting (`[Linear k C]
  [IsFiniteAbelianCategoryOverField k C]`, progenerator `P`), deriving the Noetherian
  instance and delegating to the engine. Same for the corollary: a ring-level
  `Theorem_9_6_4_corollary_of_isNoetherian` engine plus the faithful over-field
  `Theorem_9_6_4_corollary`.
- Updated the sole downstream consumer, §9.7 `nonempty_equivalence_fgModuleCat_Bn` in
  `Introduction_9_7_Morita.lean`, to call the ring-level corollary engine, preserving that
  file's deliberate ring-level (no `k`-linearity) design.
