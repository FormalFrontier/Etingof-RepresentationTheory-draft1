* `Etingof.exists_simple_character_eq_sub (A B : FDRep ℂ G)`: if
  `(card G : ℂ)⁻¹ • ∑ g, (χ_A g − χ_B g) * conj (χ_A g − χ_B g) = 1` and
  `finrank ℂ B < finrank ℂ A`, then there is a **simple** `W : FDRep ℂ G` with
  `χ_W = χ_A − χ_B`.
* `Etingof.simpleOfVirtualChar A B _ _ : FDRep ℂ G` — the representation itself (a real
  `def`, body is `Exists.choose`, no `sorry`), with `simpleOfVirtualChar_simple` (instance),
  `simpleOfVirtualChar_character` (`@[simp]`) and
  `finrank_simpleOfVirtualChar : dim W = dim A − dim B`.

Two obligations that previously fell on the caller are now discharged internally:

1. **The complete family.** `Etingof.Lemma5_7_2` takes a family of pairwise non-isomorphic
   simples plus an integer expansion of the virtual character over it. Callers do not have
   one. But every finite group has one — `exists_simples_sum_finrank_sq_eq_card` (the column
   representations of the Wedderburn–Artin decomposition of `ℂ[G]`) — and
   `Etingof.FDRep.exists_character_eq_sum` expands `χ_A` and `χ_B` over it with
   natural-number multiplicities `aᵢ`, `bᵢ`, so `χ_A − χ_B = Σ (aᵢ − bᵢ) χ_{Tᵢ}` is an
   integer combination for free. The caller only has to supply `A` and `B`.
2. **The two inner-product conventions.** `Lemma5_7_2` pairs `χ(g)` against `χ(g⁻¹)`; the
   Chapter 5 computations (`Etingof.Lemma5_25_3_innerProduct`) pair against
   `starRingEnd ℂ (χ g)`. `Etingof.char_inv_eq_conj` (Chapter4/Discussion_4_4) converts, so
   `simpleOfVirtualChar`'s hypothesis is stated in the `starRingEnd` form and plugs straight
   into `Lemma5_25_3_innerProduct` with no reshaping.
