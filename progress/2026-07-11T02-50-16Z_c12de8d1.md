## Key finding (obstruction)

The polynomial-case pattern in `Example9_4_4.lean` does **not** transfer. That proof uses
`ModuleCat.extendScalars`, whose Mathlib signature requires `[CommRing R] [CommRing S]`
(`Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/ChangeOfRings.lean:389`). For the path algebra the base
extension is the **noncommutative induction** `A ⊗_S −` along the vertex embedding
`S = Q → k → A`, `(aᵢ) ↦ ∑ aᵢ eᵢ`. The vertex idempotents `eᵢ` are **not central** in `A`, so `A`
is not a commutative-base `S`-algebra and `extendScalars` is inapplicable. Building `A ⊗_S −` as a
left-adjoint-to-`restrictScalars` functor (arbitrary rings) that preserves projectives is genuine
new infrastructure not in Mathlib; that plus the standard-resolution exactness computation is the
remaining work (tracked in #6420).
