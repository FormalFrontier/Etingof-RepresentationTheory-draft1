  (`Etingof.simpleReflection`, Def 6.4.10) of the Cartan matrix A = 2·Id − adj
  (Def 6.4.1) acting on the root lattice ℤⁿ. Now imports Definition6_4_1 and
  Definition6_4_10 instead of just Mathlib.

  coxeterElement` so the proofs still see the underlying foldr.
