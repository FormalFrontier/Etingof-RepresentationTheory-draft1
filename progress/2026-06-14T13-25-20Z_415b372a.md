### Key design decision

Both helpers are stated over **explicit center/arm submodules**
(`W0 : Submodule F (Fin (6*(m+1)) → F)`, etc.) rather than the rep family
`W : ∀ v, Submodule F ((t125Rep_kQ …).obj v)`. Stating them against the rep
made `Membership` synthesis fail in the signature (the `obj`/`t125Dim` match
does not reduce under the `attribute [-instance]` pragma). The explicit-block
form elaborates cleanly, is reusable by the Ẽ₆/Ẽ₇ prefix/suffix flags, and
call sites just pass `W ⟨0⟩`, `W ⟨2⟩`, … (the block dims match `t125Dim` by
defeq at the call site, where the expected type is known).
