## Accomplished

Worked issue #7882 (Problem 8.2.10(i), exactness of the Koszul complex). Landed the first of
three pieces and decomposed the rest.

New module `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter8/KoszulBasis.lean` puts the Koszul complex into
coordinates, which is what any characteristic-free exactness proof needs:

* `Etingof.finsetPos s a = #{c ∈ s | c < a}` with `Etingof.orderEmbOfFin_finsetPos`, saying it is
  the index of `a` in the increasing listing of `s`, and `Etingof.orderEmbOfFin_erase`, saying
  that deleting that index gives the increasing listing of `s.erase a`.
* `Etingof.exteriorContraction_basis_of_notMem` / `Etingof.exteriorContraction_basis_of_mem` —
  the contraction `ι_{xₐ*}` on the wedge basis of `⋀ⁱ V`: zero when `a ∉ s`, and
  `(-1)^(finsetPos s a + 1) e_(s \ {a})` when `a ∈ s`.
* `Etingof.koszulKBasis k V b i` — the `k`-basis `x^α ⊗ e_s` of `Cᵢ = SV ⊗[k] ⋀ⁱ V`, the tensor
  product of Mathlib's `Module.Basis.symmetricAlgebra` (monomials) and
  `Module.Basis.exteriorPower` (subsets).
* `Etingof.ι_mul_symmetricAlgebra_basis` — multiplying a monomial basis vector by `xₐ` bumps the
  exponent, via `SymmetricAlgebra.equivMvPolynomial`.
* `Etingof.koszulD_koszulKBasis` — the differential in coordinates,
  `d (x^α ⊗ e_s) = ∑_{a ∈ s} (-1)^(finsetPos s a + 1) x^(α + eₐ) ⊗ e_(s \ {a})`.
* `Etingof.koszulAug_koszulKBasis` — `ε (x^α ⊗ 1) = if α = 0 then 1 else 0`.

## Current frontier

The route was chosen deliberately. The issue offered two options; the regular-sequence/induction
route needs mapping cones, base change of resolutions along `SV' → SV`, a long exact sequence,
and an identification of `koszulComplex` with an iterated cone — none of which the repo has. The
route taken instead is the algebraic Morse matching: an explicit characteristic-free contracting
homotopy on the monomial/subset basis,

`h (x^α ⊗ e_s) = -(x^(α - e_p) ⊗ e_(insert p s))` when `p = min supp α` is strictly below every
element of `s`, and `0` otherwise.

Its correctness was checked by hand on six small cases (recorded in the triage comment on #7882)
and the general two-case argument is in #7911. Everything it needs is `Finset`/`Finsupp`
bookkeeping, so it does not wait on missing infrastructure.
