## Accomplished

Closed out **Problem 4.12.2(d)** (`irreducible_dim`, issue #6094): every irreducible complex
representation of the Heisenberg group `Heisenberg p` has dimension `1` or `p`. The file
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Problem4_12_2.lean` is now sorry-free;
`#print axioms Etingof.Problem4_12_2.irreducible_dim` = `{propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}`.

Proof outline (follows the book's sum-of-squares argument):
- Added helper lemmas: `rhoHom_central` (`ρ_z(⟨0,0,1⟩) = z^{(-1).val}·id`), `finrank_rhoHom`
  (`dim R_z = p`), `character_rhoHom_central` (`χ_{R_z}(⟨0,0,1⟩) = z^{(-1).val}·p`),
  `powNegOneVal_inj` (`w ↦ w^{(-1).val}` injective on `p`-th roots, since it is `w ↦ w⁻¹`),
  and a general pigeonhole `surj_of_injective_of_sum_eq` (an injection into `Fin n` whose image
  carries the full positive-weight sum is surjective).
- Exhibited family indexed by `(G →* ℂˣ) ⊕ {k : ZMod p // k ≠ 0}`: the `p²` characters
  (`FDRep.of (charRep χ)`, simple via `charRep_simple`, dim 1) and the `p-1` reps
  `R_{ζ^k}` (`ζ` a primitive `p`-th root, simple via part (b) `irreducible_iff` +
  `simple_fdRepOf_of_isSimpleModule`, dim `p`).
- Pairwise non-isomorphism via `FDRep.char_iso`: characters separated as homs (equal traces
  give equal `χ`); `R_z` separated from characters by dimension; `R_z` separated from each other
  by the central character `z^{(-1).val}·p`.
- `∑ dim² = p²·1 + (p-1)·p² = p³ = |G|`; injecting into `exists_simples_sum_finrank_sq_eq_card`
  and using positivity of every `dim²` forces the injection surjective, so every enumerated
  simple (hence any simple `σ`) is `≅` a family member and has dim `1` or `p`.
- Universe handling: the theorem's `W : Type*` is transported into `ℂ`'s universe with the
  `transportModule`/`repOfModule` machinery from `Infrastructure/SimpleModuleCount.lean`.
