Statement: for `V : FDRep ℂ G` with `G` finite, there is `q : ℚ` with
`algebraMap ℚ ℂ q = ∏_{g ≠ 1} χ_V(g)·χ_V(g⁻¹)` — i.e. `β` is rational.

Proof architecture (avoids extending automorphisms to ring endos of ℂ):
- Realize `K = ℚ(ζ_N)` as the intermediate field `ℚ⟮ζ⟯ ⊆ ℂ` for `ζ = e^{2πi/N}`
  (`Complex.isPrimitiveRoot_exp`), with `IsCyclotomicExtension {N} ℚ K` (via
  `IsPrimitiveRoot.adjoin_isCyclotomicExtension` after
  `IntermediateField.adjoin_simple_toSubalgebra_of_isAlgebraic`), hence
  `IsGalois ℚ K` and `FiniteDimensional ℚ K`.
- Lift `β` to `β_K ∈ K`: for each `g`, build `x g ∈ K` as the sum over the
  eigenvalue finset `∑_μ (dim Eμ) · μ_K` (each eigenvalue `μ` is an `N`-th root
  of unity, hence in `K`), reusing `trace_pow_eq_sum_eigenvalues`. Two facts:
  `algebraMap K ℂ (x g) = χ_V(g)`, and for any `φ` powering roots of unity to
  the `j`-th, `algebraMap K ℂ (φ (x g)) = χ_V(gʲ)`.
- Fixed by all of `Gal(K/ℚ)`: each `φ` acts on roots of unity as `ζ ↦ ζʲ`
  (`IsPrimitiveRoot.autToPow_spec`), so `φ (x g) = x (gʲ)` (injectivity of
  `algebraMap K ℂ`), and reindexing along `g ↦ gʲ` (`prod_ne_one_pow`, with `j`
  coprime to `N` via `ZMod.val_coe_unit_coprime`) gives `φ β_K = β_K`.
- Conclude `β_K ∈ range (algebraMap ℚ K)` from
  `IsGalois.mem_range_algebraMap_iff_fixed`, then push to ℂ via
  `IsScalarTower.algebraMap_apply`.
