Key design decision: built the eigenspaces from the **ambient** `Zamb` on `W4 ⊗ W4` (one

subtype-of-subtype carrier `↥(eigenspace zEnd μ)` triggered an `AddCommGroup` typeclass
diamond that `FDRep.of` could not resolve even at 400k synth heartbeats; the ambient
intersection `range asym ⊓ eigenspace Zamb μ` is a plain submodule of `W4 ⊗ W4` and `FDRep.of`
accepts it directly (same shape that worked for `lam2`).
