    eval (fun _ => (1:ℚ)) (schurPoly N ν.parts) * coeff μ (schurPoly N ν.parts)

### Proof structure (all assembled from existing sorry-free infrastructure)

- `factorial_mul_sum_eq_sum_perm` — orthogonality inversion: `d!·∑ᵥ Fν Gν =
  ∑_{σ∈S_d} (∑ᵥ χᵥ(σ)Fν)(∑ᵥ χᵥ(σ)Gν)`, using `charValue_row_orthogonality`
  (already proven in `CharacterOrthogonality.lean` — the flagged-hard piece was
  already done; summing over permutations gives the z_μ-weighted column
  orthogonality automatically).
- `eval_one_powerSumCycleProduct`, `coeff_powerSumCycleProduct_schur` — Frobenius
  change of basis (`Proposition5_21_1_univ`) applied to the functionals `eval 1`
  and `coeff μ`.
- `eval_one_eq_sum_coeff` — `eval 1 (P_σ) = ∑_β coeff β (P_σ)` over the degree-`d`
  monomials (homogeneity via `psumPart_isHomogeneous`).
- `sum_card_NNMatrixGen_eq_prod_choose` — the stars-and-bars matrix count:
  `∑_β #{N×N matrices, rows β, cols μ} = ∏_j C(μ_j+N−1,N−1)`. Proved via a
  fiberwise sum over row margins (`Finset.card_eq_sum_card_fiberwise`) collapsing
  to column-constrained matrices, factored over columns, with the per-column
  count `card_boundedFun_sum_eq_choose` (= `Sym.card_sym_eq_choose`).
- The power-sum Cauchy bridge (`powerSum_bilinear_coeff_gen` +
  `fullCauchyProd_coeff_eq_card_gen`) connects the σ-sum to the matrix count.
