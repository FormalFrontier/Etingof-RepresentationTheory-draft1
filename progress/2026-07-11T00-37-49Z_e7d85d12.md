block-orthogonality theorem plus reusable composition-factor infrastructure in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter9/Problem9_5_3.lean`, all sorry-free and axiom-clean
(`#print axioms` shows only `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`):

- `Etingof.isCompositionFactor_iff` — a composition factor is exactly a simple quotient of a
  submodule (`∃ P ≤ M, ∃ surjection ↥P → S`). This reformulation avoids quotient-of-quotient
  gymnastics.
- `Etingof.IsCompositionFactor.of_submodule` / `.of_surjective` — composition factors transfer
  through submodules (mono) and surjective images (epi).
- `Etingof.exists_isCompositionFactor` — every nonzero module has a composition factor
  (nonzero element → cyclic submodule → maximal left ideal above its annihilator → simple
  quotient, shrunk into universe `v` via `Small.{v} R`).
- `Etingof.Problem953.hom_subsingleton_of_not_linked` (**deliverable 1/2**) — `Hom(M,N)=0`
  when `M, N` lie in different blocks: any composition factor of `range f.hom` links to both
  `S` (through `M`) and `T` (through `N`), forcing `AreLinked S T`, contradiction.
