## Accomplished

Closed the Wave-2 fidelity gap flagged in issue #5656 for
`Chapter4/Theorem4.1.1` (Maschke). The book's part (ii) asserts three things:
(1) an algebra isomorphism `ψ : k[G] → ⊕ᵢ End(Vᵢ)`; (2) that `ψ` is also a
representation isomorphism (regular rep `≅ ⊕ᵢ dim(Vᵢ)·Vᵢ`); (3) the
sum-of-squares formula `|G| = Σᵢ (dim Vᵢ)²`. The formalized
`Theorem4_1_1_sum_of_squares` only surfaced (3).
