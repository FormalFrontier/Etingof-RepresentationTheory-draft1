## Accomplished

Feature session on **#6859** (Ch9 #Problem9.5.3-iii): proved `simple_iff_triv_or_std`
sorry-free in `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter9/Problem9_5_3_S3Char2.lean`
(axioms = `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`). Every simple
`S : ModuleCat.{0} (k[S₃])` over a field `k` of characteristic `2` is `≅ trivMod k`
or `≅ stdMod k`.

The proof is fully elementary (no Brauer theory, no `M₂(k)`-module classification, no
`algebra_decomposition` dependency):

- The class sum `e = (123)+(132)` (`eStdCI`, already in-file) is a central idempotent, so
  by Schur (`centralIdempotent_smul_simple`) it acts on `S` as `0` or as the identity.
- **`e` acts as `0`:** deduce the `3`-cycle `c` acts as the identity on all of `S`
  (`c·(1+e) = 1+e`, and `(1+e)` acts as `id`). A nonzero `tsw`-fixed vector `u`
  (`tsw = (0 1)`, an involution with `(tsw-1)² = 0` in char 2) is then fixed by every
  group element, giving a nonzero intertwiner `trivMod → S`, `t ↦ t·u`.
- **`e` acts as identity:** then `c² + c + 1 = 0` on `S`. A nonzero `tsw`-fixed `u` yields
  the intertwiner `stdMod → S` sending the sum-zero coordinates `(v 0, v 2)` of `v` to
  `v 0 · u + v 2 · (c·u)`; equivariance under `c` and `tsw` reduces to `c²+c+1=0`, the
  relation `tsw·c = c²·tsw`, and sum-zero (`v 0 + v 1 + v 2 = 0`).
- A reusable private lemma `nonempty_iso_of_genEquivariant` promotes a nonzero `k`-linear
  map intertwining the two generators `thc`/`tsw` to a `k[S₃]`-linear map (via
  `MonoidAlgebra.induction_on`, propagating equivariance from generators to all of `S₃`
  by an explicit 6-element enumeration of `S₃`), then invokes `LinearMap.bijective_of_ne_zero`
  (Schur) and `LinearEquiv.toModuleIso`.

Key infrastructure discovered/used: the abstract carrier `↥S` of a `ModuleCat (k[S₃])`
object is equipped with a `k`-module via `Module.compHom (algebraMap k (k[S₃]))` plus
hand-built `IsScalarTower`/`SMulCommClass`; `Representation.asModuleEquiv_map_smul` +
`asAlgebraHom_single_one` give the coordinate action; `∀ g : S₃, g = ...` (6-way) is
`decide`-able but must be stated with `g` universally quantified (not as a free variable).
