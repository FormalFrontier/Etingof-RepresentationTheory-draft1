### Two things worth recording

**Do not compute the kernel; build the inverse.** The obvious route to `algEquiv` is
`aeval : ℂ[x,y] → A` plus `ker = I₂`, and the hard direction (`ker ≤ I₂`) needs "every polynomial
with vanishing degree-`0` and degree-`1` coefficients lies in `I₂`" — real `MvPolynomial` coefficient
work. Going the other way costs almost nothing: `TrivSqZeroExt.liftEquivOfComm` builds
`A →ₐ[ℂ] ℂ[x,y]/I₂` from a linear map `(Fin 2 → ℂ) → ℂ[x,y]/I₂` with vanishing products, and the
vanishing is immediate because each `X i * X j` is a *generator* of `I₂`. Then the two round trips
are pure `ext` lemmas: `TrivSqZeroExt.algHom_ext` one way, `Ideal.Quotient.algHom_ext` composed with
`MvPolynomial.algHom_ext` the other. No coefficient manipulation anywhere.

**One computation gives both halves of the problem.** `PartC.smul_eq`:

```
a • f = a.fst • f + f (inr a.snd) • ε
```

where `ε : A^*` reads off the coefficient of `1`. Read left to right it says `A • f ⊆ ℂf + ℂε`, a
plane in the three-dimensional `A^*`, so no `f` is cyclic. Read at `a` in the maximal ideal
(`a.fst = 0`) it says `a • f = f(a) • ε`, so the image of `m` on `V` is exactly the line `ℂε`, hence
every nonzero submodule contains `ε` and two complementary submodules would both contain it. The
socle being a *line* is the whole content of indecomposability here; no Fitting/endomorphism-ring
machinery is needed. Non-cyclicity is proved by exhibiting `ℂf + ℂε` as
`LinearMap.range (plane f)` for `plane f : ℂ × ℂ →ₗ[ℂ] V`, which makes
`LinearMap.finrank_range_le` do the dimension count directly.
