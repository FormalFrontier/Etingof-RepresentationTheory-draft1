## Accomplished

Formalized the characteristic-`p` footnote to Theorem 3.7.1 (issue #7469): "the character
of the representation `pV`, where `V` is any representation, is zero."

New file `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Discussion_footnote_3_7_1.lean`:

- `Etingof.diagPi` — the coordinatewise ("diagonal") endomorphism of `ι → V` induced by
  `f : V →ₗ[k] V`, `g ↦ fun i => f (g i)`. For `f = a • ·` this is exactly the pointwise
  `A`-action on the direct sum `ι → V`.
- `Etingof.trace_diagPi_fin` — `trace (Fin n → V) (diagPi f) = n • trace V f`, proved by
  induction on `n`, splitting `Fin (n+1) → V ≃ₗ V × (Fin n → V)` via `Fin.consLinearEquiv`
  and applying `LinearMap.trace_prodMap'` (direct-sum additivity of the trace) together with
  `LinearMap.trace_conj'`.
- `Etingof.character_fin_pi` — general form `χ_{nV} a = n • χ_V a`, obtained by identifying the
  action endomorphism of `Fin n → V` with `diagPi` of the action endomorphism of `V`.
- `Etingof.character_pcopies_eq_zero` — the footnote proper: `χ_{pV} = 0` under `[CharP k p]`,
  via `nsmul_eq_mul` and `CharP.cast_eq_zero`.
