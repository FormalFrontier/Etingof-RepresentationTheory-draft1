## Accomplished

Triaged issue #5474 (discharge the `sorry` in `Chapter8/Example8_1_7.lean`, the full
projective↔injective-dual biconditional). Determined an honest proof is infeasible in a
single session because it requires two pieces of homological infrastructure absent from
Mathlib, and decomposed it into well-scoped infrastructure sub-issues rather than
relocating the `sorry`.

Investigation findings:
* The Lean statement is the general biconditional `Module.Projective A P ↔
  Module.Injective Aᵐᵒᵖ (Module.Dual k P)` over a finite-dimensional algebra, for an
  **arbitrary** module `P` (not finitely generated). The module docstring (from #5417)
  commits to this general form and is correct.
* Mathlib has Lambek only for the **character** module (`Module.Flat.iff_characterModule_injective`,
  cogenerator ℚ/ℤ over ℤ) — not for the k-linear dual with the contragredient action.
* Mathlib has flat ⟹ projective only for **finitely presented** modules
  (`Module.Flat.projective_of_finitePresentation`); it has **no** perfect-ring / Bass
  theorem, which is what covers arbitrary modules over a finite-dimensional algebra.
* Building blocks for the Lambek k-dual direction exist
  (`Module.Flat.lTensor_preserves_injective_linearMap`, `LinearMap.exists_extend` over a
  field) but the tensor–hom adjunction `Hom_{Aᵐᵒᵖ}(X, Hom_k(P,k)) ≅ Hom_k(X ⊗_A P, k)`
  must be constructed.
* The project's own Theorems 8.1.1/8.1.5 only restate Mathlib's projective/injective
  characterizations — they do not supply the duality.

Created:
* #5475 — Lambek for the k-linear dual: `Module.Flat A P ↔ Module.Injective Aᵐᵒᵖ (Module.Dual k P)`.
* #5476 — flat ⟹ projective over a finite-dimensional algebra (Bass / perfect rings).

Marked #5474 as `depends-on: #5475, #5476` (now `blocked`), left a `Decomposed into …`
breadcrumb, and `coordination skip`ped it (→ `replan`). It remains as the trivial
assembly step once both infra issues land.
