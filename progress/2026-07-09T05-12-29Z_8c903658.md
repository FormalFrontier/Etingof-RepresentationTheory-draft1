### Implementation note for successors

The extra `bcModRestrict : Module A (L ⊗[K] V)` instance perturbs typeclass search: with it
in scope, elaborating `rep`/`repTensor` triggers a slow/looping `Algebra K (End L (L ⊗ V))`
synth, and `TensorProduct.comm K L V` picks an inconsistent `Semiring K` path (a
`Field.toSemifield` diamond) that fails to unify at `≪≫ₗ`. The fix used here: prove
`repTensor_one_tmul_apply`, `toPiLinearEquiv`, and `toPiLinearEquiv_tmul` *before* declaring
the instance. Also, building an `A`-`LinearEquiv` by reusing the `K`-equiv's `AddEquiv`
(`{ e.toAddEquiv with map_smul' := … }`) fails with a `bcModRestrict` vs
`TensorProduct.instModule` mismatch; build a fresh `→ₗ[A]` map and use
`LinearEquiv.ofBijective` instead.
