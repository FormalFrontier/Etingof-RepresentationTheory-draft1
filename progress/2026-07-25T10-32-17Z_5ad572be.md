## Current frontier

Design note for successors: the book states Remark 6.4.11 for operators on `ℝⁿ`, but every
other Section 6.4/6.7 declaration in this project (roots, simple roots, reflections, Coxeter
elements) lives on the root lattice `ℤⁿ`, so `weylGroup` does too. The module docstring
records why nothing is lost: the simple roots are simultaneously a `ℤ`-basis of `ℤⁿ` and an
`ℝ`-basis of `ℝⁿ`, so restriction to the lattice is injective on the subgroup generated by
the `sᵢ` — and that is also exactly what makes the finiteness argument work.
