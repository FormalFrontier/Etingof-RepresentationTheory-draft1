### The breakthrough — a clean, fully-classical proof

All prior sessions (4+) and two AI second opinions had concluded fact 1 was a
multi-session "James/Fulton column-straightening" nut requiring a new
sign-reversing involution on column-non-standard fillings. That framing was
aimed at the *residual* `X = f_w − M`. For `f_w(σ)` **itself**, the right
mechanism is the classical **von Neumann / Young dichotomy**, which is
all-or-nothing and exactly sufficient:

Computational validation first (mandated; scripts in `/tmp`, 8 shapes incl.
(3,3),(4,2),(3,2,1),(2,2,2), zero violations):
* `f_w(σ) ≠ 0  ⟺  w ∈ P_λ·Q_λ  ⟺  coeff of [σ] in f_w(σ) is ≠ 0` (always `±1`);
* von Neumann dichotomy: for every `w`, *either* two same-column positions map
  under `w` to the same row (giving an odd transposition `(a b) ∈ Q_λ` with
  `w(a b)w⁻¹ ∈ P_λ`), *or* `w ∈ P_λ·Q_λ` — mutually exclusive.

Proof of fact 1:
* If `w ∉ P_λ·Q_λ`: the odd column element `h := w⁻¹ t w ∈ Q_λ` drives a
  fixed-point-free sign-reversing involution `q ↦ q·h` on `Q_λ` that preserves
  every tabloid `[w q⁻¹ σ]`; the defining sum equals its own negation, so
  `f_w(σ) = 0` and the claim is vacuous.
* If `w = p·q₀ ∈ P_λ·Q_λ`: the fibre over `[σ]` is the singleton `{q₀}`
  (because `P_λ ∩ Q_λ = {1}`), so `[σ]` occurs with coefficient `sign(q₀) = ±1`.
  With the existing `twistedPolytabloid_support_bound` (all support `⊴ [σ]`),
  `[σ]` is the unique dominance-maximal support tabloid — and `σ` is
  column-standard.
