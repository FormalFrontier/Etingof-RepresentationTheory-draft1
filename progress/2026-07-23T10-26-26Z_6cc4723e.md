representation of a `p`-group over a field of characteristic `p` is trivial.

- Root cause: the `key` sublemma inside `Etingof.Problem4_1_4` used
  `Finsupp.induction_linear` on `r : MonoidAlgebra k G`. A Mathlib update made
  the recursor produce terms typed as `G →₀ k` rather than the definitionally
  equal `k[G]`, so `map_zero` / `map_add` against `ρ.asAlgebraHom` (which expects
  `k[G]`) no longer matched. This left unsolved goals at lines 172 and 176.
- Fix: replaced the induction with `MonoidAlgebra.induction_on`, whose `hM` /
  `hadd` / `hsmul` cases carry properly-typed `k[G]` terms. Rewrote each case
  (single-basis, additive, scalar) to close cleanly.
- Added `Chapter4/Problem4_1_4_Test.lean`: a downstream import that pins the
  public signature (`#check @Etingof.Problem4_1_4`) and applies the theorem in an
  `example`, forcing a fresh re-elaboration of the endpoint. Registered it in
  `Chapter4.lean`.
- Verified: `lake env lean` direct-checks both the source and the test with no
  errors; `lake build …Problem4_1_4_Test` succeeds (8581 jobs) with no warnings.

No `sorry`, `admit`, or replacement axiom. Public conclusion
`∀ g, ρ g = LinearMap.id` and hypotheses unchanged.
