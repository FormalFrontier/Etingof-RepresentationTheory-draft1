## Accomplished

Resolved fidelity-sweep finding #5366 (Chapter2/Remark2.11.4 — tensor product
over a ring). Re-confirmed the coverage gap: the remark generalizes the tensor
product to `V ⊗_A W` for an arbitrary (possibly noncommutative) ring `A`, with
`V` a right `A`-module and `W` a left `A`-module, via the balancing relation
`v a ⊗ w = v ⊗ a w`. Mathlib's `TensorProduct` requires a `CommSemiring`, so
this balanced module tensor product is genuinely absent — the finding was a
true positive, not a false positive.

Formalized it directly from the book's description in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter2/Remark2_11_4.lean`:

- `Etingof.tensorRelations A V W` — the set of defining relations (additivity in
  each variable plus the balancing relation), following Mathlib's convention
  that a right `A`-module is a left `Aᵐᵒᵖ`-module (so `v a = op a • v`).
- `Etingof.TensorProductOverRing A V W` — the quotient of
  `FreeAbelianGroup (V × W)` by `AddSubgroup.closure (tensorRelations A V W)`,
  with its inherited `AddCommGroup` instance.
- `tmul A v w` — the elementary tensor (class of `FreeAbelianGroup.of (v, w)`),
  with the three book relations discharged as `add_tmul`, `tmul_add`, and
  `smul_tmul` (the balancing relation).

Registered the file in `Chapter2.lean` and set `fidelity: verified` (with
`fidelity_decl: Etingof.TensorProductOverRing`) for the item in
`progress/items.json`.
