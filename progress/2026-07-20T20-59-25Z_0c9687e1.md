Key technical note: real irreducibility does **not** formally imply complex irreducibility (that
is the *real type* content), so the combinatorial sign-averaging/rotation argument had to be
redone over ℂ. This was done by transporting each pointwise `conjRep R w = ± w'` fact through
`cx` and copying the two proof bodies with `ℝ → ℂ` scalar substitutions. Two ℂ-specific
adaptations were required: (1) `linarith` is unavailable over the unordered field ℂ, replaced by
`linear_combination` / `exact` in the decomposition lemmas; (2) `ring`/`linear_combination` treat
`fin_cases`-produced `Fin.mk` indices as distinct atoms from `OfNat` indices, so the decomposition
closers use `exact hd i` / `exact ho i j` / `exact htrace` (defeq-based) rather than
`linear_combination`.
