## Accomplished

Feature session on **#4612** (T(1,2,5) = Ẽ₈ orientation-generic
leaf-equality case tree for `t125Rep_kQ`, sub-B of #4559). On orientation
I found the issue is **substantially larger than one session and lacks any
landed structural precedent**:

- The construction (`FieldGenericT125.lean`) is structurally novel: a
  6-block center `F^{6(m+1)}` with an arm-2 *prefix* flag, an arm-3 *suffix*
  flag (5 hops), and an arm-1 eigenvalue *3-plane* tube — not the two-leaf
  star (`starEmbed1/2_F` + `d5tildeGamma_F`) that the landed D̃₅/D̃₆/D̃₇
  `leaf_equalities` proofs are built on.

  still open**: Ẽ₆ = T(2,2,2) (#4576), Ẽ₇ = T(1,3,3) (#4569). None landed,
  so there is no template to mirror and no flag-collapse infrastructure to
  reuse. The D̃ precedents themselves carry 9–13 deferred `sorry`s in their
  non-canonical orientation branches.

So I built the **orientation-independent foundation** (sub-B1 down-payment)
that any version of the collapse needs, sorry-free:

- `prefixBlockProj_comp_embed_F`, `suffixBlockProj_comp_embed_F`,
  `blockProjAtN_comp_embed_F`: the flag/single-block projections are left
  inverses of their embeddings (the step that extracts a leaf component
  from a center membership).
- `prefixBlockEmbed_injective_F`, `suffixBlockEmbed_injective_F`.
- `prefixBlockEmbed_comp_F`, `suffixBlockEmbed_comp_F`: each flag is a single
  block embedding (composition laws).

pre-existing `t125Rep_kQ_isIndecomposable` stub.
