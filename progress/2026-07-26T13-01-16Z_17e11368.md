* `Etingof.jacobson_le_radTensorSum` — the hard inclusion, with semisimplicity of
  `(A/Rad A) ⊗ (B/Rad B)` as an explicit hypothesis (no finiteness needed).
* `Etingof.isSemisimpleRing_tensorProduct` — that hypothesis discharged over an
  algebraically closed field.
* `Etingof.jacobson_tensorProduct_eq`, `Etingof.quotientJacobsonTensorEquiv` — the book's
  two displayed identities, unconditional for finite-dimensional `A`, `B` over

  `A'`, `B' ⊆ End M`, i.e. under exactly the hypotheses Theorem 3.10.2 carries.
