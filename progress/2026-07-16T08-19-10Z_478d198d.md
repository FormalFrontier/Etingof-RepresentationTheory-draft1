## Accomplished
Closed the `isAffineDynkinDiagram_of_type` sorry in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter6/Problem6_1_3_continued_tildeE.lean`
(issue #6762) — each extended diagram `Ãₙ, D̃ₙ, Ẽ₆, Ẽ₇, Ẽ₈` is proved to be an
affine Dynkin diagram (all six `IsAffineDynkinDiagram` clauses). Sorry-free
(axioms: `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`).

Key pieces added (all reusable):
- `posSemidef_of_nonpos_offDiag_kernel` — the crux: a symmetric integer matrix
  with nonpositive off-diagonal entries that annihilates a strictly-positive
  vector is positive semidefinite. Proof is the weighted-Laplacian
  sum-of-squares identity `2·xᵀAx = ∑ (-Aᵢⱼ) mᵢ mⱼ (xᵢ/mᵢ − xⱼ/mⱼ)²`, worked
  over `ℚ` then cast back to `ℤ`. The "row-sum against `m` vanishes" step kills
  the `yᵢ²` sums entirely, so no diagonal/off-diagonal split is needed.
- Structural clauses `AffineType.adj_isSymm / adj_diag / adj_zero_or_one`
  (symmetry, no self-loops, `0/1` entries) by per-type case split.
- Connectivity via `Relation.ReflTransGen` of the edge relation `AdjEdge`:
  `clause_of_reflTransGen` converts reflexive-transitive reachability into the
  explicit `List.get`-indexed edge-path clause (using
  `List.exists_isChain_ne_nil_of_relationReflTransGen` + `isChain_iff_getElem`);
  `reflTransGen_symm` + `connected_of_reach_base` reduce full connectivity to
  reach-from-a-base. Per type: `Ãₙ` by a cycle induction, `D̃ₙ` by a central
  chain induction plus explicit leaf/branch edges, `Ẽ₆/Ẽ₇/Ẽ₈` by explicit
  reach chains (skeleton via `refine`, edges by `decide`).
