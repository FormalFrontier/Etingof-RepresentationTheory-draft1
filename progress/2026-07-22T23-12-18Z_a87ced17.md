## Decisions / patterns

  (`simp [evalMap]`) no longer closed — simp unfolded `Basis.constr` into a
  Finsupp sum. Replaced with an explicit `rw [evalMap_apply]; exact b.constr_basis k g i`.

  only in the proof, not the statement); silenced locally with
  `set_option linter.unusedFintypeInType false in`.
