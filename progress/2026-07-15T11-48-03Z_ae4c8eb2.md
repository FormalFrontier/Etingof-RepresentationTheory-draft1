## Key pattern discovered

`cartan_det` macro — computing an explicit `n×n` sparse integer determinant without
the `n!` blowup. Stage 1 `simp only [Matrix.det_succ_row_zero, …, Fin.succ_succAbove_succ,
Fin.succ_succAbove_zero, Fin.zero_succAbove, Matrix.cons_val_succ, mul_zero, zero_mul, …]`
reduces `succAbove` *symbolically* in `0`/`.succ` form (no `Fin.lt_def`/`if` `isDefEq`
cost) so `zero_mul` prunes the zero cofactors before the expansion explodes; Stage 2
`<;> norm_num [Fin.succAbove, Fin.lt_def, Fin.castSucc, Matrix.cons_val_*, …]` cleans the
small residual. Documented as gotcha #6 in `.claude/skills/lean-formalization/SKILL.md`.
Anti-patterns confirmed: `decide` on `Matrix.det` (maxRecDepth), single combined
`simp` with `Fin.succAbove` unfold (isDefEq timeout, 4 min), plain
`simp [det_succ_row_zero]` (maxSteps / leftover `succAbove`).
