## Accomplished

Worked issue #7639 ("Prove finite length of FGModuleCat A objects in both encodings and
bridge them"). Landed **two of the three deliverables** as sorry-free, tested infrastructure
files:

1. `Infrastructure/FGModuleCatFiniteLength.lean` — `Etingof.hasFiniteLength_fgModuleCat`:
   every `X : FGModuleCat A` over a Noetherian+Artinian ring satisfies the categorical
   `Etingof.HasFiniteLength X`. Proved by inducting on Mathlib's `IsFiniteLength` of the
   underlying module and feeding the categorical short exact sequence `0 → N → M → M⧸N → 0`
   (`Etingof.submoduleSES`, short-exact via the fully faithful homology-preserving inclusion
   into `ModuleCat`, `ShortComplex.exact_map_iff_of_faithful`) to
   `HasFiniteLength.of_shortExact`, with the simple quotient handled by the existing
   `simple_fgModuleCat_of_isSimpleModule` bridge. Supplies the `finiteLength` field of
   `IsFiniteAbelianCategoryOverField k (FGModuleCat A)`.

2. `Infrastructure/FGModuleCatSubobjectFiniteDimensional.lean` —
   `Etingof.finiteDimensionalOrder_subobject_fgModuleCat`: `FiniteDimensionalOrder (Subobject X)`
   for the same `X`. Built the order embedding `Subobject X ↪o Subobject (incl X)`
   (`Etingof.subobjectInclOrderEmbedding`, via the fully faithful mono-preserving inclusion),
   composed with `ModuleCat.subobjectModule` to get a strictly monotone map into
   `Submodule A X`, and transported the submodule lattice's finite Krull dimension
   (`RingTheory/Length`) back via `Order.krullDim_le_of_strictMono`. Supplies the
   `finiteDimensionalOrder_subobject` field of `IsFiniteAbelianCategory (FGModuleCat A)`.

Each has a `_Test.lean` with a `#guard_msgs #print axioms` check (only `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). Both registered in `EtingofRepresentationTheory.lean`.

## Current frontier

The third deliverable — the **general abelian-category bridge** relating the two encodings
(`HasFiniteLength X → FiniteDimensionalOrder (Subobject X)`, and/or the converse) — was split
out to a fresh sub-issue. Its hard step is length-additivity of the subobject lattice across a
short exact sequence (`of_shortExact`), which needs pullback/image chain-bounding in a general
abelian category and is a substantial standalone piece.
