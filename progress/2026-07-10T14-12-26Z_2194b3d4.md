## Accomplished

Discharged the single `sorry` in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Problem3_8_4_Descent.lean` (issue #6158):
`exists_fg_subalgebra_baseChange_iso` is now proved sorry-free. Given an
`L ⊗[K] A`-linear iso `e : L ⊗[K] V ≃ L ⊗[K] W` (with `V`, `W` finite dimensional
over `K`), it produces a finitely generated `K`-subalgebra `R ⊆ L` carrying an
`R ⊗[K] A`-linear iso `R ⊗[K] V ≃ R ⊗[K] W`.

Proof structure (follows the 7-step route in the file docstring):
- `K`-bases `bV`, `bW` (`Module.finBasis`) base-changed to `L`; coordinates
  `c i j`, `d j i` of `e`, `e.symm` over those bases.
- `R := Algebra.adjoin K {entries}` (`Subalgebra.fg_adjoin_finset`), introduced
  **opaquely via `obtain`** so later `isDefEq`/`whnf` never unfold the adjoin.
- Descended maps `φ`, `ψ` via `Basis.constr` over `↥R`.
- Coefficient-inclusion maps `incV`, `incW := LinearMap.rTensor _ R.val.toLinearMap`,
  injective by flatness (`Module.Flat.rTensor_preserves_injective_linearMap`,
  `V`/`W` free over the field `K`), `↥R`-semilinear along `R.val`, and `A`-equivariant.
- Two intertwining identities `incW ∘ φ = e ∘ incV` and `incV ∘ ψ = e.symm ∘ incW`,
  proved on the `↥R`-basis and extended by `Basis.sum_repr`. These deliver, via
  injectivity of `incV`/`incW`, both the inverse relations (`ψ∘φ = id`, `φ∘ψ = id`)
  and the `A`-equivariance of `φ`, delegating all the arithmetic to `e`.
- Package `φ` as the required `↥R ⊗[K] A`-linear equivalence (the `map_smul'`
  generator argument mirrors `Functoriality.pushEquiv`).

## Key patterns discovered

- The decisive performance fix: a subalgebra defined as `Algebra.adjoin K {…}` must
  be introduced as an **opaque** hypothesis (`obtain ⟨R, …⟩ : ∃ R, …`), not a
  transparent `let`. As a `let`, every unification tried to unfold the adjoin,
  producing `whnf`/`isDefEq` heartbeat timeouts everywhere downstream.
- Delegating to `e` through injective coefficient-inclusion maps avoids ever having
  to compute `φ`/`ψ` on general (non-basis) vectors.
- The proof needs `set_option maxHeartbeats 800000` (with the required explanatory
  comment) — it is a genuinely large single-theorem elaboration.
