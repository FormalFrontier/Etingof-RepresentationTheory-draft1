## Accomplished

Proved `isSimpleRing_of_not_isOfFinOrder` (Problem 2.7.5(a), simplicity) sorry-free in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter2/Problem2_7_5.lean` (issue #6202), via the book's

New private infrastructure added to `namespace Etingof.Problem2_7_5`:
- Generator/monomial elements `gX, gX', gY, gY', gMono` (as `qWeylAlgebra` elements) with
  `@[simp]` coe lemmas.
- `kappa a := ↑a (single (0,0) 1)` — the coefficient function; `kappa_eq_zero_iff`,
  `kappa_sub`, `kappa_smul` for its linear behaviour.
- `kappa_conjX` / `kappa_conjY`: conjugating by `x` (resp. `y`) scales the coefficient at `r`
  by `q^{-r.2}` (resp. `q^{r.1}`). Proved by expanding `↑a` in the monomial basis and reusing
  the file's `finsupp_sum_single_emb_apply`.

The main proof: `IsSimpleRing.of_eq_bot_or_eq_top`, then strong induction on the support size
of `kappa` of a nonzero ideal element. Card 1 ⇒ the element is a scalar times a monomial, which
is a unit (`smul_mul_smul_comm` + `qMono_mul`), so `1 ∈ I` and `I = ⊤`. Card ≥ 2 ⇒ pick two
support points differing in some coordinate, conjugate by the matching generator and subtract a
scalar multiple to kill one point while keeping the other (nonzero because `q` is not a root of
unity, via `injective_zpow_iff_not_isOfFinOrder`), landing a strictly-smaller-support element of
`I` and invoking the induction hypothesis.
