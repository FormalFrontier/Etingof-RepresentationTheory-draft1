## Accomplished

Fixed the left factor of `localBiRep` (issue #5536). The localization left action
`localLeftAlgHom`/`localLeftRep` in `Chapter5/LocalizationGLBiAction.lean` previously
extended `polyLeftRep` (the transpose action `φ ↦ φ(gᵀ ·)`), giving the wrong
`GL × GL`-structure `⊕_λ L_λ ⊗ L_λ` instead of Etingof's `⊕_λ L*_λ ⊗ L_λ`.

Changes:
- Added `glInvTransposeFun` / `glInvTranspose : GL_N →* GL_N`, the inverse-transpose
  automorphism `g ↦ (g⁻¹)ᵀ` (with `glInvTransposeFun_coe` / `glInvTranspose_coe` as
  `rfl` coe lemmas).
- Redefined `localLeftAlgHom g` to lift `lTransAlgHom ((g⁻¹)ᵀ)` instead of
  `lTransAlgHom (g)`. Since `lTransAlgHom M` realizes `Mᵀ`-multiplication, `M = (g⁻¹)ᵀ`
  gives the substitution `X ↦ g⁻¹ X`, i.e. Etingof's `φ ↦ φ(g⁻¹ ·)`.
- `localLeftAlgHom_algebraMap`, `_one`, `_mul`, `localLeftAlgHom_comp_localRightAlgHom`,
  and `localLeftRep_algebraMap` updated mechanically (multiplicativity/unit/commutation
  now come from `map_mul`/`map_one` of the monoid hom + precomposition).
- Added `eval_lTransAlgHom` (matrix-level: `eval g (lTransAlgHom M p) = eval (Mᵀ·g) p`,
  left analogue of `eval_rTransAlgHom`) and `evalGLAway_localLeftRep`
  (`evalGLAway (localLeftRep g₀ x) g = evalGLAway x (g₀⁻¹ * g)`, left analogue of
  `evalGLAway_localRightRep`).

`polyLeftRep` (the polynomial transpose action on `A`) is left untouched, as the issue
requested.
