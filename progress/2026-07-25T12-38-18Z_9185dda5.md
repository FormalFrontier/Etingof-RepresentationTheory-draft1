## Design note

The multiplicities are extracted through **characters**, not by peeling summands off `V` one at
a time. Peeling requires re-indexing `⨁ (p : Σ i, Fin (n i))` after each step (an `Equiv`
between dependent sigma types with a `Function.update`), which is disproportionately painful.
Instead: induct to get `χ_V = Σᵢ nᵢ χ_{Tᵢ}`, pair with `χ_S` via Theorem 4.5.1(i) to get
`dim Hom(S, V) = Σᵢ nᵢ · dim Hom(S, Tᵢ)` for *every* `S`, observe that the right-hand side is
literally `dim Hom(S, isotypicSum T n)`, and feed that to `iso_of_hom_finrank_eq`. The resulting
isomorphism is still a genuine `FDRep` isomorphism produced by Maschke + Schur, as the issue
required; characters are only used to compute the exponents.

Anyone needing "every representation decomposes as `⨁ᵢ (Tᵢ)^(nᵢ)`" elsewhere in the book should
call `Etingof.FDRep.nonempty_iso_isotypicSum` rather than re-derive it.
