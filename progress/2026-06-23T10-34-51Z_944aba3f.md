## Key decision
The issue suggested a Gale-Ryser/dominance (Hall) inequality on per-row
cumulative counts. Instead the characterization routes through SYTs: the
order-statistics/Hall exchange argument that the raw dominance condition would
require is **already** encapsulated in `column_standard_coset_has_syt'`
(PolytabloidBasis.lean:549 — row-sorting a column-standard filling yields an
SYT). So the SYT-tabloid condition is mathematically equivalent to Gale-Ryser,
sharper (ties directly to the SYT basis tabloids), and sorry-free with no new
combinatorial machinery. Backward direction uses `sytPerm_isColumnStandard'`.
