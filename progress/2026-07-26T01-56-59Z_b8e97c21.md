## Key decisions and patterns

* **The two orders are equal, and that is proved, not assumed.** The issue explicitly
  forbade silently substituting dominance for the root order. Both directions are proved:
  `≼ → ⊵` by summing indicator differences over `Finset.range k` (each positive root
  contributes `[i<k] - [j<k] ≥ 0`), and `⊵ → ≼` by the explicit witness "`D (k+1)` copies
  of the simple root `e k - e (k+1)` for `k < n`", where `D k = S_k(μ) - S_k(λ)`. The
  telescoping coefficient identity `D (i+1) - D i = μ_i - λ_i` is what makes it work, and
  the witness is built by an explicit `ℕ`-recursion (`domWitnessAux`) rather than
  `List.flatMap`, which keeps every step a one-line `List.map_append` / `Finset.sum_range_succ`
  rewrite.
* **Matrix inversion by nilpotency, not by determinant.** Triangularity gives `K = 1 + N`
  with `N` supported strictly below the diagonal. A determinant argument would prove
  invertibility but *not* that the inverse is again supported on the order (a linear
  extension of the dominance order is too coarse to recover it). Instead `Etingof.domRank
  la = ∑_{k ≤ n} S_k(la)` grades the partitions: it is monotone for dominance and strictly
  monotone on strict dominance, so `N` raises the grade by `≥ 1`, hence `N ^ ((n+1)*n+1) =
  0`. Then `∑_k (-N)^k` is a two-sided inverse (`geom_sum_mul` / `mul_geom_sum`) *and* is
  visibly supported on the order, giving invertibility and the support statement together.
  The reusable pieces are the private predicates `TriSupp` (supported on dominance; closed
  under `1`, `*`, `-`, `^`, `∑`) and `GradedBy` (raises `domRank` by `≥ d`).
* **Kostka numbers are taken in their Young's-rule form** (`Etingof.spechtMultiplicity`),
  which is what Theorem 5.15.1 supplies. Reconciling that with the semistandard-tableau
  count `Etingof.KostkaNumber` of Definition 5.14.2 is separate work already tracked by
  issue #7457; this is stated in the module docstring and the coverage note rather than
  papered over.
* Small reusable additions to the partition API: `Nat.Partition.partAt`,
  `sum_take_eq_sum_partAt`, `sortedParts_length_le`, `partAt_eq_zero_of_le`,
  `sum_take_of_le`, and `Nat.Partition.Dominates.refl` / `.trans` (the project previously
  had only `.antisymm`).
