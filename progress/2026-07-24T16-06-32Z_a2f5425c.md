## Accomplished

Completed issue #7685: the **universal mapping property for the `q`-Weyl algebra**, in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter2/QWeylAlgebraUniversal.lean`.

A prior session (merged commit 0017ccf8, #7687) had already landed the hard target-side
commutation lemmas: the unit-group reordering `unit_YpowXpow` (`Yᵐ Xⁿ = qUᵐⁿ Xⁿ Yᵐ`) and the
operator reordering law `op_mul` (`op (i,j) · op (i',j') = q^{j i'} • op (i+i', j+j')`). This
turn added the remaining pieces:

- Source monomial basis: `qWeylMono`, `qWeylMono_mul` (source reordering), linear independence
  and spanning (from `qMono_linearIndependent` / `qWeyl_toSubmodule_eq_span`), packaged as
  `basis : Basis (ℤ × ℤ) k (qWeylAlgebra k q)`.
- The lift: `toEndLinear` via `Basis.constr`, multiplicativity on basis monomials reduced
  directly to `op_mul`, extended bilinearly via `Basis.ext` + `LinearMap.mulLeft/mulRight`, and
  packaged as the algebra hom `toEnd : qWeylAlgebra k q →ₐ[k] Module.End k V`.
- Characterizing equations `toEnd_x` (`x ↦ X`) and `toEnd_y` (`y ↦ Y`).
- Convenience wrapper `module` (`Module.compHom` through `toEnd.toRingHom`) plus
  `module_isScalarTower`, producing the `Module (qWeylAlgebra k q) V` + `IsScalarTower` instances
  used in `Problem2_7_5.lean`.

Note: everything is stated over a general `CommRing k`, not just `ℂ`, generalizing the issue's
ask at no extra cost.
