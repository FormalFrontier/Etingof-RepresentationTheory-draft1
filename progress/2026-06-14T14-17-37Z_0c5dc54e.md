## Blockers

**Deliverable 2 of #4638 as literally stated ("three leaf subspaces equal for
any complementary invariant pair, via `compl_le_forces_eq`") is not provable as
a standalone stepping stone — it is circular.** The membership criteria proven
this turn give the *exact* iff
`x ∈ W₁(2) ↔ (0,x,x) ∈ W₁(0)`, `x ∈ W₁(4) ↔ (x,0,x) ∈ W₁(0)`. The implication
`W₁(2) ≤ W₁(4)` that `compl_le_forces_eq` would need is
`(0,x,x) ∈ W₁(0) ⟹ (x,0,x) ∈ W₁(0)`, which is **false** for a general center
subspace: e.g. `W₁(0) = ⟨(0,1,1)⟩` gives `W₁(2) = ⊤` but `W₁(4) = ⊥`. Such a
`W₁(0)` is excluded only by the global three-plane brick obstruction
(`π_B`/`π_C` cannot both embed in a 2-dim complement) — i.e. the leaf
subspaces coincide *only because* the surviving pairs are trivial, which is the
indecomposability theorem itself. So leaf-equality is a corollary of triviality,
not a route to it.

Recommendation for the planner: re-scope the remaining collapse (deliverable
2 conclusion + deliverable 3) into sub-C #4577 as a brick contradiction
consuming the criteria + plane-splits, rather than a leaf-equality lemma. A
sibling counterexample for the D̃ family already lives at
`progress/d6tilde-mixed-leafeq-counterexample.py`.
