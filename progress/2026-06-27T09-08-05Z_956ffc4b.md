## Accomplished

One-shot dispatch on **#5325** (`clength_additive` — the categorical Jordan–Hölder additivity crux

Net new, sorry-free, reusable lemmas in `Length.lean`:

1. `pullback_bot_eq_kernelSubobject` — `(Subobject.pullback g).obj ⊥ = kernelSubobject g`
   (`g⁻¹(0) = ker g`), via `le_kernelSubobject` one way and the pullback universal property
   (`IsPullback.lift`) the other.
2. `imageSubobject_pullback_arrow_comp` — for an **epi** `g`, `g(g⁻¹(T)) = T`, i.e.
   `imageSubobject (((pullback g).obj T).arrow ≫ g) = T`. Uses `Abelian.epi_fst_of_isLimit`
   (pullback of an epi is epi) so the image of the composite collapses to the mono `T.arrow`.
   This is the section identity making `Subobject.pullback g` injective.
3. `height_top_le_coheight_kernel` — **epi-side inequality**:
   `height ⊤(X₃) ≤ coheight (ker g)`. `Subobject.pullback S.g` is strictly monotone (injective by
   the section identity) and sends `⊥ ↦ ker g`, so `coheight_le_coheight_apply_of_strictMono` plus
   `coheight ⊥ = height ⊤` (via `krullDim`, transported across `WithBot.coe_inj`) gives it.
4. `height_top_add_le` — **lower bound (heights)**:
   `height ⊤(X₁) + height ⊤(X₃) ≤ height ⊤(X₂)`. Combines the already-landed down-interval iso
   `height_mk_eq_height_top` (with `mk f = ker g` by exactness, `exact_iff_image_eq_kernel`), the
   epi-side inequality (3), and the modularity-free order lemma `height_add_coheight_le_height_top`.
5. `clength_add_le` — **lower bound (lengths)**: `clength X₁ + clength X₃ ≤ clength X₂`, the
   `ENat.toNat` transport of (4) (all heights finite via `height_top_lt_top`).

`clength_additive` is now `le_antisymm (clength_le_add hS) (clength_add_le hS)` — **sorry-free**.
`clength_le_add` (the upper bound) is the lone `sorry`, with a sharp docstring route and tracked as
#5520.
