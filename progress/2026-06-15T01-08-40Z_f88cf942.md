## Current frontier

The theorem is now in a position where it *can* be proved (downstream of
`decompose_polynomial_gl_rep`). The proof (deliverable 3) is **not** closable
with the current upstream API; investigation found three concrete gaps:

1. **Algebraicity hypothesis.** `decompose_polynomial_gl_rep` requires
   `halg : IsAlgebraicRepresentation N M.ρ`. Algebraicity is structural
   ("matrix entries are polynomials") and is **not** implied by
   `formalCharacter M = schurPoly N lam` + `h_dim`. The signature must gain
   `halg`, and the consumer must supply algebraicity of
   `detTwistedSchurModuleRep` — for which **no lemma currently exists** (no
   `schurModuleRep`/`detTwist` algebraicity lemma in the repo).
2. **`h_span` threading.** `decompose_polynomial_gl_rep` also needs
   `h_span : ⨆ μ, glWeightSpace … = ⊤`. The consumer already proves exactly this
   (`Proposition5_22_2` `h₁_top`), so adding `h_span` to the signature is the
   clean route.
3. **Abstract-simple character classification.** Step 4 needs the abstract
   simples `L i` of the decomposition to have `formalCharacter = schurPoly` of
   distinct antitone partitions, to apply
   `SchurWeylSimplesClassification.{formalCharacter_linearIndependent_of_eq_schurPoly,
   glTensorRep_schurWeyl_simples_formalCharacter_linearIndependent}`. But
   `decompose_polynomial_gl_rep` **hides** the tensor-power decomposition data
   `(e, he)` those lemmas consume. Either `decompose_polynomial_gl_rep` must
   additionally expose that its simples are `schurPoly`-classified, or a bridge
   lemma is needed.
