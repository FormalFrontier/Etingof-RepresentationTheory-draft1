Statement:

```lean
theorem range_matHom₄_not_finite_of_three_ne_zero (k : Type*) [Field k] (h3 : (3 : k) ≠ 0) :
    ¬ Module.Finite k (LinearMap.range (matHom₄ k).toLinearMap)
```

Approach (a period-2 ladder inside the range, all scalars powers of 3):
- `G3mat = 3(E₀₁+E₁₂)·t = ⁅NY,⁅NY,⁅NY,NX⁆⁆⁆` and `G1mat = -(E₁₀+E₂₁)·t = ⁅NY,NX⁆`, both images
  of free-Lie brackets of `x,y`, hence in the range.
- The operator `Ψ(v) = ⁅⁅v, G3mat⁆, G1mat⁆` satisfies `Ψ(p·(E₂₁-E₁₀)) = -9·X²·p·(E₂₁-E₁₀)`
  (two entrywise `Matrix.ext` bracket computations: `bracket_r_G3`, `bracket_D_G1`).
- `wSeq n` (free-algebra witnesses) with `matHom₄ (wSeq n) = 3·(-9)ⁿ·(E₂₁-E₁₀)·t^{2n+2}`
  (`matHom₄_wSeq`, by induction from `bracket_NX_G3` base + `Ψ`).
- Coefficient nonzero iff `3·(-9)ⁿ ≠ 0`, i.e. `char k ≠ 3` (works in char 2 since `3 = 1`).
- Distinct `t`-degrees give linear independence: pull back along the `(2,1)`-entry functional
  `entry21` to reduce to independence of `{3(-9)ⁿ X^{2n+2}}`, established from
  `Polynomial.basisMonomials` + `LinearIndependent.units_smul`.
- Infinite independent family in a `Module.Finite` space is impossible
  (`Module.Finite.not_linearIndependent_of_infinite`).
