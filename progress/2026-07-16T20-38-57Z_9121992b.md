- `isPole_smul` — pole-set invariance: if `g ∈ G` and `v` is a pole, then `g • v` is a

- `pole_order_data` — for finite `G ≤ SO(3)` with `2 ≤ Nat.card G`, produces the multiset
  `m` of pole-orbit stabilizer orders with each `mᵢ ≥ 2`, each `mᵢ ∣ n`, satisfying
  `2(1 - 1/n) = ∑ᵢ (1 - 1/mᵢ)`, and applies `pole_order_diophantine` to land the five
  families (`{n,n}`, `{2,2,k}`, `{2,3,3}`, `{2,3,4}`, `{2,3,5}`).

Proof strategy: Mathlib's Burnside lemma (`sum_card_fixedBy_eq_card_orbits_mul_card_group`)
gives `∑_g |Fix g| = k·n`; the class formula (`card_eq_sum_card_group_div_card_stabilizer`)
gives `|poleSet| = ∑ᵢ n/mᵢ`; `|Fix 1| = |poleSet|` and `|Fix g| = 2` for `g ≠ 1` (from
`nontrivial_fixed_unit_vectors`) give `∑_g |Fix g| = |poleSet| + 2(n-1)`. Combining and
clearing denominators yields the identity. Cyclicity of stabilizers is **not** needed for
the counting — only `mᵢ ≥ 2` (pole has nontrivial stabilizer) and `mᵢ ∣ n` (Lagrange).
