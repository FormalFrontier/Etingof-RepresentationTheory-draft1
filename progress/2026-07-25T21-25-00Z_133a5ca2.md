## Decisions and patterns

**The proof is the scalar torus, not the `SL_N` torus.** The issue proposed redoing
`AlgIrrepGLNonIso`'s `glWeightSpaceℤ`/`diagUnit` argument for the determinant-one subtorus. That
works but is a lot of weight-space bookkeeping. The cheaper route uses only the *centre*:

* `s·1ᴺ` acts on `L_λ` by the single scalar `s^{|λ|}`, `|λ| = ∑ᵢ λᵢ`. The `det^{-λ.shift}`
  normalization built into `algIrrepGLRepρ` contributes `s^{-N·λ.shift}` and exactly cancels the
  `+N·λ.shift` that `toNatWeight` puts into the Schur module's tensor degree, so the total weight
  is the only surviving invariant.
* The scalars *inside* `SL_N` are the `N`-th roots of unity. An `SL_N`-equivariant isomorphism
  must match the scalar each one acts by, and a primitive root (available in an algebraically
  closed field of characteristic zero) turns that into `N ∣ |μ| - |λ|`. That divisibility is
  precisely what makes `c = (|μ| - |λ|)/N` an integer.
* `GL_N = (scalars)·SL_N` over an algebraically closed field, so once `λ` is replaced by
  `λ + c·1ᴺ` (harmless, by the already-proved `algIrrepGL_slEquiv_constShift`) the two total
  weights agree, the scalar factors cancel on both sides, and the `SL_N`-map is *already*
  `GL_N`-equivariant. Then `algIrrepGLRepρ_iso_iff_eq` gives `λ + c·1ᴺ = μ` on the nose.

The whole thing needs `schurModuleSubmodule_ne_bot` (one nonzero vector to compare scalars on) and
nothing else from the `GL_N` weight-space machinery.

**Mathlib entry points worth remembering.** A primitive `N`-th root of unity in an algebraically
closed field of characteristic zero is `HasEnoughRootsOfUnity.exists_primitiveRoot k n`, which
fires off `IsSepClosed.hasEnoughRootsOfUnity` given `[NeZero ((n : ℕ) : k)]`. Move it to `kˣ` with
`IsPrimitiveRoot.coe_units_iff`, then `IsPrimitiveRoot.zpow_eq_one_iff_dvd` converts
`u ^ (|μ| - |λ|) = 1` straight into `(n : ℤ) ∣ |μ| - |λ|`.

**Traps hit (each cost a build cycle):**

* **`AlgIrrepGL` has its own `Module` instance, so lemmas stated over `SchurModuleSubmodule` will
  not `rw` into a goal at the `AlgIrrepGL` carrier.** `AlgIrrepGL.module` is
  `show Module k ↥(SchurModuleSubmodule …) from inferInstance` — defeq to the submodule's instance
  but not syntactically it, so `rw [schurModuleRep_scalarGL]` fails with an "argument … has type
  `@Representation … (↥(SchurModuleSubmodule …)) …` but is expected to have type
  `@Representation … (AlgIrrepGL n lam k) …`" mismatch. Fix: a `change` that restates the goal on
  the submodule carrier (with `(v : SchurModuleSubmodule k n lam.toNatWeight)` ascriptions), do
  the rewriting there, and let defeq carry it back. Stating the *conclusion* at the `AlgIrrepGL`
  type is still right — that is where callers use it.
* **`map_mul`/`map_inv` unfold `detChar` to `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.det`**, after which
  `detChar_scalarGL` no longer matches. Keep an unfolded copy (`det_scalarGL`) to rewrite with.
* **`omit [inst] in` goes *before* the docstring**, not between the docstring and the declaration;
  the latter is a parse error (`unexpected token 'omit'; expected 'lemma'`) whose reported column
  points nowhere useful.
* `Matrix.smul_mulVec_assoc` does not exist; the lemma is `Matrix.smul_mulVec`.
* `Representation.Equiv`'s pointwise `IntertwiningMap.isIntertwining` takes `ρ` and `σ` as
  *explicit* arguments, so `E.isIntertwining g v` fails to elaborate. The `∘ₗ` form
  `E.isIntertwining' g` does work with dot notation (the file already relied on it); wrap it once
  as `equiv_apply_rho E g v := LinearMap.ext_iff.mp (E.isIntertwining' g) v` and phrase everything
  downstream on `E.toLinearEquiv`, which is also what `asModuleEquivOfIntertwiner` consumes.
