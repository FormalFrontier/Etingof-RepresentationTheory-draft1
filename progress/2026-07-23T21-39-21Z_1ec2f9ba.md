### Root cause
The Mathlib bump to `v4.32.0-rc1` added *shortcut* `Semiring ℂ`/`CommSemiring ℂ`/
`AddCommMonoid ℂ` instances (`delta% inferInstance` in `Data/Complex/Basic.lean`).
`Representation.character` requires `[Field k]` and derives the carrier's
`AddCommMonoid`/`Semiring` from an `[AddCommGroup V]` binder, whereas the composite
representations built by `Representation.tprod`/`prod`/`directSum` carry the
`TensorProduct.addCommMonoid` / `Prod.instAddCommMonoid` / `instAddCommMonoidDirectSum`
instances directly. These are genuinely defeq (verified by `rfl`), but during
dot-notation elaboration the carrier `V` is a metavariable, so the elaborator eagerly
assigns the `AddCommGroup.toAddCommMonoid` monoid slot and then fails to unify it with
the composite's monoid at reducible transparency. Result: three "application/typeclass"
failures at the old lines 944, 1303, 1575.

### Fixes (three sites)
1. `Vrep_tprod_Vrep_character`: pin the carrier via
   `Representation.character (V := TensorProduct ℂ ...) (tprod ...) g`; Mathlib's
   `Representation.char_tensor` shares `character`'s instance setup so the proof matches.
2. `rhsRep_character`: pin the carrier in the statement, and prove by building
   standalone carrier-pinned `char_prod`/`char_directSum` terms (`have key := ...`) and
   rewriting with them. The final arithmetic bullet `show`s the goal with the natural
   `↥Vsub.toSubmodule` atom (defeq to `↥(zeroSum K)` produced by `char_directSum`) so
   `ring` sees a single atom.
3. `Vrep_tprod_Vrep_equiv_rhsRep`: supply `(V := ...) (W := ...)` and both
   representations explicitly to `equiv_of_character_eq_reducible` so the carriers are
   pinned (fixes the stuck `FiniteDimensional ℂ ?m`).

Also added `set_option maxHeartbeats 1000000 in` on `rhsRep_character` for headroom.

### Preserved (per the issue)
