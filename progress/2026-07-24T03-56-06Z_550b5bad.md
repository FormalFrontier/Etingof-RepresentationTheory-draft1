## Accomplished

Closed #7487: strengthened Exercise 8.2.2 from "every module has *a* projective
resolution" to the book's explicit "consisting of free modules" construction.

- Added `Etingof.FreeResolutionBuilder`, a resolution builder mirroring Mathlib's
  `CategoryTheory.ProjectiveResolution.of` but parameterised by an *explicit* choice
  of projective cover `over X ↠ X`. Because every term is literally `over (…)`, any
  structural property of `over` is inherited by the whole resolution. This is the key
  move: Mathlib's generic `ProjectiveResolution.of` routes through the opaque
  `EnoughProjectives` interface (`Classical.choice`), so its terms are provably
  projective but *not* provably free.
- Instantiated the builder with the free cover `M ↦ (M →₀ A)` (`FreeResolution.freeOver`,
  basis `Finsupp.basisSingleOne`, epi `Finsupp.linearCombination A id`).
- New public theorem `Exercise_8_2_2_free`:
  `∃ P : Etingof.ProjectiveResolution M, ∀ n, Module.Free A (P.complex.X n)`,
  for every left module over an arbitrary ring `A`.
- Retained the original `Exercise_8_2_2` unchanged.
- Updated `progress/items.json` coverage for `Chapter8/Exercise8.2.2` to
  `covered_full` / `fidelity: verified`.
