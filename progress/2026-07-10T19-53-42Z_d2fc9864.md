## Key decision (statement correction)

The original spec RHS `(n + m - 1).choose (m - 1)` is **false at `m = 0`**: natural-number
subtraction collapses it to `(n-1).choose 0 = 1` for every `n`, but `k[]` ≅ `k` has dimension `1`
only in degree `0`. I changed both theorems to the mathematically-equal-for-`m≥1` and
universally-correct form `(n + m - 1).choose n` (= `Nat.multichoose m n`), which is exactly what
Mathlib's `card_finsuppAntidiag_nat_eq_choose` produces and what makes the power-series identity
hold for all `m` including `0`. This is faithful to the book's binomial (same number when `m ≥ 1`);
docstrings explain the choice. New imports: `RingTheory.MvPolynomial.Basic`,
`RingTheory.PowerSeries.WellKnown`, `Algebra.Order.Antidiag.FinsuppEquiv`.
