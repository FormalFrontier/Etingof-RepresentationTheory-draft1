### Signature change (deliberate, documented)

The `proof_wanted` was stated `{k : Type*} [Field k] [IsAlgClosed k]`. The honest
provable statement is `(k : Type) [Field k] [IsAlgClosed k] [CharZero k]`: the
det-twist route (Prop 5.22.2 → Theorem 5.22.1 Weyl character formula,
`SchurWeylFormalCharacterIso`) requires `CharZero` and fixes `k : Type` (universe 0).
The entire GL/Schur representation theory in Chapter 5 lives in char 0, universe 0,
so this is the correct home for the identity. Nothing depended on the
`proof_wanted`, so the restriction breaks no downstream code. The docstring notes
the char-zero / universe-0 constraint.
