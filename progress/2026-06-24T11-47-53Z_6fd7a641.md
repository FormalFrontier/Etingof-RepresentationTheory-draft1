## Accomplished

Replaced the vacuous `: True` stub of `Etingof.Lemma5_4_7`
(`Chapter5/Lemma5_4_7.lean`, issue #5126) with a faithful statement and full
proof.

- **Statement:** for `g` with `|C(g)| = p^k` (`p` prime, `k > 0`), there exists
  a nontrivial irreducible `V` with `p ∤ dim V` and `χ_V(g) ≠ 0`. Expressed via
  the project's Wedderburn enumeration `IrrepDecomp`: `∃ V : FDRep ℂ G, Simple V
  ∧ ¬Nonempty (V ≅ FDRep.of (trivial)) ∧ ¬(p ∣ finrank V) ∧ V.character g ≠ 0`.
- **Proof:** mirrors the book's algebraic-integer argument (and the inlined
  argument already present in `Theorem5_4_6`'s `no_prime_power_conjClass`):
  column orthogonality `sum_dim_character_eq_zero` gives `∑ d_i χ_i(g) = 0`;
  the trivial term contributes `1`; if every nontrivial `p ∤ dim` irrep had
  `χ(g) = 0`, the surviving `p`-divisible terms factor as `p·S` with `S` an
  algebraic integer, forcing `S = -1/p`, contradicting `Etingof.Proposition5_2_5`.
- Reproduced the three private helpers (`character_isIntegral`,
  `trivial_character_eq_one`, `trivialFDRep_simple`) and `card_conjClass_one`
  locally in a `Etingof.Lemma5_4_7Aux` namespace, since those in `Theorem5_4_6`
  are `private` and not importable.

Dependency #5128 (Proposition 5.3.2) is closed; the proof here does not actually
need 5.3.2 — only character-value integrality plus column orthogonality, both
available.
