## Next step

Prove `exists_octahedral_faithful_hom`. It decomposes into three real pieces, and is genuinely
multi-session:

1. **Single-orbit infrastructure (API gap).** The 4-diagonal quotient needs the fact that the
   eight order-`3` poles form a *single* `G`-orbit of size `8`, closed under antipode
   (`w ↦ -w`). `pole_order_data`'s external signature only exposes
   `∀ x ∈ m, ∃ b, Nat.card (stabilizer G b) = x` — existence of one representative per multiset
   entry, NOT that entries of multiplicity `1` correspond to a single orbit. This "one orbit per
   distinct multiset value" fact is internal to `pole_order_data` and must be exposed (or
   re-proved). Likely wants its own sub-issue: strengthen `pole_order_data` (or add a companion
   lemma) to return, for each `x ∈ m`, the *orbit* of order-`x` poles with its cardinality
   `|G| / x`, and antipode-closure.
2. **Quotient construction.** Build `D` = order-`3` poles modulo `w ~ ±w`, prove `|D| = 4`
   (free `C₂` antipode action on an `8`-set), give it `MulAction ↥G D`, and transport to
   `Equiv.Perm (Fin 4)` exactly as `so3_tetrahedral_of_poleData` Step 4 does. NB: this is
   *data* (`instance`/`def`), so it must be constructed sorry-free — do not land it until (1)
   is available.
3. **Faithfulness (geometric coplanarity).** A kernel element `g ≠ 1` fixes each diagonal
   setwise ⟹ `g • w = ±w` for all order-`3` poles ⟹ (via `nontrivial_fixed_unit_vectors` +
   `isCyclic_stabilizer_pole`) `ord g = 2` and `g • w = -w` for *all* order-`3` poles ⟹ they
   are coplanar (the `(-1)`-eigenplane of `g`). Refute via the `G`-invariant normal line and
   `isCyclic_of_common_fixed_vector` forcing a pole of stabilizer order `≥ 12`, contradicting
   the maximal pole order `4`. (A "maximal pole order = max of `m`" lemma may be a useful
   supporting fact — also currently not exposed.)

Recommend a planner decompose #6972 into: (a) the single-orbit / pole-orbit-structure lemma,
(b) the quotient + faithfulness given (a).
