## Accomplished

Closed issue #7251 (Ch5: irreducibility of L_λ for dominant integer weights
outside the Schur–Weyl range). The deliverable — irreducibility of `algIrrepGLRepρ`
for *every* dominant integer weight with no `∑ λ.toNatWeight ≤ n` restriction — was
found to be **already formalized** in the codebase, downstream of the construction
file:

- `Etingof.algIrrepGLRepρ_isSimpleModule` (`Chapter5/Theorem5_23_2_PeterWeyl.lean:270`):
  `IsSimpleModule (k[GL_n]) (algIrrepGLRepρ n lam k).asModule` for general
  `k : Type` `[Field][IsAlgClosed][CharZero]` and *any* `DominantWeight n` — **no
  degree guard**. Verified axiom-clean (`propext / Classical.choice / Quot.sound`,
  no `sorryAx`).
- Its degree-guard-free core `Etingof.schurModule_isSimple_general`
  (`Chapter5/CleanCharacterExtractionBase.lean:83`): unconditional Schur-module
  simplicity, built on the already-existing
  `schurModuleSubmodule_isSimple_centralizer_general`. Also axiom-clean.

The math: a `det^{-λ.shift}` twist preserves simplicity
(`isSimpleModule_charTwistRep`), reducing simplicity of `L_λ` to that of the shifted
Schur module `schurModuleRep k n λ.toNatWeight`. Since `λ.toNatWeight : Fin n → ℕ`
has at most `n` parts by construction, `S^λ(k^n)` is always irreducible — the old
`∑ λ ≤ n` guard on the ℂ statement was a mere artifact of the ℂ-specific
special-block lemmas.

The Stage 3.7 audit #7245 flagged this item covered_partial because it only
inspected `algIrrepGLRep_isSimple` (ℂ, degree-guarded) in the construction file
`AlgIrrepGLRep.lean` and missed the general lemma proved in the Peter–Weyl
development.
