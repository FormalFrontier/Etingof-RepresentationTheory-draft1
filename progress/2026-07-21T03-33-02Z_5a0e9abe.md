## Accomplished

Worked issue #7127 (feat, Ch3): the finiteness half of Theorem 3.5.4. Added a new
additive file `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Theorem3_5_4_Finiteness.lean`
formalizing the two ingredients of the book's finiteness argument (Theorem 3.5.4,
first two paragraphs):

- `Etingof.simple_finiteDimensional` — every irreducible representation of a finite
  dimensional algebra is finite dimensional. Proof follows the book: for nonzero `v₀`,
  `A • v₀ = span A {v₀}` is a nonzero submodule of the simple `V`, hence `⊤`; so
  `toSpanSingleton A V v₀` is surjective; restricting scalars to `k` exhibits `V` as a
  `k`-linear quotient of the finite dimensional `A`, giving `FiniteDimensional k V`.

- `Etingof.card_irreducibles_le_finrank` — any `Fintype`-indexed family of pairwise
  nonisomorphic finite dimensional irreducibles has `Fintype.card ι ≤ finrank k A`.
  Proof follows the book: `density_theorem_part2` (Theorem 3.2.2(ii)) gives surjectivity
  of `φ : A →ₐ[k] ∏ᵢ End k (V i)`, so `finrank (∏ End Vᵢ) ≤ finrank A`; then
  `card ι = ∑ᵢ 1 ≤ ∑ᵢ finrank (End Vᵢ) = ∑ᵢ (finrank Vᵢ)² = finrank (∏ End Vᵢ)`,
  using `finrank Vᵢ ≥ 1` (each `Vᵢ` nonzero as it is simple).

Both are axiom-clean: `#print axioms` on each yields exactly
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. `structure_mod_radical` is untouched (additive).
The new file is wired into `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3.lean`; both the file and
the `Chapter3` aggregator build cleanly.

## Next step

A follow-up issue should scope the construction of the complete family. This is the hard,
type-theoretic part: it needs a maximal-cardinality pairwise-non-iso family (existence
via the new `card_irreducibles_le_finrank` bound + a maximum over bounded cardinalities),
with maximality giving completeness. Representing "families of module Types up to iso"
cleanly (e.g. via representatives `A ⧸ J` for maximal left ideals `J`) is the main design
question and likely warrants its own decomposition. The two lemmas landed here are the
reusable prerequisites for that construction.
