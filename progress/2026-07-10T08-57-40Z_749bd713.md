## Key patterns / decisions
- Route (as the issue suggested): `rad = Ring.jacobson R` annihilates every simple module
  (`IsSemisimpleModule.jacobson_le_annihilator`), so a simple `R`-module descends to a simple
  module over the semisimple quotient `A = R ⧸ rad` (`Module.IsTorsionBySet.module` +
  `LinearMap.isSimpleModule_iff_of_bijective` on the identity semilinear map). Over the
  semisimple (Noetherian) ring `A`, each simple module embeds into `A` as an ideal
  (`IsSemisimpleRing.exists_linearEquiv_ideal_of_isSimpleModule`) lying in one of the finitely
  many isotypic components (`Finite (isotypicComponents A A)`).
- The map `⟦X⟧ ↦ isotypicComponent A A (descent of X)` is choice-free on the value (uses
  `LinearEquiv.isotypicComponent_eq`), well-defined on iso classes, and injective — so
  `Finite.of_injective` into `isotypicComponents A A` finishes it. Avoids `Shrink`/universe
  juggling entirely.
- Small reusable private helpers: `isTorsionBySet_jacobson`, `jacobsonModule`,
  `isSimpleModule_jacobsonModule`, and `promoteEquiv`/`demoteEquiv` (R-linear ⇄ A-linear
  equivalences between descended modules, the two actions agreeing definitionally).
- Inlined `IsArtinianRing`→`IsSemiprimaryRing` rather than importing Chapter8/SemiprimaryAlgebra.
