## Accomplished

Resolved fidelity issue #5619 (Chapter3/Definition3.4.1 — `Etingof.Filtration` dropped the
boundary conditions).

The book's Definition 3.4.1 says a (finite) filtration of `V` is a sequence
`0 = V₀ ⊂ V₁ ⊂ ⋯ ⊂ Vₙ = V`. The previous Lean definition was

```
abbrev Etingof.Filtration A V := RelSeries {p : Submodule A V × Submodule A V | p.1 < p.2}
```

a bare strictly ascending chain of submodules with no constraint that the chain starts at `0`
or ends at `V`. Any chain between two arbitrary submodules qualified, so the type modeled "a
chain of submodules" rather than "a filtration of `V`".

Fix (`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Definition3_4_1.lean`): replaced the `abbrev` with a
`structure` that bundles the underlying chain with the two dropped conjuncts:

- `chain : RelSeries {p : Submodule A V × Submodule A V | p.1 < p.2}` — the ascending chain,
- `head_eq_bot : chain.head = ⊥` — the filtration starts at `0` (`V₀ = ⊥`),
- `last_eq_top : chain.last = ⊤` — the filtration ends at `V` (`Vₙ = ⊤`).

`Etingof.Filtration` had no downstream users (Lemma 3.4.2 uses `CompositionSeries` directly with
its own `head = ⊥ ∧ last = ⊤` conditions), so strengthening the definition broke nothing.

No `sorry`, no `native_decide`. The structure is a real construction (no sorried data).
