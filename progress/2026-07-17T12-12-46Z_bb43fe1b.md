## Notes / patterns discovered

- The recurring friction was the `extTensorFunctorLeftObj X Y` vs `((extTensorFunctorLeft).obj X).obj Y`
  discrepancy: definitionally equal but not equal at `instances` transparency, so `rw` chokes when a
  fixed-type `eqToHom` (from a `mapBifunctorDesc`) is composed with an `extTensorFunctorLeftMap`.
  Fix: state the sign identity in functor form and convert the naturality output to functor form via
  `show … from rfl` before any structural `rw`.
- `Functor.map_comp_assoc` (`F.map (f ≫ g) ≫ h = F.map f ≫ F.map g ≫ h`) is the clean tool for
  combining two adjacent functor-maps that carry a trailing morphism.
- `homYoneda` is only registered `Additive`, not `Linear`, so `Functor.map_units_smul` is
  unavailable; convert the `ℤˣ`-smul to `zsmul` via `Units.smul_def` then use `op_zsmul` +
  `Functor.map_zsmul`.
