### Worth recording

#7749 carries a genuine mathematical gap that triage did **not** paper over: five of the six
`OddLayer b' c''` fields have paper proofs written out in the issue body, but
`⁅a₄, b'⁆ = 2 D c''` does not. The decomposing worker documented that every route it tried
(commuting `A₄` past `A₁` via `ad b₂`; `A₄ = [D, A₃]`; `[X, A₄] = ad ⁅a₀,a₄⁆`; `serre_y` applied to
`b'`, `c`, `Dc`) is a tautology — a consequence of the commutation relations, which are
automatically consistent — and that new input is needed, most plausibly a bracket of a layer-2
element (`b₀ = ⁅a₀,a₃⁆`, `b₁ = ⁅a₀,a₄⁆`, `b₂ = ⁅a₁,a₄⁆`) against `c`. That is a real open sub-problem,
not a stale plan, which is why #7749 stays claimable rather than being rewritten.

Also noted in #7749: the induction has to carry the *pair* `(b, c)`, since `⁅a₁, b'⁆ = 0` is not a
formal consequence of `EvenLayer c` alone. #7750's deliverables should be read with that in mind.
