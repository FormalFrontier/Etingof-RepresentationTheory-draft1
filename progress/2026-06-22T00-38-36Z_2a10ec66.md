(`SpechtModuleBasis.lean:2236`). Confirmed obstruction this session:

- The route's "IH-available representative `β'` (`[β'] ≼ [σ]`)" is **too weak**.
  `ih` requires `β'` lex-strictly-below σ: `srRank β' < srRank σ` OR
  (`[β'] = [σ]` ∧ `rowInvCount' β' < rowInvCount' σ`).
- When the dominance-maximal tabloid has `[β] = [σ]` (the `perQ_eqHi` region;
  empirically the max of `Δ` equals `[σ]` ~65–75% of the time), a col-std rep
  `β'` of `[β]` need not be lex-below σ (e.g. `β' = σ` has equal
  `rowInvCount'`), so `ih` cannot discharge `ψ_{β'}`.
- The unconditional base straightening
  `generalizedPolytabloidTab_mem_span_polytabloidTab` (line ~2992) is **proved
  downstream** of `twistedPolytabloid_residual_in_V`, so using it here is
  circular. Verified the dependency direction.
- Therefore a naive peeling "assembly" (peel `c·ψ_{β'}` for an arbitrary col-std
  rep, recurse on lex `(maxSrRank, cardAtMax)`) is **unsound in the `[β]=[σ]`
  case** and would silently introduce a second hole — do not land it.

So #4995 sub-A is genuinely "the crux must deliver a col-std rep that is
lex-below σ (strictly fewer row inversions when `[β']=[σ]`)" — the substantive
James/Fulton content, not a packaging of the elimination loop.
