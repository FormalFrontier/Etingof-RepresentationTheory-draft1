## Accomplished

Issue #7796 (Ch4 Problem 4.12.11 part (a)): replaced the docstring-only claim that the
3-dimensional summand of `End(ℝ³)` "is the standard representation `V`" with an actual
theorem. All five deliverables landed in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Problem4_12_11.lean`, sorry-free and axiom-clean.

* `stdRep : Representation ℝ SO3 (Fin 3 → ℝ)`, `A • v = A *ᵥ v`, built from
  `Matrix.mulVecLin` (`map_one'`/`map_mul'` from `mulVecLin_one`/`mulVecLin_mul` plus `rfl`).
* `hatMap : (Fin 3 → ℝ) →ₗ[ℝ] EndV`, `v ↦ !![0, -v 2, v 1; v 2, 0, -v 0; -v 1, v 0, 0]`,
  with `hatMap_mem : hatMap v ∈ skewSub`, and the inverse `unhat M = ![M 2 1, M 0 2, M 1 0]`
  with both round-trips (`unhat_hatMap`, `hatMap_unhat`).
* `transpose_mul_hatMap_mulVec (A : EndV) (v)` : `Aᵀ * hatMap (A *ᵥ v) * A = A.det • hatMap v`.
  This is a polynomial identity in the entries of `A` and `v`, valid for every `3 × 3` matrix;
  it closes by `fin_cases i <;> fin_cases j <;> simp [...] <;> ring` after expanding
  `Matrix.mul_apply`, `Fin.sum_univ_three`, `Matrix.mulVec`, `dotProduct`,
  `Matrix.det_fin_three`.
* `hatMap_mulVec (A : SO3) (v)` : `hatMap (A *ᵥ v) = conjRep A (hatMap v)`, obtained from the
  polynomial identity by conjugating with `A · Aᵀ = 1` and using `det A = 1`. Over `O(3)` the
  identity picks up a factor of `det A`, so this step is exactly where the summand is pinned
  to the standard representation rather than its determinant twist.
* `skewRep : Representation ℝ SO3 skewSub` (the restriction of `conjRep`, using the existing
  `conjRep_invariant`), `hatEquiv : (Fin 3 → ℝ) ≃ₗ[ℝ] skewSub`, and the headline
  `hatEquiv_equivariant : hatEquiv (stdRep A v) = skewRep A (hatEquiv v)`.

Docstrings on the module, on `skewSub`, and on `skewSub_irreducible` now cite
`hatEquiv_equivariant` instead of asserting the isomorphism. `progress/items.json` for
`Chapter4/Problem4.12.11` records the new declarations in `fidelity_decl` and describes the
construction in `coverage_note`/`fidelity_note`.
