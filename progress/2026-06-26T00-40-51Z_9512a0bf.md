Key gotchas recorded for the skill:
- `MonoidAlgebra ℂ G` vs `G →₀ ℂ` carry *different* `AddCommMonoid`/`Module` instances on the same
  carrier; `LinearMap.comp` (`∘ₗ`) and `LinearMap`-equality types reject the mismatch even though
  the instances are defeq. Bridge with an all-`rfl` identity `LinearEquiv toFinsuppLE`, and keep
  group-algebra maps typed in `MonoidAlgebra`.
- A bare `Finsupp.single h r * (algebra)` fails to elaborate (`Finsupp` has no `Mul`); use
  `MonoidAlgebra.single` in any multiplied position.
- `rw`-ing `leftRegular` (which lives on `G →₀ ℂ`) applied to a `MonoidAlgebra`-typed argument
  yields a "not type-correct under instances" motive failure; sidestep by using a `MonoidAlgebra`
  left-multiplication rep, or term-mode `show ... from`.
- `Representation.IndV.mk` is a reducible abbrev, so `simp` unfolds it and breaks `ind_mk`/lemma
  matching; re-fold with `change` after `ext_ring`.
