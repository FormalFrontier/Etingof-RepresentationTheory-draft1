  nonzero rep has scalar `0`) and `Etingof.indecEigenvalue_unique` (uniqueness of the
  eigenvalue).

  `k`-algebra homomorphism `χ_V : Z(A) →ₐ[k] k` (realised as the central character of an
  irreducible subrepresentation `S ≤ V` from Problem 2.3.15) with `ρ(z) - χ_V(z)`
  nilpotent for every `z`. Captures "single eigenvalue = scalar on an irreducible
  subrep" + "χ_V is a homomorphism".

  dual numbers `k[ε]`, the central nilpotent element `ε` acts non-scalarly; with
  `Etingof.epsCenter` witnessing `ε ∈ Z(k[ε])`.
