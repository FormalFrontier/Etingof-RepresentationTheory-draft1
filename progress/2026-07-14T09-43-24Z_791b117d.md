  part (ii): if `B` is degenerate then `Cl(V)` is not semisimple. Proof: a nonzero radical
  vector `v` gives `a = ι v` with `a² = 0` anticommuting with every generator; via the grade
  involution `a * r = involute r * a` (Clifford induction), so `a * r * a = 0`. Hence `a`

  `Ring.jacobson`, while `a = ι v ≠ 0` (injectivity of `ι` in char ≠ 2 via
  `CliffordAlgebra.equivExterior` + `ExteriorAlgebra.ι_eq_zero_iff`) — contradicting
  `IsSemisimpleRing.jacobson_eq_bot`.
