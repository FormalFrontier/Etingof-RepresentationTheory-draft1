## Accomplished

Proved `Etingof.Problem2_7_4.center_charP` sorry-free (issue #6188): in characteristic `p`,
the center of the Weyl algebra equals `k[xᵖ, yᵖ] = Algebra.adjoin k {xᵖ, yᵖ}`.

- `⊇`: `Algebra.adjoin_le` with `x_pow_char_mem_center` / `y_pow_char_mem_center`.
- `⊆`: expand a central `z` in the char-free monomial basis. Centrality gives `ad_x z = 0`
  and `ad_y z = 0`; the coordinate formulas force `(j)·c(i,j) = 0` and `(i)·c(i,j) = 0`, so in
  characteristic `p` every support point `(i,j)` has `p ∣ i` and `p ∣ j`
  (`CharP.cast_eq_zero_iff`). Each such monomial `xⁱyʲ = (xᵖ)^{i/p}(yᵖ)^{j/p}` lies in the
  adjoin, and reassembling `z` from its basis expansion (`Finsupp.sum` + `Subalgebra.sum_mem`)
  puts `z ∈ k[xᵖ, yᵖ]`.
