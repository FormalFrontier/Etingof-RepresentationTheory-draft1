## Notes / patterns discovered

- `fin_cases i` on `i : ZMod 4` yields `⟨k, ⋯⟩` anonymous-constructor forms that do NOT match
  lemmas stated with literals (`a 3` etc.) and break `rw`. Use
  `rcases zmod4_cases i with rfl | rfl | rfl | rfl` (where `zmod4_cases` is proved by
  `revert i; decide`) to substitute genuine literals `0,1,2,3`.
- `decide` needs a metavariable/free-variable-free goal: prove `∀ i, P i` shape with
  `revert i; decide`, not `P i` for a local `i`.
- Group products in `QuaternionGroup 2` (e.g. `a 1 * xa 0 = xa 3`) close by `decide`, but
  hoist them into named `have`s with explicit `(... : QuaternionGroup 2)` ascription — inline
  `show _ from by decide` inside `simp only [...]` under `<;>` triggers "expected type must not
  contain metavariables".
- Concrete `ℂ` identities involving `I` (e.g. `-s = I * (I * s)`) close uniformly with
  `norm_num [Complex.ext_iff]`.
