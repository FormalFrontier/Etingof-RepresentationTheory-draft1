## Accomplished

Proved the highest-weight multiplicity-one fact `schurPoly_coeff_self_ne_zero`
(issue #4949) sorry-free, in `Chapter5/Proposition5_21_1.lean` (its natural home
next to `schurPoly`, `schurPoly_mul_vandermonde`, and `alternant_coeff_kronecker`).

The proof follows the documented leading-monomial strategy in the degree-lexicographic
monomial order. Three new lemmas:

- `degLex_alternant_monomial_le` — among all rearrangements of a strictly-decreasing
  exponent tuple `e`, the sorted tuple is the degLex maximum (min-moved-index lex
  argument: the first index `i₀` where `σ⁻¹` moves a point has `σ⁻¹ i₀ > i₀`, so
  `e (σ⁻¹ i₀) < e i₀`).
- `degLex_degree_alternantMatrix_det` — for strictly-anti `e`, the degLex leading
  monomial of `det (x_i^{e_j})` is `x^e`. Lower bound from `alternant_coeff_kronecker`
  (coeff at `x^e` is `1`); upper bound by expanding the determinant as a sum of signed
  monomials and applying `degree_sum_le` + the lex-maximality lemma.
- `schurPoly_coeff_self_ne_zero` — reads off `degree S_λ = symm λ` from
  `degree (S_λ · Δ) = degree D_{λ+δ}` via `MonomialOrder.degree_mul` (over the field ℚ)
  and additivity `symm (λ+δ) = symm λ + symm δ`, then `coeff_degree_ne_zero_iff`.
