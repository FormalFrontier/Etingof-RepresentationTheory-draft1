### The construction

The route the issue suggested (push the grading back along `FreeLieAlgebra → FreeAlgebra`) needs
PBW, which Mathlib does not have. Instead the grading comes from a **single scaling Lie algebra
homomorphism**, and this turned out to be short:

* `degAlg k = AddMonoidAlgebra k (ℕ × ℕ)`, with monomials `degMon p = single p 1`;
* `degAlg k ⊗[k] FreeLieAlgebra k (Fin 2)` is a `k`-Lie algebra by Mathlib's base change
  (`Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/BaseChange.lean`, `LieAlgebra.ExtendScalars.instLieRing` and
  `instBaseLieAlgebra`);
* `scaleHom = FreeLieAlgebra.lift k (fun i => degMon (genDeg i) ⊗ₜ of k i)` — on paper
  `u ↦ s^(deg_x u) t^(deg_y u) ⊗ u`;
* `homogProj p = tCoeff p ∘ scaleHom`, where `tCoeff p` reads off the coefficient of `degMon p`.

The key trick that keeps this cheap: **do not define `freeDeg p` as the range of `homogProj p`.**
Define it independently as the span of the bracket monomials of bidegree `p` (the inductive
`IsBracketWord`). Then

* `scaleHom u = degMon p ⊗ₜ u` for `u ∈ freeDeg p` (induction on the word), which gives
  orthogonality of the projections *directly*, with no coassociativity / comultiplication
  argument (`homogProj_of_mem`);
* `⨆ p, freeDeg p = ⊤` because that supremum is closed under the bracket (bracket additivity
  of the bidegree) and contains the generators, so it is a Lie subalgebra containing
  `Set.range (of k)`; the project's existing `lieSpan_range_of_eq_top` finishes it.

Independence + spanning is `DirectSum.isInternal_submodule_iff_iSupIndep_and_iSup_eq_top`, giving
`isInternal_freeDeg`, and `IsInternal.chooseDecomposition` gives the `DirectSum.Decomposition`
instance.

Homogeneity of `relIdeal k n` (deliverable 2) is the standard "ideal generated by homogeneous
elements": `relIdealHomog n = {u | ∀ p, homogProj p u ∈ relIdeal n}` is a `LieIdeal` — the
`lie_mem` field expands `⁅v, u⁆` via `sum_lie_sum` over finite bidegree decompositions of `v` and
`u`, and every term `homogProj p ⁅v_q, u_r⁆` is either `⁅v_q, u_r⁆ ∈ relIdeal` or `0` — and it
contains both relators, which are homogeneous of bidegrees `(2,1)` and `(1, n+1)`.

The quotient grading (deliverable 3) is `gDeg n p = (freeDeg p).map (proj n)`, with projections
`gProj n p` obtained from `homogProj p` by `Submodule.liftQ` (well defined exactly because
`relIdeal` is homogeneous), and the same two-line independence/spanning argument.
