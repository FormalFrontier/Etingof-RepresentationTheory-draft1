## Notes for successors

* The mathematical shape of the characteristic-`p` argument is worth reusing for any presented
  algebra with a `p`-central element: `ker` of the nilpotent-or-invertible generator is a
  submodule, the `p`-th power of that generator is central because `p = 0` in `k`, and its
  eigenspaces are submodules, so on an irreducible it is a scalar.
* Two Lean traps cost real iterations here and are now recorded in the `lean-formalization` skill:
  a phantom-parameter carrier needs its representation retyped at the carrier before the generator
  action reduces definitionally, and a `set`-bound vector family zeta-unfolds under `rw`, so
  `smul_smul` silently rewrites inside it. Introducing the family opaquely with `obtain` fixes it.
