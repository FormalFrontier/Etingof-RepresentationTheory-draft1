## Blockers

Key finding for successors: stating the degree-(n+1) homotopy identity as a `restrictScalars k`
LinearMap equality is infeasible for symbolic `n` — `LinearMap.CompatibleSMul (barModule (n+2))
(barModule (n+1)) k A` instance synthesis times out even at 1M heartbeats. The pointwise form
(`barDiff_contraction_homotopy`) avoids this entirely and is equally usable. Prefer pointwise
statements over `restrictScalars` map equalities for barModule maps with symbolic degree.
