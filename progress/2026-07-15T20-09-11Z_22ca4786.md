`Hᵢ(C ⊗ D) ≅ ⨁_{p+q=i} H_p(C) ⊗ H_q(D)`.

In `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter7/KunnethChainComplexNat.lean`:

- Added `import EtingofRepresentationTheory.Chapter7.Problem7_8_7` (the file previously only had
  `import Mathlib`, so the Chapter 7 Künneth result was not in scope).
- New general helper `Etingof.sigmaIsoOfInjOfIsZeroCompl` (with `sigmaSupportHom`/`sigmaSupportInv`
  and the reduction lemmas `ι_sigmaSupportHom`/`ι_sigmaSupportInv`): a **coproduct supported on the
  image of an injection** — if `F j` is a zero object for every `j` outside the image of an
  injective `ι : I → J`, and `F (ι a) ≅ G a`, then `∐ F ≅ ∐ G`. This is the categorical tool the
  issue's step 4 asked for (the extra `ℤ`-antidiagonal summands vanish).
- Filled the `kunnethChainComplexNat` proof following the book route: (1) `homology_extend_iso`
  transports `Hᵢ(C⊗D)` to `H_{-i}(extend (C⊗D))`; (2) apply `H_{-i}` to the crux iso
  `nonempty_tensorObj_extend_iso`; (3) Chapter 7's universe-general `Problem7_8_7_iv` at degree
  `-i`; (4) `sigmaIsoOfInjOfIsZeroCompl` reindexes the `ℤ`-coproduct `⨁_{a+b=-i}` onto the
  `ℕ`-antidiagonal `⨁_{p+q=i}` via `ι (p,q) = (-p,-q)`, with the `a>0`/`b>0` summands killed by
  `homology_extend_isZero`.
