  categories lack infinite coproducts, the general "quotient of a coproduct" route is
  unavailable; instead a Noetherian maximal-image argument. Take the maximal element `m` (ACC)
  of the images of finite maps `P^n ⟶ X` (`biproduct.desc` of finite families of `P ⟶ X`). If
  `m ≠ ⊤`, the cokernel `X ⧸ m` is nonzero, the separator gives a nonzero `g : P ⟶ X ⧸ m`,
  projectivity lifts it to `glift : P ⟶ X` along the epi quotient, and adjoining `glift`
  (`Fin.cons`) yields a strictly larger image — contradiction. Hence `m = ⊤`, i.e. the map is
  epi.
