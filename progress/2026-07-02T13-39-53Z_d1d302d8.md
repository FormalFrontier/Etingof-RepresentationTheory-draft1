## Notes for future agents

- **Instance diamond on doubly-nested submodules**: `↥E` for `E : Submodule ℂ ↥lam2Sub.toSubmodule`
  defaults to `Submodule.addCommMonoid`, but `LinearMap.trace_conj'` needs `AddCommGroup`. The fix
  is `letI : AddCommGroup (↥E) := E.addCommGroup` at the top of the proof — a local high-priority
  instance makes every `↥E` (the intertwiner `e`, the traces) resolve to the `AddCommGroup`-derived
  `AddCommMonoid`, matching `trace_conj'`. Without it you get an "Application type mismatch" on the
  `e` argument (`E.addCommMonoid` vs `AddCommGroup.toAddCommMonoid`).
- Build the intertwiner with `LinearEquiv.ofBijective` of an explicit corestricted `codRestrict`
  map, NOT `Submodule.equivMapOfInjective` (which also hits the diamond).
- For the golden-ratio arithmetic: `norm_num [Q5toC, muPlus, muMinus, chiA5, cons-lemmas,
  Q5.*_re/_im]` reduces the class-`j` goals (it closes the all-zero class 1 outright); the two
  5-cycle classes then need `linear_combination (±10) * sqrt5_sq`. `simp only` does NOT reduce
  the `OfNat`/`Neg` `Q5` projections — use `norm_num`.
