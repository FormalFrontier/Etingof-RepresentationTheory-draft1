## Key decision
Used the sign convention `χ̃_V(g) = (χ_V(1)/|G|)·χ_V(g)` (the central idempotent attached
to the **dual** `V*`). With this normalization the book's recovery formula holds exactly:
`χ̃_V(1) = χ_V(1)²/|G|`, so `√(|G|/χ̃_V(1)) = |G|/χ_V(1)` and the witness is `c = |G|/χ_V(1)`.
This keeps every stated theorem a *true* (non-vacuous) statement.
