## Decisions

The family is presented concretely as quotients `A ⧸ M` by maximal left ideals rather than as
an abstract `∃ ι, ∃ V : ι → Type u, ...` bundle. This keeps every needed instance
(`Module k`, `IsScalarTower k A`, `FiniteDimensional k`, `IsSimpleModule A`) inferable and
makes the result usable without unpacking a pile of existentially quantified instances.

Existence is proved by a maximum-cardinality argument rather than by exhibiting a
canonical set: `Etingof.card_irreducibles_le_finrank` bounds every pairwise-nonisomorphic
finite family by `finrank k A`, so `Nat.findGreatest` picks a family of maximum cardinality,
and maximality forces exhaustiveness (an omitted irreducible could be adjoined via `insert`,
contradicting maximality). This avoids needing finiteness of the type of maximal left ideals.
