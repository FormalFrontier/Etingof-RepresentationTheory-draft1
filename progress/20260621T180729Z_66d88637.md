### Key gotcha (cost ~5 build iterations)

The commutation proof must be **fully syntactic**. The natural
`exact AlgHom.congr_fun (lTransAlgHom_comp_rTransAlgHom ↑g ↑h) f` blows up `whnf`
(even at 6.4M heartbeats): comparing the `Module.End`-multiplied bi-action endos
against the comp form forces reduction through the underlying `MvPolynomial.aeval`s.
Fix: `LinearMap.ext` (not `ext`, which over-applies into `MvPolynomial` coeff
extensionality), then `rw [Module.End.mul_apply, …, polyLeftRep_apply,
polyRightRep_apply]` (all `rfl`-lemmas) to reach the applied form, and discharge
by `rw [AlgHom.comp_apply, AlgHom.comp_apply] at h2; exact h2` rather than an
`exact` that triggers the expensive defeq. With the syntactic route the proof
needs **no** `maxHeartbeats` bump (default budget, 8s file build).
