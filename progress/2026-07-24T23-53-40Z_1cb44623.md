* `Xlin_pow_char` (`Xᵖ = α·1`) and `Ylin_pow_char` (`Yᵖ = cᵖ·1`) — the central-scalar actions.
* `exists_unique_pow_char` — Frobenius is a bijection, so `(α,c) ↦ (α,cᵖ)` enumerates central

## Decisions

* Parameterized the family by `(α, c)` with `yᵖ` acting by `cᵖ`, rather than by the central
  character `(α, β)` directly. Frobenius is bijective over an algebraically closed field of
  characteristic `p`, so this is the same indexing set; `c` is what actually appears in the
  operator, so this avoids threading a `p`-th-root choice function through every definition.
  `exists_unique_pow_char` records the translation.
* `Fin p` has no ambient `NatCast`; `open scoped Fin.NatCast` is required to write `(t : Fin p)`.
  `ring` does not work on `Fin p` goals here, `abel` does.
