## Accomplished

Implemented issue #4885 (sub-A of #4875): the algebra-side Dedekind/Artin lemma that
trace-character functionals of pairwise non-isomorphic finite-dimensional simple modules are
linearly independent over the base field. New file
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter5/CharacterIndependence.lean`, sorry-free, axioms limited to
`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`.

Key deliverable:
`Etingof.traceCharacter_linearIndependent` — for a family `M : ι → Type` of `A`-modules
(`A` a `𝕜`-algebra, `𝕜` a characteristic-zero field, each `M i` finite-dimensional over `𝕜`,
simple as an `A`-module, pairwise non-isomorphic), the functionals
`traceChar M i : A →ₗ[𝕜] 𝕜`, `a ↦ trace_𝕜 (a • · : M i → M i)`, are `𝕜`-linearly independent.

Proof route (Jacobson density / bicommutant, no algebraic closedness needed):
- `CharacterIndependence.offDiag_eq_zero`: Schur's lemma (`LinearMap.bijective_or_eq_zero`) gives
  `Hom_A(M i, M k) = 0` for `i ≠ k`.
- `CharacterIndependence.proj_comm`: hence the projection of `𝕄 = ∀ i, M i` onto its `j`-th factor
  commutes with every `A`-endomorphism, i.e. lies in the bicommutant `End_{End_A 𝕄}(𝕄)`.
- `CharacterIndependence.exists_separating`: `A` surjects onto that bicommutant
  (`Module.Finite.toModuleEnd_moduleEnd_surjective`), producing `a ∈ A` acting as `id` on `M j`
  and `0` on the others.
- Testing a vanishing combination against the separating element for `j` gives
  `g j · finrank 𝕜 (M j) = 0`; `finrank ≠ 0` in char zero closes it.

The lemma is stated more generally than the issue requested (any char-zero field, not just `ℂ`),
which strictly subsumes the `ℂ` use case for the assembly issue #4887.

Added the file to the `Chapter5.lean` aggregator.
