* `pointCoords` / `pointOfCoords`: the bijection `W(m) ≃ (WIdx m → k)` identifying the
  representation space with affine space `𝔸ᴺ`.
* `IsAlgDense`: the book's coefficientwise notion of Zariski-dense.
* `exists_isAlgDense_orbit`: **finitely many orbits ⟹ an algebraically dense orbit**.
  Product-of-witnesses argument: if no orbit were dense, the product of one nonzero
  vanishing witness per orbit (finite, by `Fintype.ofFinite` on the orbit quotient) is a
  nonzero polynomial vanishing on all of `𝔸ᴺ`, contradicting `MvPolynomial.funext` over
  the infinite field.
* `evalAt g : B →ₐ[k] k`: per-group-element evaluation of the principal-open coordinate
  ring, lifted across the `detProd⁻¹` localization via `IsLocalization.liftAlgHom`
  (`detProd` evaluates to a product of nonzero `det (g i)`, a unit).
* `evalAt_map_genMatB` / `evalAt_map_genMatBInv`: `evalAt g` sends the generic matrices to
  the actual `↑(g i)` and `↑(g i)⁻¹` (inverse matched via `Matrix.inv_eq_right_inv`,
  avoiding `Ring.inverse`).
* `evalAt_comp_orbitComorphism`: the evaluation identity
  `evalAt g ∘ orbitComorphism v₀ = aeval (pointCoords (g • v₀))` (rectangular matrices, so
  `Matrix.map` + `Matrix.map_mul`, not square `mapMatrix`).
* `injective_orbitComorphism_of_isAlgDense` and `exists_injective_orbitComorphism`: the
  deliverable — for finitely many orbits over an infinite field, a base point whose
  orbit-map comorphism is injective.
