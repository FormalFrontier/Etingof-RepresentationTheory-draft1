injection `⟨i, p⟩` (`p ∘ i = id`) of base changes over an **arbitrary** field extension

generated `K`-subalgebra `R ⊆ L`:

```lean
theorem exists_fg_subalgebra_baseChange_directSummand
    [FiniteDimensional K V] [FiniteDimensional K W]
    (i : (L ⊗[K] V) →ₗ[L ⊗[K] A] (L ⊗[K] W)) (p : (L ⊗[K] W) →ₗ[L ⊗[K] A] (L ⊗[K] V))
    (hpi : p.comp i = LinearMap.id) :
    ∃ R : Subalgebra K L, R.FG ∧
      ∃ (i' : (R ⊗[K] V) →ₗ[R ⊗[K] A] (R ⊗[K] W))
        (p' : (R ⊗[K] W) →ₗ[R ⊗[K] A] (R ⊗[K] V)), p'.comp i' = LinearMap.id
```

## Key patterns / decisions

- **Direct mirror of the iso descent proof.** The proof is the split-injection analog of the
  merged `exists_fg_subalgebra_baseChange_iso` in the same file: choose `K`-bases of `V`, `W`,
  read off the matrix entries `c` (of `i`) and `d` (of `p`) over the base-changed bases, take
  `R = Algebra.adjoin K (entries)`, rebuild `i'R`, `p'R` over `↥R` via `Basis.constr`.
- **One relation instead of two.** The iso case descends `e.symm ∘ e = id` *and* `e ∘ e.symm = id`;
  here only `p ∘ i = id` is needed. It descends via the injective flat coefficient inclusion
  `incV := LinearMap.rTensor V RA.val.toLinearMap` (`Module.Flat.rTensor_preserves_injective_linearMap`,
  `V` free over the field `K`): `incV (p'R (i'R x)) = p (incW (i'R x)) = p (i (incV x)) = incV x`
  using the two intertwining identities `int_i`, `int_p` and `hpi`, then cancel `incV`.
- **Upgrading `R`-linear maps to `R ⊗[K] A`-linear.** Both `i'R`, `p'R` are built `↥R`-linear by
  `constr`; each is packaged into a `→ₗ[↥R ⊗[K] A]` map by a hand-written structure whose
  `map_smul'` reduces `(t ⊗ₜ a) • ·` to `(t ⊗ₜ 1) * (1 ⊗ₜ a)` and combines `↥R`-linearity
  (`smul_tmul_one`) with `A`-equivariance (`i_Aequiv`/`p_Aequiv`) — same recipe as the iso
  packaging and `Functoriality.pushHom`.
- **Final relation by defeq.** With `i'`, `p'` `let`-bound, `p'.comp i' = LinearMap.id` follows
  from `LinearMap.ext` + `change p'R (i'R x) = x` + the descended `split` lemma (the `change`
  zeta/proj-reduces through the structure coercions; `simp`/`simpa` did not fire on the `let`).
- **maxHeartbeats.** Needs `set_option maxHeartbeats 1600000 in` (double the iso lemma's 800000)
  — the extra map upgrades push elaboration past the iso lemma's budget.
