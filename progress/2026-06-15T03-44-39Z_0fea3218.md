  `Σ adjᵢⱼ mᵢmⱼ < 2 Σ mᵢ²`, i.e. `dim W < dim G` ⟺ `dim W ≤ dim G − 1`.
- `titsForm_posDef_of_cone` (core) — extend positivity of `xᵀ(2·I−adj)x` from the
  nonzero nonnegative integer cone to *all* nonzero integer vectors via the `|·|`
  trick (`adjᵢⱼ(|xᵢ||xⱼ| − xᵢxⱼ) ≥ 0`). Only `adjᵢⱼ ≥ 0` is needed; no ℝ-density
  argument, since `IsDynkinDiagram` quantifies positivity over **ℤ**.
- `isDynkinDiagram_of_cone` (packaged) — graph-shape clauses + cone hypothesis ⟹
  `Etingof.IsDynkinDiagram n adj`. Ready for the final wiring in S4 (#4786).

over `Fin n → ℤ`, not ℝ. So directive step 4 (extend to ℝⁿ via density of ℚ) is
unnecessary for this definition — steps 1–3 suffice.
