Landed **reusable shared infrastructure** (all sorry-free) that the n=2/n=3 cases consume:

- `lieSpan_range_of_eq_top` — the free Lie algebra on `Fin 2` is generated by its two
  generators (corestriction + `FreeLieAlgebra.hom_ext`).
- `proj : Free →ₗ⁅k⁆ g k n`, `proj_apply`, `proj_surjective`, `proj_eq_zero_iff`.
- `xb`/`yb`/`zb` generator images, `zb_eq`, `lieSpan_gens_eq_top` (𝔤ₙ Lie-generated by `x̄, ȳ`).
- `relator1_mem`, `lie_xb_zb` (relation `[x̄,[x̄,ȳ]] = 0`, all `n`).
- Two proof patterns: **linear independence** via a Lie hom into `gl₃(k)` (`x↦E₀₁, y↦E₁₂`,
  both relators ↦ 0, images independent) — `matHom`/`indep_one`; and **spanning** via a
  `Submodule.span_induction₂` closure of `span{x̄,ȳ,z̄}` combined with `lieSpan_gens_eq_top` —
  `span_eq_top_one`. Basis assembled with `Module.Basis.mk`.

Key technical notes for successors:
- Use `attribute [local instance] LieRing.ofAssociativeRing` to view `Matrix (Fin m) (Fin m) k`
  as a Lie algebra; fully type-annotate elementary matrices (define `E01/E12/... : Matrix ...`)
  to avoid index literals defaulting to `ℕ`.
- `Basis` is `Module.Basis` in this Mathlib (v4.32.0-rc1); import `Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.Basis.Basic`.
- Matrix commutators compute cleanly via `LieRing.of_associative_ring_bracket` +
  `Matrix.single_mul_single_same` / `single_mul_single_of_ne` (simp discharges `Fin` index ≠).
