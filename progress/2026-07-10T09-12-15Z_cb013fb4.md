Proof structure (matches the issue's 4-step sketch):
1. **Helper `exists_monic_irreducible_smul_ne_top`** — from a nonzero `v`, its annihilator
   `ker (toSpanSingleton k[x] P v)` is a proper nonzero ideal `(g)` (nonzero because `k[x]` is
   not finite-dim over `k`, `Polynomial.not_finite`); a monic irreducible factor `p ∣ g`
   (`Polynomial.exists_monic_irreducible_factor`) kills `w = (g/p)•v ≠ 0`, so mult-by-`p` is
   non-injective hence (finite dim, `LinearMap.injective_iff_surjective`) `p • ⊤ ≠ ⊤`.
2. `P/(p•P)` is a nonzero `K = k[x]/(p)`-vector space (`Module.isTorsionBy_quotient_element_smul`);
   a basis coordinate (`Module.Basis.ofVectorSpace` + `forall_coord_eq_zero_iff`) gives a nonzero
   `k[x]`-linear `φ : P → K`, built by hand (its `map_smul'` bridges `k[x]`- and `K`-actions via
   `Module.IsTorsionBy.mk_smul` and `Algebra.smul_def`/`Ideal.Quotient.algebraMap_eq`).
3. Lift `φ` through the reduction `k[x]/(p^N) ↠ k[x]/(p)` (`Ideal.Quotient.factorₐ`,
   `factor_surjective`) with `N = dim_k P + 1`.
4. `red (h y₀) = φ y₀ ≠ 0` ⟹ a preimage `a` of `h y₀` is coprime to `p^N`
   (`Irreducible.coprime_iff_not_dvd` + `.pow_right`), so `h y₀` is a **unit** ⟹ `h` surjective
   ⟹ `dim_k P ≥ dim_k (k[x]/(p^N)) = N·deg p ≥ N > dim_k P` (`finrank_quotient_span_eq_natDegree`,
   `LinearMap.finrank_le_finrank_of_surjective`), contradiction.
