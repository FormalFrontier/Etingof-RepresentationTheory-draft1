## Accomplished

Proved `Etingof.Problem2_14_3.exists_tensorHom_adjunction` (issue #6217) — the tensor-Hom
adjunction `Hom_𝔤(V ⊗ W, U) ≅ Hom_𝔤(V, U ⊗ W*)` for finite-dimensional Lie-algebra
representations. `Chapter2/Problem2_14_3.lean` is now sorry-free.

Construction (statement unchanged from the issue):
- `dualHomEquiv : (W →ₗ[k] U) ≃ₗ⁅k,L⁆ U ⊗ W*` — a Lie-module equivalence built from Mathlib's
  `dualTensorHomEquiv k W U` (an equiv since `W` is finite dimensional) and `TensorProduct.comm`;
  the content is a `map_lie'` check that `u ⊗ φ ↦ (w ↦ φ(w) • u)` is 𝔤-equivariant.
- `congrHomRight` — postcomposition by a Lie-module equivalence, giving the `k`-linear iso of Hom
  spaces `(V →ₗ⁅k,L⁆ B) ≃ₗ[k] (V →ₗ⁅k,L⁆ C)`.
- Final equivalence: `(TensorProduct.LieModule.liftLie k L V W U).symm.trans (congrHomRight dualHomEquiv)`.

Also added a `lean-formalization` skill entry documenting (a) the assembly recipe and (b) the
`.toFun`-blocks-`simp` gotcha when writing `map_lie'` via structure-spread `{ e with map_lie' := … }`
(prepend `LinearMap.toFun_eq_coe, LinearEquiv.coe_coe` to the `simp only` set).
