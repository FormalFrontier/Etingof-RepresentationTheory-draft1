Mathematical content: a cyclic module over a PID has projective dimension `≤ 1`.

**Ext side (clean route).** Build the length-`1` free resolution `0 → R →(·a) R → R/(a) → 0`
in `ModuleCat R` by hand (`ModuleCat.shortComplexOfCompEqZero` + `shortComplex_shortExact`),
then `hasProjectiveDimensionLT_two_of_shortExact` (new helper wrapping
`ShortComplex.ShortExact.hasProjectiveDimensionLT_X₃` with both free terms projective) gives
`HasProjectiveDimensionLT (R/(a)) 2`, and `HasProjectiveDimensionLT.subsingleton` kills `Extⁱ`
for `i ≥ 2`. `a = 0` handled separately (`R/(0) ≅ R` free).

**Tor side (harder — opposite ring).** `Etingof.Tor` resolves the right module in
`ModuleCat Rᵐᵒᵖ`, so I hand-build the resolution over `Rᵐᵒᵖ` (`zmodMopResolution`,
`polyMopResolution`) with `Rᵐᵒᵖ`-linear `·a` and quotient maps, feed it to
`Etingof.Functor.leftDerived_sixTerm_exact` (already in the repo), and squeeze the middle `Tor`
between the two vanishing free-term `Tor` via a new `isZero_obj_two_of_sixTerm_exact` helper
(`ShortComplex.Exact.isZero_X₂`). `a = 0`/`f = 0` handled by an explicit `Rᵐᵒᵖ`-linear
equiv to the free module (`zmodZeroOpEquiv`, `polyZeroOpEquiv`).
