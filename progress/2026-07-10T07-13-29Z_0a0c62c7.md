### Proof strategy (self-contained, reuses only part (i))

Rather than assemble semisimplicity / "determined by all simples" machinery, I derived the
two coefficient identities directly from part (i) via a trace:

- `trace_rho_comp_psi V W x`: `tr(V.ρ x ∘ ψ_W|_V) = (dim W/|G|) · ∑_g χ_W(g) χ_V(x g⁻¹)`
  (expand `asAlgebraHom_psi`, push the composition through `Module.End` mul, linearity of
  trace, `character = trace ∘ ρ`).
- `conv_self` / `conv_other`: substitute `psi_acts_self` (`= id`) resp. `psi_acts_other`
  (`= 0`) into that trace, giving `(dim V/|G|) ∑_g χ_V g χ_V(x g⁻¹) = χ_V x` and the
  off-diagonal `… = 0`.
- `psi_mul_psi_eq A B`: expand `ψ_A · ψ_B` (`single_mul_single`, a per-`a` conjugation
  `Equiv` reindex `b ↦ a⁻¹ b⁻¹`, `Finset.sum_comm`) into
  `∑_k (c_A · (c_B · ∑_a χ_A a χ_B(k⁻¹ a⁻¹))) • single k 1`.
- `psi_eq`: re-index `ψ_V` as `c_V • ∑_k χ_V(k⁻¹) • single k 1`.

Idempotence and orthogonality then follow by rewriting the per-`k` coefficient with the
matching convolution identity (`conv_self` → `χ_V(k⁻¹)`, `conv_other` → `0`).
