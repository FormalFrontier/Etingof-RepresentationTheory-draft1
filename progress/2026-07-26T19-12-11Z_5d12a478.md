General essential-image results (stated at the generality they hold in, for a fully faithful
functor between arbitrary categories):

* `Etingof.essImage_prop_of_isLimit` / `Etingof.essImage_prop_of_isColimit`: a (co)limit of a
  diagram valued in the essential image of a fully faithful `F` lies in the essential image,
  provided `F` preserves (co)limits of that shape. The proof lifts the diagram through
  `Functor.essImage.liftFunctor` and transports the preserved (co)limit along
  `Functor.essImage.liftFunctorCompIso`.
* Instances derived from those: `IsClosedUnderLimitsOfShape`, `IsClosedUnderColimitsOfShape`,
  `IsClosedUnderFiniteProducts`, `IsClosedUnderFiniteCoproducts`, `ContainsZero`,
  `IsClosedUnderKernels`, `IsClosedUnderCokernels` for `F.essImage`.
* `Etingof.essImage_image`: closure under images, obtained from closure under kernels and
  cokernels via `Abelian.imageIsoImage`.

Definition 7.7.1 proper:

* `Etingof.moduleEmbeddingRing`, `Etingof.moduleEmbedding`, `Etingof.moduleRealization`: the ring
  `A`, the Freyd-Mitchell embedding `C ⥤ ModuleCat A`, and the full subcategory of `A`-mod it cuts
  out (its essential image).
* `Etingof.moduleRealizationEquiv : C ≌ (moduleRealization C).FullSubcategory` and
  `Etingof.moduleRealizationEquivCompι`, the comparison with the inclusion functor.
* `Etingof.exists_equiv_moduleCat_fullSubcategory`: the packaged source statement. For every
  abelian `C` there are a ring `A` and a full subcategory `P` of `A`-mod containing a zero object,
  closed under isomorphism, finite products, finite coproducts, kernels, cokernels and images,
  with `C ≌ P.FullSubcategory`.
* `Etingof.abelianOfClosedFullSubcategory`: the converse, re-exposing Mathlib's
  `Abelian/Subcategory` instance.
