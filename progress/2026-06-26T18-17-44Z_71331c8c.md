Replaced it with the genuine bijection from the free algebra's universal property:
`FreeAlgebra.lift k : (Fin n → Module.End k V) ≃ (FreeAlgebra k (Fin n) →ₐ[k] Module.End k V)`.
A representation of an algebra `A` on `V` is exactly an algebra hom `A →ₐ[k] Module.End k V`,
so this `Equiv` *is* the claimed bijection `ρ ↔ (ρ(xᵢ))ᵢ`, for general `n` (not just `Fin 3`).
Added two unfolding examples making the correspondence concrete in both directions:
`(lift k).symm ρ i = ρ (ι k i)` and `lift k f (ι k i) = f i`.
