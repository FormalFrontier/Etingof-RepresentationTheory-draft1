## Next step

The issue's suggested route (deliverable 4 via "the `n` trace forms are linearly independent")
is only valid over a **splitting field** — over a non-splitting field an individual `τ_S` can be
identically zero, so no size-`n` independent family exists (`Exercise4_2_3_FieldGeneral.lean`
documents this). The field-general strict bound must go through **base change**:
`#(IrrepClasses k G) ≤ #(IrrepClasses K G) < #(ConjClasses G)` with `K = AlgebraicClosure k`.
- The `≤` is `natCard_irrepClasses_le_of_ringHom_field` (#6127, in progress).
- The strict `<` over algebraically closed `K` feeds the size-`n` independent trace-form family
  (the split-field Wedderburn content of #6126) into the new
  `card_lt_conjClasses_of_traceForm_linearIndependent`.
There is also an **import-cycle obstruction**: `Exercise4_2_3.lean` cannot import
`FieldGeneral`/`StrictBound`, so the assembled `Exercise4_2_3` theorem must move to a downstream
file. All of this is spelled out in **#6139** (depends-on #6126, #6127).
