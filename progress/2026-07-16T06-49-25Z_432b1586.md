Found and fixed a **signature defect**, and landed the **positive-degree half** of the proof.

- **Signature fix.** `extTensorProjectiveResolution` was declared over `[CommRing k]`, but its
  `quasiIso` field is *false* over a general commutative ring — it is the vanishing of
  `Tor_{>0}^k(M₁, M₂)`, nonzero already for `k = ℤ`, `M₁ = M₂ = ℤ/2` (there
  `Tor_1^ℤ(ℤ/2, ℤ/2) = ℤ/2`). Tightened the section to `[Field k]` (no downstream consumer of the
  resolution exists yet, so this is safe) and documented the counterexample in the module docstring.

- **`restrictScalars_preservesHomology`** — restriction of scalars along any ring hom (incl.
  noncommutative target `Aᵢᵐᵒᵖ`) preserves homology. The `ChangeOfRingsExact` instances only cover
  *commutative* target rings; used the left-adjoint route instead (`restrictCoextendScalarsAdj` ⟹
  preserves cokernels; `restrictScalars` preserves monos + zero morphisms ⟹
  `preservesHomology_of_preservesMonos_and_cokernels`).

- **`homology_mapHomologicalComplex_projective_isZero_succ`** — a homology-preserving functor sends
  a projective resolution to a complex acyclic in positive degrees (via the mapped quasi-iso `Q.π`
  and `isZero_single_obj_homology`).

- **`homology_tensorObj_res_isZero_succ`** — the positive-degree acyclicity:
  `IsZero ((tensorObj (res₁Complex P₁) (res₂Complex P₂)).homology (n + 1))`, via
  `kunnethChainComplexNat` (every Künneth summand vanishes since one index is positive).

All three new lemmas are sorry-free (`#print axioms`: `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`).
`lake build …ExternalTensorResolution` succeeds; the only remaining `sorry` is the `quasiIso` field.
