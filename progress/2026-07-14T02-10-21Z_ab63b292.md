## Accomplished

Discharged the `sorry` at `tracelessSymSub_irreducible` in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter4/Problem4_12_11.lean` (issue #6547): the 5-dimensional
`SO(3)`-representation `W = tracelessSymSub` (traceless symmetric `3×3` matrices under
`conjRep A M = A M Aᵀ`) is irreducible over ℝ, sorry-free. The proof follows the planned
spectral-theorem-free route:

- **`wbasis : Fin 5 → EndV`** + `wbasis_mem` + `traceless_sym_decomp` (the 5-vector basis and
  entrywise decomposition, mirroring the landed `sbasis`/`skew_decomp` from #6539/PR #6549).
- **V4 sign-averaging projections** `projA/projB/projC`: for `N ∈ U`,
  `N01•w0 = ¼(N − Dx·N − Dy·N + Dz·N) ∈ U` (and the `w1`, `w2` analogues). Proved via the
  character lemmas `conjRep_{Dx,Dy,Dz}_{w0..w4}` (15 concrete-matrix `simp` lemmas) plus
  `map_add`/`map_smul` and `module` — this sidesteps the fragile `star`/`vecMul` expansion that
  fails when the conjugated matrix is a variable.
- **Cyclic spread** via `Pc` (`conjRep_Pc_{w0,w1,w2,w3}`): `w0 ∈ U ⟹ w1, w2 ∈ U` and `w3 ⟹ w4`.
- **45° rotations** `Rz45`, `Ry45 ∈ SO(3)` (with `c45 = √2/2`, `c45_sq : c45*c45 = 1/2`);
  `conjRep_Rz45_w0 = -w3` bridges off-diagonal to diagonal, and
  `conjRep_{Rz45,Ry45}_{w3,w4}` handle the purely-diagonal case (`Rz45` when `M00≠M11`,
  `Ry45` when `M00=M11`, producing a nonzero off-diagonal component to bootstrap `w0`).

Key pattern discovered: for `conjRep D (variable matrix)` identities, decompose the variable via
`traceless_sym_decomp`, push `conjRep` through with `map_add`/`map_smul` + the concrete
`conjRep D (wbasis k)` character lemmas, then close with `module`. Direct entrywise `simp`
(`conjRep_apply` + `Matrix.mul_apply` + `star`) does NOT reduce when the middle factor is a free
variable — `star`/`vecMul` are left un-evaluated.
