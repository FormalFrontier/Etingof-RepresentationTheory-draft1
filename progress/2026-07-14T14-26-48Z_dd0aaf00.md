## Accomplished

Feature session on **#6587** (Ch8 flatness crux: tensoring with a projective right module is
exact). Created `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter8/TensorProjectiveExact.lean` and wired it into
the `Chapter8.lean` aggregator. Landed the following **sorry-free, reusable** infrastructure:

- `shortExact_of_retract` — `ShortComplex.ShortExact` is stable under retracts (general, `[Abelian D]`;
  mono/epi via `MorphismProperty.of_retract`, exactness via a retract of homology + IsZero).
- `unitorEquiv`/`unitorNatIso` — the left unitor `Aᵐᵒᵖ ⊗_A N ≅ N`, and the natural iso
  `tensorLeftFunctor A (of Aᵐᵒᵖ) ≅ forget₂ (ModuleCat A) AddCommGrpCat`.
- `unit_map_shortExact` — **unit case**: tensoring a SES with the regular module `Aᵐᵒᵖ` is short exact
  (transfer along `unitorNatIso` + `forget₂` exactness via `map_of_exact`).
- `tensorLeftNatTrans`, `tensorBifunctor` — functoriality of `tensorLeftFunctor A -` in the right
  module argument (bifunctor `ModuleCat Aᵐᵒᵖ ⥤ (ModuleCat A ⥤ AddCommGrpCat)`).
- `mapRetract` — a module retract `P ◁ F` induces a short-complex retract
  `S.map (tensorLeftFunctor A P) ◁ S.map (tensorLeftFunctor A F)`.

The **main theorem** `tensorLeftFunctor_map_shortExact` (P projective ⇒ `P ⊗_A -` preserves short
exactness) is fully assembled: P projective is a retract of the free module `↑P →₀ Aᵐᵒᵖ` (counit
of the free/forget adjunction, `counit_epi_of_R_faithful` + `Projective.factorThru`), and short
exactness transfers from the free case via `mapRetract` + `shortExact_of_retract`.
