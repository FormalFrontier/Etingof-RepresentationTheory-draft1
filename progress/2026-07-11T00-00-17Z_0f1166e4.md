  `d∘d=0` proofs in #6336 will need.

Key patterns: A-linearity of the differential comes for free from `AlgebraTensorModule.lift`
because the leading `A`-factor is only ever left-multiplied; `LinearMap.mk₂`'s H4 slot wants
`c • a • w = a • c • w` orientation (used `(smul_comm a c w).symm`); `simp only` needs
`LinearEquiv.coe_toLinearMap` before `TensorProduct.assoc_tmul` will fire on `.toLinearMap`-coerced
`assoc`.
