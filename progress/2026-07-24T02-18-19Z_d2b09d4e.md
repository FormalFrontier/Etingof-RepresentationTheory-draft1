## Accomplished

Closed issue #7637 (the crux gap blocking `IsFiniteAbelianCategory (FGModuleCat A)`).
Added `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Infrastructure/SimpleModuleFamily.lean` (+ `_Test.lean`)
constructing, for a finite-dimensional `k`-algebra `A`, the complete finite family of simple
`A`-modules:

* `Etingof.exists_simpleModule_family` — a `Fin n`-indexed family of simple submodules
  `S i ⊆ A ⧸ rad A`, each `Module.Finite k`, `IsSimpleModule A ↥(S i)`, together with
  completeness: every simple `A`-module `M` satisfies `∃ i, Nonempty (M ≃ₗ[A] ↥(S i))`.
* `Etingof.exists_fgModuleCat_simple_family` — the same data as objects of `FGModuleCat A`
  (`ι = Fin n`, `simpleObj = FGModuleCat.of A ↥(S i)`, categorical `Simple`, categorical
  `iso_of_simple`), directly feeding the `ι / simpleObj / simple_simpleObj / iso_of_simple`
  fields of `Etingof.IsFiniteAbelianCategory`.
* Supporting categorical bridges (over any `IsNoetherianRing A`):
  `simple_fgModuleCat_of_isSimpleModule` and `isSimpleModule_of_simple_fgModuleCat`.

Build is clean (`sorry`-free; only the pre-existing project-wide header-copyright style
warning, identical to `SimpleModuleCount.lean` / `IrreducibleEnumeration.lean`).

## Current frontier

Key proof techniques (validated end-to-end):
* `A` fin-dim over field ⇒ `IsArtinianRing.of_finite k A` ⇒ `IsSemiprimaryRing` ⇒
  `IsSemisimpleRing (A ⧸ Ring.jacobson A)` (automatic instances).
* Cross-ring semisimple transfer: `LinearMap.isSemisimpleModule_iff_of_bijective` on the
  `σ`-semilinear identity (`σ = Ideal.Quotient.mk`) makes `A ⧸ rad A` a semisimple
  `A`-module.
* Completeness via `IsSemisimpleModule.jacobson_le_annihilator` + cyclic surjection
  `A ↠ M` factored through `A ⧸ rad A` (`Submodule.liftQ`) + `DFinsupp.lhom_ext'` to find a
  nonzero component + Schur (`LinearMap.bijective_of_ne_zero`).
* `FGModuleCat`↔`ModuleCat` `Simple` transfer: `forget₂` is `Full`/`Faithful` and, over a
  Noetherian ring, `PreservesMonomorphisms` (it creates finite limits), so a ported
  reflection lemma handles `IsSimpleModule → Simple`, and a submodule-inclusion-mono argument
  handles `Simple → IsSimpleModule`.
