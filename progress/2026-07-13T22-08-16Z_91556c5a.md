## Accomplished

Feature session on issue #6526 (Ch9 cons-splitting, deliverables 2-3 of the standard
resolution length grading). Landed the **combinatorial / basis-level core** of the
cons-splitting `A_n ⊗_S V ≅ A_{n+1}` in a new file
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter9/PathAlgebraConsSplitting.lean` (sorry-free), registered
in the `Chapter9` aggregator:

- `exists_cons_decomp` — a path of length `n+1` factors as a length-`n` initial path followed
  by one final arrow (via Mathlib `Quiver.Path.length_ne_zero_iff_eq_cons`).
- `cons_decomp_unique` — that factorization is unique (middle vertex, initial path, final
  arrow), via `Quiver.Path.cons.injEq`.
- `exists_ofPath_mul_arrowElt` — every length-`(n+1)` basis path of `A` equals
  `ofPath p * arrowElt e` for its unique decomposition (surjectivity of concatenation onto
  degree `n+1`).
- `ofPath_mul_arrowElt_inj` — concatenation is injective on composable (path, arrow) pairs
  (the `Mono` content at the basis level).

Together with the appending seed `ofPath_mul_arrowElt` (already on main in
`PathAlgebraLengthGrading.lean`), these give the full index/basis-level bijection between
length-`(n+1)` paths and composable (length-`n` path, arrow) pairs — the genuine inverse data
the bundled `≃ₗ` will rest on.
