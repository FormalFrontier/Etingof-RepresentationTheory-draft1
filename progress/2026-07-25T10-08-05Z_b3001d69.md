## Current frontier

The single remaining obstruction to the unconditional spanning statement is the vanishing of
`topDefect k m` for `m ≥ 2`, i.e. `⁅⁅a₁, a₃⁆, D (evenTower k m)⁆ = 0`. That is exactly #7759
(`Injective (gbar k)`), which is blocked on #7764 (the `ℕ²`-bidegree grading, PR #7767 open).

The workable alternative recorded in #7750 — proving `⁅a₄, oddTop c⁆ ∈ span k {c'', D c'', D² c''}`
instead of its exact value — does not avoid the gap: by `EvenLayer.defect_eq` that membership is
equivalent to the defect lying in the same three-dimensional layer, which is not available from
the commutation relations. What *is* available unconditionally is the weaker
`span (range loopFam₄) + Z(𝔤₄) = ⊤`, which is what `span_loopSet_eq_top` records.
