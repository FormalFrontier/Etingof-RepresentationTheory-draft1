## Accomplished

Fixed the fidelity gap in `Chapter4/Definition4.10.1` (issue #5620).

- **Re-confirmed the finding.** The book (`blobs/Chapter4/Definition4.10.1.md`)
  defines `X_G` with entries `a_{ij} = x_{g_i g_j}` (forward product). The Lean
  definition `Etingof.FrobeniusDeterminant` used `x_{g_i g_j⁻¹}`, which is in fact
  the matrix of the regular-representation operator `L(x) = ∑ x_g ρ(g)`, not the
  book's `X_G`. The book's own proof of Theorem 4.10.2 distinguishes the two and
  notes they agree "up to sign" (column permutation `h ↦ h⁻¹`). So the gap was real.

- **Definition (`Definition4_10_1.lean`).** Changed the entries to
  `MvPolynomial.X (g * h)`, matching the book exactly. Updated the module/decl
  docstrings to record the relationship to the operator matrix and the sign.

- **Theorem 4.10.2 (`Theorem4_10_2.lean`).** Renamed
  `frobeniusDet_eq_prod` → `frobeniusDet_eq_signSmul_prod` and reworked it to prove
  `FrobeniusDeterminant = sign(inv) • ∏ blockPoly^d`, where `sign(inv)` is the sign
  of the inversion permutation `Equiv.inv G` (a `±1` unit in `k`). The new step uses
  `Matrix.det_permute'` on the column permutation. The main theorem then absorbs the
  sign into the irreducible factors: since `k` is algebraically closed it has a
  `d₀`-th root `c` of the sign, and scaling the `0`-th block polynomial by `C c`
  (a unit, preserving irreducibility, non-association, and total degree) yields the
  exact equality `det X_G = ∏ P_j^{deg P_j}`. This mirrors the book, where the `P_j`
  are only defined up to proportionality.
