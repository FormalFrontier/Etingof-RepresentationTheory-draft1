## Accomplished

Closed the theorem-evidence gap in `Chapter8/Definition8.1.8` identified by
issue #8533.  Added `Etingof.projectiveObject_iff`, proving that the categorical
lifting predicate for `P` is equivalent to exactness of the covariant
preadditive Hom functor `Hom(P, -)`.  Added the dual theorem
`Etingof.injectiveObject_iff` for the contravariant functor `Hom(-, I)`.

Exactness is stated explicitly as preservation of finite limits and finite
colimits.  The always-available left-exact half comes from preadditive Yoneda;
projectivity or injectivity supplies the finite-colimit half, and Mathlib's
converse theorems recover the lifting predicates from that half.

The claim ledger now records the projective and injective characterizations as
separate claims, each citing its corresponding iff theorem rather than only an
abbreviation.
