## Accomplished

Closed the month-old `bug`-labelled fidelity gap #5361 on `Chapter2/Example2.9.12`
(`U(𝔰𝔩(2))` is the algebra generated by `e`, `f`, `h` with `he−eh=2e`, `hf−fh=−2f`,
`ef−fe=h`). This was the oldest unclaimed issue in the queue and the only one still
carrying a vacuity verdict from the wave-1 sweep.

**State on arrival.** The issue text was stale: the original `example : Algebra k
(UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L)` had already been replaced by a real coordinate model
`sl2 k = k × k × k` and `sl2_universal_relations` proving the three commutator identities
in `U(sl2 k)`. The live gap was the one in `items.json`'s `fidelity_note`: the coordinate
model was not tied to the canonical traceless-matrix `𝔰𝔩₂`, and *neither* half of the
book's actual sentence ("**is the algebra generated by** e, f, h **with relations** ...")
was formalized.

**What landed** (three commits, all on `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter2/Example2_9_12.lean`):

1. *Generation.* `adjoin_range_ι` — the image of `ι` generates the universal enveloping
   algebra of any Lie algebra, proved by pushing `TensorAlgebra.adjoin_range_ι` through
   the surjection `RingQuot.mkAlgHom`. Then `adjoin_ι_e_f_h : Algebra.adjoin k
   {ι e, ι f, ι h} = ⊤`.

2. *Completeness of the presentation.* `PresRel` names the three relations on
   `FreeAlgebra k (Fin 3)`; `Presentation k := RingQuot (PresRel k)` is the algebra the
   book describes. `presentationEquiv : Presentation k ≃ₐ[k] UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k
   (sl2 k)`, with `presentationEquiv_gen_zero/one/two` matching the three presented
   generators to `ι e`, `ι f`, `ι h`. Both round trips are proved (`UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.hom_ext`
   one way, `RingQuot.ringQuot_ext'` + `FreeAlgebra` generators the other).

3. *The `𝔰𝔩(2)` in question, and non-vacuity.* `slEquiv : sl2 k ≃ₗ⁅k⁆
   LieAlgebra.SpecialLinear.sl (Fin 2) k` sends `e, f, h ↦ E₀₁, E₁₀, E₀₀−E₁₁`;
   `isSl2Triple_slEquiv` records that these form an `IsSl2Triple` in Mathlib's sense.
   `stdRep` (the standard 2-dimensional representation, built via `liftTriple`) yields
   `ι_e_ne_zero` and hence `Nontrivial` instances for both `U(sl2 k)` and `Presentation k`
   over nontrivial `k` — the presentation does not collapse to the zero ring.

**Key reusable piece.** `liftTriple`: a triple `(X, Y, Z)` in an associative `k`-algebra
satisfying the three relations *is* a Lie algebra morphism out of `𝔰𝔩(2)`. The bilinear
commutator expansion is done there exactly once and then used for the presented algebra,
for `U` itself, and for the matrix representation. The `module` tactic discharges the
expansion cleanly; that is worth remembering for similar structure-constant computations.
