### Decision: verbatim move (kept each decl's own binders)

The issue suggested dropping the explicit `k` binder and using the section
variable. I moved the decls verbatim instead, preserving each signature exactly.
This is safer here because the 11 decls are heterogeneous: 7 carry
`(k : Type u) [Field k] [IsAlgClosed k] [CharZero k]`, one omits `CharZero`, one
is fully generic over a ring `R` (no `k`), and two `private` lemmas carry an
*implicit* `{k}`. Dropping binders uniformly would have flipped the private
lemmas' `k` from implicit to explicit (breaking their call sites) and added a
spurious `CharZero` to the no-`CharZero` decl. A local binder of the same name
simply shadows the section variable; Lean accepted it with no error.
