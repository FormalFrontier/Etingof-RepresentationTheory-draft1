## Accomplished

Formalized part **(g)** of Etingof Problem 2.15.1 (issue #5281): the Casimir
operator `C = EF + FE + H²/2` acts on the `d`-dimensional irreducible
`sl(2)`-representation `V_d` as the scalar `(d-1)(d+1)/2`, i.e. `λ(λ+2)/2` on
`V_λ` with `d = λ+1`.

Added to `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter2/Sl2Irrep.lean`:
- `casimir_rhoEFH` (private): the endomorphism identity
  `rhoE·rhoF + rhoF·rhoE + ½·rhoH² = ((d-1)(d+1)/2)·1` on `V_d`, proved by a
  direct pointwise computation on the standard basis. On `e_k` the three
  summands contribute `(k+1)(d-k-1)`, `k(d-k)`, and `(d-1-2k)²/2`, whose sum is
  `(d-1)(d+1)/2` independent of `k` — so the scalar nature drops out without
  Schur's lemma.
- `casimir_eq_scalar`: the same statement phrased via the representation images
  `ρ(e), ρ(f), ρ(h) = rhoLieHom d sl2_{e,f,h}`.
- `casimir_eq_scalar_lambda`: the book-faithful corollary, eigenvalue
  `λ(λ+2)/2` on the `(λ+1)`-dimensional `V_λ`.

All sorry-free. `Sl2Irrep.lean` and the dependent `Theorem2_1_1.lean` both build.

The normalisation is the book's `C = EF + FE + H²/2` (eigenvalue `λ(λ+2)/2`),
which is exactly half the `sl2_casimir = h² + 2ef + 2fe` (eigenvalue `n(n+2)`)
used internally in `Theorem2_1_1.lean` — consistent.
