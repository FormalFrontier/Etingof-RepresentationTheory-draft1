## Key patterns discovered

- `Representation.asModule ρ` is `def asModule _ := V` with derived instances and
  `asModuleEquiv = LinearEquiv.refl`. As a result `DirectSum.IsInternal` (a purely
  additive `Bijective (coeAddMonoidHom …)` statement) transfers from the
  ℂ[A5]-submodule family `N` to the same-carrier `Submodule ℂ`-family
  `(ofSubmodule' (N k)).toSubmodule` by a bare `exact hInternalN` — the two
  `coeAddMonoidHom`s are defeq. This is the crux that makes D3 short.
- `Subrepresentation.ofSubmodule'` lands in `Submodule ℂ W` (the genuine
  representation space, with its real `Pi` module instance), sidestepping the
  `ρ.asModule` vs `Fin n → ℂ` instance-mismatch friction.
- `mem_ofSubmodule'_iff` is `.rfl`, so membership in the transported submodule is
  definitionally membership in the ℂ[A5]-submodule.
