## Accomplished

Executed feature issue #7409 — restored the source content of Etingof Lemma 3.1.6 (subfamily-selection splitting) in `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter3/Lemma3_1_6.lean`.

The pre-existing `Etingof.surjective_map_splits` only asserted existence of an arbitrary complement of `ker f`. Added the faithful, family-aware results:

- `Etingof.exists_subfamily_isCompl_ker`: given a family `p : ι → Submodule A V` of simple submodules with `⨆ i, p i = ⊤` and any `f : V →ₗ[A] U`, produces a subset `J ⊆ ι` such that `⨆ i ∈ J, p i` is a genuine complement (`IsCompl`) of `ker f`. Proof follows the book: Zorn's lemma to pick a maximal `J` with `f` injective on `V_J` (disjoint from `ker f`), then Schur (each `p i` is an atom, `isSimpleModule_iff_isAtom` + `IsAtom.not_le_iff_disjoint`) to derive a contradiction if `V_J ⊔ ker f ≠ ⊤`.
- `Etingof.exists_subfamily_bijective`: the restriction `f.domRestrict (⨆ i ∈ J, p i)` is bijective (needs surjectivity of `f`).
- `Etingof.exists_subfamily_linearEquiv`: packages that as a `LinearEquiv` agreeing with `f` on the sub-sum.
- `Etingof.exists_subfamily_linearEquiv_of_isInternal`: book form taking `DirectSum.IsInternal p`.
- Helper `Etingof.disjoint_sup_of_disjoint` (algebraic core of enlarging the subfamily by one simple summand).

Independence of the family is not required for the selection argument, so the general theorems only assume a spanning family of simples; the `_of_isInternal` variant matches the book's `V = ⨁ Vᵢ` phrasing exactly.
