  - center-center: `if_neg` for the impossible disjunction, then
    `hadj_diag center` closes `adj center center = 0`. ✓

    identical.) ✓
  - leaf-leaf: `if_neg` for impossible disjunction, then `hadj_indep`

- **Final invocation**:
