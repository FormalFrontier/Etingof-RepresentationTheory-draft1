## Accomplished

Proved `finrank_irreducible_le_char` (issue #6801) sorry-free in
`Chapter2/Problem2_16_4.lean` — the dimension-bound half of the characteristic-`p`
classification of irreducible `𝔰𝔩(2)` representations: over an algebraically closed field
of characteristic `p > 2`, every irreducible finite dimensional representation has dimension
`≤ p`. Its signature is unchanged; `#print axioms` = `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

New infrastructure added to the file (all sorry-free):
- `lie_sl2_h_e`, `lie_sl2_h_f`, `lie_sl2_e_f` — the `𝔰𝔩(2)` bracket relations, and
  `sl2_decomp` — every element is `x₀₁·e + x₁₀·f + x₀₀·h`.
- `eq_top_of_lie_closed`, `lie_closed_of_efh`, `span_closed_of_gens`,
  `finrank_le_of_orbit_top` — submodule/span plumbing.
- `lie_schur` — Schur's lemma for a finite dimensional irreducible Lie module over an
  algebraically closed field (an operator commuting with the action is a scalar), proved
  directly via an eigenspace being a nonzero submodule.
- Inside the main proof: operators `E,F,H = toEnd` of the basis; the `[H,E]=2E` etc.
  operator relations; power-commutator inductions giving centrality of `E^p`, `F^p`
  (char `p`); Schur then yields scalars `α,β`; and the two cyclic-submodule constructions
  (`E` nilpotent → `F`-orbit; `E` injective → `E`-orbit from a joint `H,FE`-eigenvector).
