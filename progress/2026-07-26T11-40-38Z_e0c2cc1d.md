### Design notes

**Which way `charW₁` points.** The coset representatives track the *row* line `[g₁₀ : g₁₁]` of
`B \ G`, whereas `charW₁`'s affine chart tracks the *column* line: the two fixed-point conditions
are `g₁₀t² + (g₀₀−g₁₁)t − g₀₁ = 0` and `g₀₁t² + (g₀₀−g₁₁)t − g₁₀ = 0`, i.e. transposes of each
other. Rather than proving `#Fix(M) = #Fix(Mᵀ)` through the discriminant (which needs separate
Artin–Schreier reasoning in characteristic 2), the file uses the explicit involution
`t ↦ −t⁻¹` of `P¹` with `0 ↔ ∞` (`projInvol`), which carries the roots of `aX² + bX − c` to the
roots of `cX² + bX − a` in one `field_simp`. No hypothesis on `p` or `n` is needed anywhere.

**The side condition is free.** The coset description of "`[1:t]` is fixed" also demands
`g₀₀ + t g₁₀ ≠ 0`. That is automatic: violating it forces `det g = 0`, discharged by a single
`linear_combination` (see `cosetIndex_cosetRep_some_mul`).

**Instance hygiene, the recurring trap.** `Theorem5_25_2.lean` carries a file-level
`instance : Fintype (GaloisField p n)`, while `GL2CharacterValues.lean` and `Lemma5_25_3.lean`
take `[Fintype (GaloisField p n)] [DecidableEq (GaloisField p n)] [Fintype (GL2 p n)]` as
binders. Mixing the two makes `rw` fail on goals that print identically. Every statement in the
new file therefore takes these as *binders*, matching the `Lemma5_25_3` convention, and
`sum_borelCharExt_conj` / `card_borelSubgroup` additionally take `[Fintype ↥(BorelSubgroup p n)]`
explicitly so the derived instance cannot drift between lemma and use site. `rootIndicator`
likewise takes `[DecidableEq (GaloisField p n)]` so that `Finset.sum_boole` lands on the same
`Finset.filter` instance that `charW₁` uses. Downstream work (#7906) should keep to the binder
convention.

**De-privatized in `Theorem5_25_2.lean`** (no other change to that file): `borel_diag00_ne_zero`,
`borel_diag11_ne_zero`, `borelCharValue`, `principalSeriesSubmodule`, `principalSeriesRep`,
