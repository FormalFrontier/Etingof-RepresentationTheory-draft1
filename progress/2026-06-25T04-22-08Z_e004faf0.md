Filled the `sorry` in `Etingof.Example5_1_3_S4` (issue #5203): **all irreducible
representations of S₄ are of real Frobenius–Schur type**, fully proved and
**axiom-clean** (`#print axioms` → only `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`;
no `sorryAx`).

Approach — a reusable "group-algebra real form" engine, added to
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter5/Example5_1_3.lean`:

- `coordForm` : the coordinate dot-product symmetric bilinear form
  `Φ(x,y) = ∑_g (x g)(y g)` on `ℂ[G]`. Proved symmetric, invariant under left
  multiplication (`coordForm_of_mul`), and `Φ(c,c) ≠ 0` when `c` has real,
  nonzero coefficients (`coordForm_self_ne_zero_of_real`, via `∑ (c g)² > 0`).
- `isRealType_of_coordForm_transport` : a simple `Representation ℂ G V` that
  admits a `ℂ`-linear intertwiner `ψ : V → ℂ[G]` (matching `ρ g` with left
  multiplication) whose image contains a real nonzero `c = ψ v₀` is of real type.
  Pull back `coordForm`; nonzero + invariant + simple ⇒ nondegenerate
  (`Etingof.nondegenerate_of_invariant_of_simple`).
- S₄ assembly: embed `ρ.asModule` as a **left ideal `I ⊆ ℂ[S₄]`** via
  `IsSemisimpleRing.exists_linearEquiv_ideal_of_isSimpleModule` (this also dodges
  the universe wrinkle — `I : Type 0` regardless of `V`'s universe), classify
  `I ≅ V_λ` via `Etingof.Theorem5_12_2_classification`, and take `c = c_λ` the
  Young symmetrizer. `c_λ` has integer coefficients
  (`youngSymmetrizer_im_eq_zero`, from `YoungSymmetrizer_eq_mapRange`), and
  `c_λ ≠ 0` from `young_symmetrizer_sq_ne_zero`.

This route deliberately avoids needing an explicit rational polytabloid basis: the
realness of `c_λ` alone supplies the non-vanishing.
