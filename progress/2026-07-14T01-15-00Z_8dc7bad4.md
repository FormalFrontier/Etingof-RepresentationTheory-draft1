infrastructure:

  each with `SO3` membership;
- `conjRep_Dz0 … conjRep_Pc2` (the 12 conjugation-on-basis computations).

Proof idea: the diagonal sign rotations `diag(±1)` (det 1) isolate each skew coordinate axis
by averaging `M ± conjRep D M`; the cyclic rotation `Pc` spreads a single coordinate across
all three axes. Nonzero `M` yields a nonzero coordinate, so every basis vector lies in the
invariant subspace `U`, giving `skewSub ≤ U` and hence `U = skewSub`.
