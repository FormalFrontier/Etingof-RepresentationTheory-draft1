## Accomplished

Closed the fidelity gap in issue #7458 (Proposition 5.21.2, first formula).

Previously `Proposition5_21_2_geometric` was stated only for `z : ℚ`, whereas the source
works with a complex variable. `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter5/Proposition5_21_2.lean`
now provides:

- `evalGeometricAt N K z` — evaluation of a rational-coefficient `MvPolynomial` at the
  geometric progression `(1, z, …, z^{N-1})` for `z` in any characteristic-zero field `K`,
  together with `evalGeometricAt_rat` identifying the `K = ℚ` case with `evalGeometric`.
- `Proposition5_21_2_geometric_mul` — the cross-multiplied identity
  `S_λ(1,z,…,z^{N-1}) · ∏_{i<j}(z^{N-1-i} - z^{N-1-j}) = ∏_{i<j}(z^{λᵢ+N-1-i} - z^{λⱼ+N-1-j})`,
  valid for every `z` with **no** nonvanishing hypothesis.
- `Proposition5_21_2_geometric_field` — the quotient form over `K`, under exactly the
  hypothesis needed to interpret the displayed quotient (each denominator factor nonzero).
- `Proposition5_21_2_geometric_zpow` — the source's *literal* form, with the book's own
  integer exponents `z^{λᵢ-i} / z^{-i}` (zero-based index `i : Fin N` carrying book index
  `i+1`), for `z ≠ 0`.
- `Proposition5_21_2_geometric_complex` and `Proposition5_21_2_geometric_complex_zpow` —
  the complex-variable specializations named explicitly.
- `Proposition5_21_2_geometric` (ℚ) is retained and is now a two-line corollary of the
  general theorem; its former 45-line proof is gone.

Supporting helpers `prod_Ioi_eq_prod_filter` (now for any `CommMonoid`) and
`prod_Ioi_sub_eq_neg_pow_mul` (replacing `ratio_vandermonde_eq_ratio_reversed`, now for any
`CommRing`) were generalized; the sign `(-1)^{#pairs}` from reversing each Vandermonde
difference is isolated once and cancelled, which is what makes the hypothesis-free
cross-multiplied form available.

`Proposition5_21_2_dimension` (the `z = 1` limit) is untouched and still builds.
