### Note on the QUEUED-check merge filter loophole

The project-CLAUDE.md merge filter passes PRs whose checks are
still QUEUED, because empty-string conclusions match neither
`FAILURE` nor `CANCELLED`. For a content-free planner self-handoff
(one new markdown file, no Lean code) this is benign — and in this
cycle it was actively useful: it unstuck cycle 816's handoff PR
after its CI had been queued without progress for ~3 minutes
(short, but in the context of the auto-merge timeline long enough
to matter). For substantive PRs, the same loophole would be a
hazard; if this ever becomes a real concern the filter should be
tightened to require `.conclusion == "SUCCESS"` on every job, but
that change is out of planner scope.
