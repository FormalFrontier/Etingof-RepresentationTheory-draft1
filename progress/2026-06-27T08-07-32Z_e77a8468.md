## Accomplished

One-shot session (`pod once 5476`) on issue **#5476** ("Infra for Example 8.1.7 (part 2):
flat ⟹ projective over a finite-dimensional algebra (Bass / perfect rings)").

The issue's *literal* deliverable — a sorry-free `Module.Flat A P → Module.Projective A P`
over a finite-dimensional algebra — is **unstatable in current Mathlib** (`Module.Flat`
requires `CommSemiring`; there is no noncommutative balanced tensor product). This was
established by a prior session, and the salvageable ring-theory infra
(`isSemiprimaryRing_of_finiteDimensional`) already landed under #5476 (PR #5484). The cogenerator
reframing of Example 8.1.7 (#5508) deliberately sidesteps flatness entirely, turning the goal
into the biconditional `Module.Projective A P ↔ Module.Injective Aᵐᵒᵖ (Module.Dual k P)`.

So this session delivered the genuinely achievable adjacent piece: the **forward direction** of
that biconditional, sorry-free, in `EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter8/Example8_1_7.lean`:

* `injective_of_leftInverse` — injectivity transfers along a split injection (retract), proved
  from the universal lifting property (no `Small` hypotheses; same-universe).
* `contragredientMap` — the contravariant dual functor on morphisms: `f : M →ₗ[A] N` induces
  `N* →ₗ[Aᵐᵒᵖ] M*` by `k`-linear precomposition, intertwining the contragredient actions.
* `freeDualEquiv` — `Module.Dual k (P →₀ A) ≃ₗ[Aᵐᵒᵖ] (∀ p : P, Module.Dual k A)`, the dual of a
  free module as a product of copies of `A*`. Underlying `k`-bijection is `Finsupp.lsum`; the
  content is `Aᵐᵒᵖ`-action compatibility.
* `projective_dual_injective` — for **any** `k`-algebra `A` (no finite dimensionality),
  `Module.Projective A P → Module.Injective Aᵐᵒᵖ (Module.Dual k P)`. `P` is a retract of `P →₀ A`;
  dualise contravariantly, identify the free dual as a product of injectives
  (`Module.Injective.pi` + `dual_regular_injective`), and finish the retract at the level of
  Baer's criterion so it survives the universe gap between `P*` and `(P →₀ A)*`. `Small`
  instances supplied explicitly via `small_max` at named universes.

The main theorem `Etingof.Example_8_1_7` now discharges its forward (`→`) direction via
`projective_dual_injective`; only the converse remains as a single `sorry`.

`lake build EtingofRepresentationTheory.Chapter8.Example8_1_7` succeeds; the only `sorry` is the
converse direction. No `native_decide`.
