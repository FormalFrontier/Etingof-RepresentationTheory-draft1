- **Topological groups and Lie groups: continuous group homomorphisms, examples (GL_n(R), SO(n), U(n))** (undergraduate_prerequisite)
  Mathlib (partial): `IsTopologicalGroup`, `ContinuousMonoidHom`, `GroupTopology`, `LieGroup`
  Topological groups and continuous homomorphisms use `IsTopologicalGroup` and `ContinuousMonoidHom`; `LieGroup` supplies smooth manifold group structures. General linear groups arise as units of normed algebras, but the full named SO(n)/U(n) example catalogue is not packaged as Lie-group instances.
