## Context (proof strategy from the book)

Book statement (blob `blobs/Chapter4/Problem4.12.6.md`): "Let `𝔽_q` be a finite field with `q`
elements, and let `G` be the group of nonconstant inhomogeneous linear transformations
`x ↦ ax + b` (`a ∈ 𝔽_q^×`, `b ∈ 𝔽_q`). Find all irreducible complex representations of `G`
and compute their characters. **Hint:** Let `V` be the representation of `G` on the space of
functions on `𝔽_q` with sum of all values equal to zero. Show that `V` is an irreducible
representation of `G`."

`G = K ⋊ Kˣ` has order `q(q-1)`. The permutation action on `K → ℂ` splits as constants `⊕ V`.
For irreducibility of `V`: the affine group acts transitively on `K` (translations) and the
stabilizer `Kˣ` of `0` acts transitively on `K \ {0}`, i.e. `G` is 2-transitive on `K`; a
2-transitive permutation representation has exactly two irreducible constituents, the trivial
one and the (irreducible) augmentation/zero-sum representation. Search Mathlib for existing
2-transitivity or permutation-character API; if none fits, prove irreducibility directly:
given `0 ≠ f ∈ U ⊆ V`, translating `f` and taking differences produces a delta-like function
whose `G`-orbit spans `V`.
