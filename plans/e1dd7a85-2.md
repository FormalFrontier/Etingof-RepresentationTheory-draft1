The book states: for a conjugation-invariant system `X` of subgroups of a finite group `G`,
the following are equivalent:

- (i) every element of `G` lies in some `H ∈ X`;

  induced representations `Ind_H^G V` for `H ∈ X` and `V` an irrep of `H`.
