## Current state

PR #2795 (merged 2026-05-17T10:22, +154 lines net in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter5/Theorem5_22_1.lean`) closes #2684:
β.3 of the Schur-Weyl L_i C-4a-i sub-β chain — off-block vanishing of
the Young symmetrizer action on simple `symGroupImage`-submodules whose
Specht-module label differs from `weightToPartition N lam`.

The PR is unreviewed. It adds three artefacts:

1. **`isIdempotentElem_eq_zero_of_trace_eq_zero`** (private) — a
   characteristic-zero linear-algebra lemma: an idempotent endomorphism
   of a finite-dimensional vector space over a `CharZero` field with
   vanishing trace is the zero map. Generic in `K` and `V`, no Young /
   Schur / Specht content. Proof uses
   `LinearMap.IsIdempotentElem.isProj_range`, `IsProj.trace`,
   `Submodule.finrank_eq_zero`, `LinearMap.range_eq_bot`.
2. **`youngSymmetrizerK_complex_eq`** (private) — bridges
   `YoungSymmetrizerK ℂ n la = YoungSymmetrizer n la`.
3. **`youngSym_action_vanishes_off_block`** — the main β.3
   deliverable. Combines β.1
   (`trace_youngSymEndomorphism_restrict_eq_sum`), β.2 (via an
   `h_label` hypothesis encoding the result of
   `trace_symGroupAction_eq_spechtModuleCharacter`),
   `youngSym_trace_kronecker'` (character orthogonality), and
   `youngSymEndomorphism_restrict_sq_scalar` (idempotent up to scalar).

The main theorem carries `maxHeartbeats 800000` and
`synthInstance.maxHeartbeats 800000` (40× default), justified in a
comment by the deep `Subalgebra → Subsemiring → Module → IsScalarTower`
synthesis chain traversed when β.1 and β.2 are applied to
`S.restrictScalars ℂ`. This is the first appearance of a 40× bump in
this file's Schur-Weyl section; the surrounding β.1/β.2 lemmas use
`maxHeartbeats 400000`.

The result feeds two downstream consumers in the queue:
- **#2693** (Schur-Weyl L_i γ.B): rank-1 dim count for the special
  Specht block — pairs with β.3 to give simplicity at the level of
  `youngSymEndomorphism` applied to `S`.
- **#2708** (Schur-Weyl L_i C-4a aggregation): closes the
  `schurModuleSubmodule_isSimple_centralizer` sorry by combining β.3
