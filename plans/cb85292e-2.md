The project is proof-complete and in the confidence phase: per-chapter

opening flagship, **Theorem 8.1.1 (the four equivalent characterisations of a
projective module)**, in
`EtingofRepresentationTheory/Chapter8/Theorem8_1_1.lean`, has **not** yet been

The book (`blobs/Chapter8/Theorem8.1.1.md`) states four equivalent properties of
a module `P`:
- (i) lifting property against surjections;
- (ii) every surjection `M → P` splits;
- (iii) `P` is a direct summand of a free module;
- (iv) `Hom_A(P, ?)` is exact.

The Lean formalization splits this into three iff theorems, each anchored on
`Module.Projective R P`:
- `Etingof.Theorem_8_1_1_i_iff_ii` (i ↔ ii), hypothesis `[Small.{v} R]`;
- `Etingof.Theorem_8_1_1_i_iff_iii` (i ↔ iii), with `Q : Type (max u v)`,
  `Module.Free R Q`;
- `Etingof.Theorem_8_1_1_i_iff_iv` (i ↔ iv), hypothesis `[Small.{v} R]`,
  "Hom exact" formalized as preservation of short exact sequences.
