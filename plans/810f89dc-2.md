### Proof outline (mandatory reading: §4.2 of `q-high-involution.md`)

1. **Compute the [σ]-component of Δ as a known scalar combination of
   |Q_eq| and Q_eq'-counts.** Subtract `c · ψ_id` to cancel the
   [σ]-component (`ψ_id ∈ V` by IH: rowInv 0 < rowInv σ when
   `hrp : 0 < rowInv σ`).

2. **The remainder `Δ - c · ψ_id` has support strictly below [σ]**
   (by R2.a's support bound + cancellation of the [σ]-term).

3. **For tabloid components `[α] ≺ [σ]` with col-std rep `σ_α`**:
   peel off via `c_α · ψ_{σ_α}` (in V by IH, srRank σ_α < srRank σ
   strictly).

4. **For tabloid components `[α] ≺ [σ]` WITHOUT col-std rep**:
   invoke a sign-cancellation involution analogous to
   `twistedPolytabloid_apply_of_not_dominates`
   (`SpechtModuleBasis.lean` line ~1506) at the
   `(γ_q · w · q⁻¹ · σ)`-level, NOT the `(q⁻¹ · σ)`-level.

   Concretely: for each non-col-std-rep tabloid `[α]`, the contribution
   to Δ from the q's whose τ_q lies above `[α]` sums to zero by an
   involution swapping q-pairs related by an explicit column
   transposition. The construction uses:
   - The pigeonhole same-row column-pair witnesses from
     `twistedPolytabloid_pigeonhole_pair` (line ~1475 — note this
     lemma is currently sorried; for **proof-level** purposes assume
     its statement; the sorry is a separate dependency tracked via
     #2543 / PR #2550 in repair).
   - The pattern `q ↦ q · swap(a₁, a₂)` lifts to the τ_q level via
     `γ_q (q · swap(a₁,a₂)) = γ_q (q) · swap(a₁,a₂)` (column
     transpositions commute with `γ_q ∈ Q_λ` up to sign by
     `garnir_pigeonhole_collapse` from #2498 / PR #2505).
