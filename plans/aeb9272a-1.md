## Deliverables

A soundness audit (no code changes unless a defect is found). Verify:

1. **No vacuous/mis-stated hypotheses.** Each theorem's statement matches its
   intended mathematical content — in particular `formalCharacter_add_of_shortExact`
   genuinely encodes a short exact sequence (`hexact : range ι = ker π` together
   with `hι_inj`, `hπ_surj`), and the weight-spanning hypotheses are real
   (`⨆ μ, glWeightSpace = ⊤`), not trivially satisfiable.
2. **Equivariance is w.r.t. the correct representations.** `quotDetProj_equivariant`
   and `exists_degree_embedding_of_simple`'s output equivariance are stated against
   `quotDetRep` / `quotDetDegreeFDRep`'s actual `ρ`, with the degree projection
   commuting with the right `GL_N` action (`homogeneousComponent_polyRightRep`).
3. **The grading is a genuine internal direct sum** (`_iSup_eq_top` + `_iSupIndep`)
   and the per-degree projection laws (`_id_on_degree`, `_zero_on_degree`) are
   consistent with it — no off-by-one or wrong-index defects that would let the
   reduction pick a spurious degree.
4. **`exists_degree_embedding_of_simple` injectivity is real**: the produced `ψ`
   is injective because `quotDetProj d ∘ φ` is nonzero on the simple `L` for some
   `d` (Schur), not by a degenerate argument.
5. **No hidden axioms / `sorry`.** Confirm `#print axioms` on
   `exists_degree_embedding_of_simple` and `formalCharacter_add_of_shortExact`
   yields only `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.
