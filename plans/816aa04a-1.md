## Context

The book statement (`blobs/Chapter4/Problem4.12.8.md`): part (a) asks to *derive*
the classification of finite subgroups of `SO(3)` as cyclic `ℤ/nℤ`, dihedral `Dₙ`,
tetrahedral `A₄`, octahedral `S₄`, or icosahedral `A₅` (via the pole-counting
argument and the diophantine identity `2(1−1/n) = Σᵢ(1−1/mᵢ)`); part (b) asks to
classify finite subgroups of `SU(2)` using the double cover `SU(2) → SO(3)`.

Points the audit should specifically resolve (do not merely restate — reach a
verdict on each):

- **Part (b) is conditional on a hypothesis, not a construction.** The theorem
  takes the double-cover homomorphism `h` and its `{±1}`-kernel property `hker` as
  *hypotheses* rather than exhibiting the map. Assess whether this is a faithful
  rendering of "classify finite subgroups of SU(2)" or a fidelity gap (e.g. the
  statement is vacuously about a possibly-nonexistent `h`, or it captures the
  double-cover order relation but not the actual classification list). Check
  whether the existence of such an `h` is established elsewhere in the repo.
- **Dihedral indexing.** The book uses `Dₙ` of order `2n` for `n ≥ 2` (with a
  footnote that a regular 2-gon is a segment). The Lean uses `DihedralGroup n`
  with an existential `∃ n`. Confirm the Mathlib `DihedralGroup n` cardinality
  convention matches the book's family and that the unconstrained `∃ n` does not
  admit degenerate members that misrepresent the classification.
- **Group-name matches.** `A₄ = alternatingGroup (Fin 4)`, `S₄ = Equiv.Perm (Fin 4)`,
  `A₅ = alternatingGroup (Fin 5)` — confirm these are the intended rotation groups
  of tetrahedron / cube-octahedron / dodecahedron-icosahedron.
- **Non-vacuity.** Confirm both headline theorems are axiom-clean
  (`#print axioms`, no `sorryAx`) and that the build is clean.
